    theme: t-bg-primary

      As well as formatting your text, you can also alter the appearance of your document by simply changing the position of text on the page. Take a look below to find out how you can amend your document:

    subInc: 
      type: reveal
      content:
        -
          title: Insert text
          copy: |
            You can insert text into an existing document by simply placing your cursor at the point you would like the new text to appear, and then just start typing.
          img: it1_u1_s3_p2_reveal1.svg
        -
          title: Move text (Drag & Drop)
          copy: |
            You can move existing text from one part of your document to another. To do this, simply click to highlight the text you want to move, then click and hold with your left mouse button and move to the position you want the text to appear. Release the left mouse button to drop the text into its new position.
          img: it1_u1_s3_p2_reveal2.svg
        -
          title: Delete text
          copy: |
            To delete text from your document, you can either:
            Place the cursor at the end of the text you want to delete, and then press the Backspace key to delete each letter / number. Or, click to highlight the text you want to delete and then press the Delete key.

          img: it1_u1_s3_p2_reveal3.svg

          title: Cut, copy and paste
          text: |
            You can move sections of text in your document using Cut, Copy and Paste – which can all be found in your Word toolbar.

            If you want to keep the original text in place while you move it, use Copy and Paste. If you use Cut and Paste, the text you highlight and cut will disappear from the screen until you paste it back into your document.
          img: it1_u1_s3_p2_slide1.svg
        -
          title: Find and replace
          text: |
            If you make a mistake throughout a document, like forgetting to use a capital letter for the name of a company, you don’t need to alter all of these mistakes separately – Find and Replace is a much quicker option.

            One thing to bear in mind when using this tool is that Word will look for every instance of the word you have stated, and automatically change it for you.
          img: it1_u1_s3_p2_slide2.svg
        -
          title: Undo / Redo
          text: |
            When you are working on a document, you can quickly and easily undo the last thing you have done (such as a word you have typed, format you have applied, etc).

            If you undo a particular action but then decide against it, you can redo it.
          img: it1_u1_s3_p2_slide3.svg
