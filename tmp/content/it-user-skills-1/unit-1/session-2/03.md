    title: Using styles
    layout: media
    theme: t-bg-primary
    copy: |
      Styles are mainly used to format headings and body text, but they can also be used in lists and tables. To apply a style to a paragraph or line of text:

      1. Highlight the text that you want to apply the Style to.

      2. In the *Styles* group, under the *Home* tab, click on the Style that you want to apply.

      You can apply different styles to different parts of text; simply use the steps above for different sections within your document. For example, you may want to apply one style to the heading of your document, and one to the body text.
