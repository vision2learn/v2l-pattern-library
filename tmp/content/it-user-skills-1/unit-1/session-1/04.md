          title: On a PC
          copy: |
            The *File Button* is in the top left corner of your Microsoft Office programs. When you click the *File* button, the window will change to give you a series of options that allows you to open, save and print your file.

            A list of recent documents will also show, you can view Info about your document, and make a number of changes using the Options command.
          img: it1_u1_s1_p4_reveal1.svg
        -
          title: On a Mac
          copy: |
            The *File Menu* item is located top left (next to the program name e.g. Word) on your Apple Menu bar. When you click the *File* button, a menu will expand, giving you a series of basic commands that allow you to open, save and print your file.

            You can also get a list of recent documents and view the Properties about your document.

          img: it1_u1_s1_p4_reveal2.svg

      The tools and options that each Ribbon contains relate to the function of that Ribbon.  Each Ribbon is accessed by clicking on the relevant tab and the commands in each tab are organised into groupings.
