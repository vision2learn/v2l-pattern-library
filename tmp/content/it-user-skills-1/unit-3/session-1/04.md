      As you build your presentation, it is very important that you check your work for consistent formatting in order to ensure that your work is professional and fit for purpose.

      A lot of companies often have their own guidelines for you to follow when creating a presentation – this is often referred to as a ‘house style’.

          title: House style
          text: |
            A ‘house style’ is a particular style that a company wants to apply to all of its written documentation and correspondence. This is used to ensure consistency in the formatting of documents. For example, you can imagine how useful this is in a large company, where hundreds of employees could be creating documents in a variety of different styles and layouts.
          img: it1_u3_s1_p4_slide1.svg
        -
          title: Guidelines
          text: |
            A ‘house style’ might include the following types of guidelines for creating a presentation:
            * Font sizes: main headings = 44pts, sub-headings = 36pts, body text = 24pts
            * The use of colour must be consistent with the company brand
            * When using bullets, include no more than 6 bullet points per slide
            * Ensure the presentation contains no more than 5 consecutive slides without a graphic
            * Keep content clear and to the point – include no more than 75 words per slide

    title: Maintaining consistency
    layout: media
    theme: t-bg-primary
    copy: |
      As we have seen, maintaining consistency throughout your presentation is very important. To help maintain consistency in your work, you can use a **master slide** to apply certain formats and designs across your presentation.

      A master slide contains information about the template you will use to create your presentation, including font styles, placeholder positions, background designs and colour schemes.

      The purpose of the master slide is to enable you to make changes to multiple slides in your presentation simply by changing one slide. For example, if you changed the font style on your master slide, this change would be repeated throughout all slides in your presentation.

    img: /images/svg/it1/it1_u3_s1_p3_sectionimage.png
