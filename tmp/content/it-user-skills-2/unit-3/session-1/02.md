    theme: t-bg-primary
    copy: |
        Microsoft Excel contains spreadsheets. These are like automatic calculators that can contain and process a lot of information. For example, imagine that you have a list of items for sale and you have added up the total cost at the bottom of the list. If one or two of the prices change, you would have to recalculate the whole thing!

        However, a spreadsheet will do this for you automatically. As soon as any numerical data in your spreadsheet changes, any calculations that relate to this data will update…saving you lots of time and work!

    title: Worksheets and workbooks
    theme: t-bg-supporting2
    copy: |
      Worksheets and workbooks are tools to help you organise different spreadsheets. The starting point for any Excel document is the workbook, which initially contains three worksheets. Worksheets in Excel are made up of cells organised into columns and rows. 
      
      Using multiple worksheets within a spreadsheet helps you to store different sets of data that all relate to the same topic. For example, you may use *Sheet1* to record your expenses for one financial year, and *Sheet2* to record expenses for the next year and so on. Take a look at the below icons to learn more.
