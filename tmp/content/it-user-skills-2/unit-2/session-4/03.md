title: Master slides
intro: |
    In PowerPoint, a master slide is a really useful way of changing the appearance of the different slides in your presentation.

    By using master slides, you can select the format and design options for your entire presentation. These effects are then automatically applied to your existing slides, and any new slides that you add to your presentation.

        ### Master slide

        When you look at your master slide, you will see that apart from the master slide, there is also a slide for every possible slide layout in your presentation.

        You can specify the design and formatting options for just the master slide, or you can edit each of these slide layouts.
