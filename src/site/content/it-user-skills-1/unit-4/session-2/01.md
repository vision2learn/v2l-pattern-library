    title: Quick quiz
    copy: |
      To begin any formula in Microsoft Excel, you must first click to select the cell where you want the result to appear and then type the = sign. This tells Excel that the entries that follow are part of a formula.

      Next, enter the cell reference (this is made up of the column letter and row number – e.g. A1, B3, C5, etc), followed by the mathematical operator and then the final cell reference or number.

      For example to add the contents of cells A1 to A2 the formula would be =A1+A2.

      Have a go at the following questions to see if you can guess the correct formulas.
