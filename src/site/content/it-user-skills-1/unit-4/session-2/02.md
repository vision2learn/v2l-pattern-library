    title: Working with multiple cells
    layout: media
    theme: t-bg-primary
    copy: |
      You can quickly and easily replicate formulae across multiple cells by clicking and dragging using the autofill or fill tool.

      By highlighting the cell containing the formula you want to replicate and then dragging up / down a column or across a row, Excel will populate the cells that you want to include in the selected formula.

      This is a very useful technique to remember when creating spreadsheets. You can see how much quicker it is to get Excel to replicate your formula – and it means you don’t have to worry about any typing errors or inaccuracies.
