      All businesses benefit from being able to store, retrieve and handle information in a consistent way. There are generally three different processes that businesses go through when dealing with information – archiving, retrieving and deleting information.

        ### The value of information

        When making decisions and developing procedures on the management of information, it helps to consider two different aspects: importance and inactivity.

        **Importance** indicates the value of information for the organisation. The higher the value, the higher the importance.

        **Inactivity** indicates whether the organisation needs to access this information to actively use it. The more likely the organisation is to want quick access, the higher the level of activity.

          title: Avoiding storage problems
          copy: |
            To avoid storage problems, a business can periodically review its information systems and take appropriate actions. These may include:
            * Removing and deleting duplicate documents and leaving a single copy
            * Removing and deleting unimportant documents that will never be used again
            * Archiving files that have to be retained.

            Most well-run businesses specify in their procedures when archiving and deleting can take place and who has the authority to make decisions on what can be archived.
