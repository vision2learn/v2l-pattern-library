On the menu that appears, you’ll see two options – Insert Columns and Delete Columns. Select one of these options depending on the change you want to make to your table.

    title: Readability
    theme: t-bg-supporting3
    copy: |
      It is important to take care when formatting your table, as you can easily make the contents of it difficult to read.

      For example, be careful not to shade your table in a colour that gives too little contrast with your text, or hide the gridlines in a way that makes it difficult for your reader to follow the flow of data in the table.

      You should take the time to check the tables in your document to make sure they are formatted correctly. For example, you should check:

      * The text and data you have entered into the table
      * The width of the table columns
      * The height of the table rows
      * The style of your table to make sure it is easy to read on the page.

      ### Text wrapping

      As well as formatting your table, you can also change how text in your document appears alongside the table. For example, if you have a table in your document, and you want the text to be positioned around it (rather than simply above and below it), you can format the table to allow text wrapping.
