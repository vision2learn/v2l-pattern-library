    copy: |
      As well as checking the accuracy of your spelling and grammar, you can also check your work by counting how many words are in your document – and there is a tool in Microsoft Word that you can use to do this.

      To carry out a word count, select Tools – Word Count from the top menu. A dialog box will appear on screen, which will show you how many pages, words, characters, paragraphs and lines are currently in your document.

      This tool is particularly useful if you have a word limit to stick to – for example, if you are writing a 1000-word report.
