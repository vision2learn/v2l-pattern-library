    copy: |
        When you open PowerPoint, each slide will appear with a number of placeholders. These are specific areas of the slide that you can start typing into. These placeholders can be for titles, subtitles, text, lists or any other information you want to include!
        
        To add text to your PowerPoint, simply left click on a placeholder and start typing to add content. Alternatively, click on the *Insert* tab, and then click on *Text Box* in the *Text* group to add a new text box to type into.

    title: Formatting text
    theme: t-bg-primary
    copy: |
        You can format the content of your presentation in order to change the way it looks. The first thing you need to do before formatting any text is to select it, so that PowerPoint knows what you are trying to do. To do this, point with your mouse at the word you want to select and then either double-click with the left mouse button to select a single word, or drag the mouse over the word (or words) to select. Your selected text will appear highlighted on the screen. Once you have highlighted the text, you are ready to format it. 
        
        Some of the most commonly used formatting options that are in the *Font* group are:

            To change the font type click the small arrow to the right of the font type menu to pull down the list of available typefaces. The text you have selected will now appear in this new font type.

            Select the text you want to emphasise and then click the relevant button in the *Font* group.

          title: Using colour
          copy: |
            When formatting your presentation, you can choose to apply colour to your text.

            To change the colour of text in your presentation, select the text you want to change, then click the *Font Color* button in the *Font* group. Your selected text will now change to the colour you have chosen.

Highlight your text and then click one of the alignment buttons in the *Paragraph* group.
