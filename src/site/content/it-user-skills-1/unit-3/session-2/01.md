    title: Changing the order of your presentation
    theme: 
    copy: |
      You can change the order of slides in your presentation and it can be very useful to be able to do this. For example, if you are putting together a particularly detailed presentation, you might lose track of the content you have added to each slide. It is often only when you finish the presentation and review your work that you realise that some information might be better placed elsewhere.
    subInc: 
      type: accordion
      content:
        -
          title: Slide order
          copy: |
            If you want to make changes to the order of slides this is a quick and easy task to do within the slides panel.

            1. Down the left-hand side of the screen, ensure that the Outline and Slides tabs are visible. If they are not, select View – Normal from the top menu.
            2. In the Slides panel, click and drag to change the order of your slides. Left-click with your mouse button on the slide you want to move and then, while holding the mouse button down, drag the slide to its new position. Release the mouse button to ‘drop’ the slide into the new position.
            3. You will see that the chosen slide now appears in a different point in your presentation and that your slides have all been renumbered to account for this change in order.
          img: /images/svg/it2/it2_u2_s3_p2_reveal2.svg
