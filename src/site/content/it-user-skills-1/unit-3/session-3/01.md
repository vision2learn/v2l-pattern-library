      As well as adding text to your presentation, you can also include images to enhance the content of your slides. 
      
      It’s easy to change the size and position of the images you insert into your presentation. For example, you might want to make the image a bit smaller or move it so that it is aligned with the text on the slide.

          title: Resize images
          text: |
            The first thing you need to do to resize an image is click on the image to select it. When the image is selected, it will appear with small circular handles around the edge. To resize your image, simply click and drag any of these handles, then release your mouse button once your image is the desired size.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Position images
          text: |
            If you want to move your image around the slide, first click on the image so that it is selected (again, you will see the small circular handles around the image). Now, click on the main image and drag it into its new location on the slide. When you’re happy with the position of the image, release your mouse button to ‘drop’ it into place.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Crop images
          text: |
            Another way to change the size of an image is to crop it, which involves cutting and removing sections of the image.
            
            To crop your image, the first thing you need to do is open up the Picture Toolbar.

      You can add sound files to your presentation in a similar way. Simply select Insert – Movies and Sounds – Sound from File from the top menu and then navigate to the sound file you want to include.
