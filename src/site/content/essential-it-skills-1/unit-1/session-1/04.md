      Your computer keyboard enables you to quickly and easily enter text and numbers into your document. 

      *Include the following within the interactivity*
      
      **Text**
      The main area of your keyboard displays the letters of the alphabet – by pressing these keys in sequence, you can type words and sentences. If you want to type capital letters, simply hold the Shift key down and then press the relevant letter key. If you’re typing a lot of capital letters continuously, turn the ‘caps lock’ function on by pressing the Caps Lock key and then just type as normal – Word will type all of your text in capitals until you press the Caps Lock key again to turn the ‘caps lock’ off.

      **Numbers**
      To enter numbers in your document, there are two options on your keyboard. You will see numbers 0 – 9 across the top of your keyboard, and they also appear in a grid (like a telephone keypad) on the right hand side of the keyboard. You can use either set of keys to type numbers into your document.

      **Symbols and special characters**
      As well as using text and numbers, you can also include a range of symbols and special characters in your document.

      *Include this:*

      The Alt key and the Ctrl key can both be used to perform certain shortcuts. By pressing these keys in combination with others, you can perform lots of different tasks. A few examples are listed below:

      Ctrl + A – Selects everything in the current document.
      Ctrl + B – Applies bold formatting to the selected text.
      Ctrl + C – Copies the selected text.
      Ctrl + I – Applies italic formatting to the selected text.
      Ctrl + S – Saves the current document.
      Ctrl + U – Underlines the selected text.
      Ctrl + V – Pastes any copied text.
      Ctrl + X – Cuts the selected text.
      Ctrl + Z – Allows you to undo your most recent action.
      Alt + F4 – Closes the current document.
      Alt + underlined letter in menu – Selects the menu option.
      Alt + Spacebar – Opens the main menu for the program you are using.

      ### Insert a symbol or special character

      *Include this:*

      In your Word document, place the cursor at the point you want to insert a character or symbol and then select Insert from the top menu and select Symbol from the drop-down menu. (In Microsoft Word 2007, select Insert from the top menu, then go to the Symbols box and click on the Symbol drop-down menu.)

      This will open up a dialog box with two tabs at the top. You can click these tabs to insert symbols, like squares or arrows, or special characters (such as © to ™).

      Simply click on the symbol or character you want to use and then click the Insert button – your chosen symbol / character will then appear in your document.

      ### Typing skills

      *INCLUDE THIS:*

      There is a technique called ’touch typing‘ that can help you to type more accurately. Touch typing means that you can type without looking at the keys on your keyboard. This helps you to type more quickly and more accurately.

      When you are touch typing, you begin by resting your thumbs on the spacebar and placing your eight fingers over the following keys on the keyboard:

      The F and J keys usually have bumps on them, so you can feel if your fingers are in the right place.

      With your fingers in this position, it is possible to reach all the other keys easily by moving your fingers on to the keys above and below your starting point.

      It takes time to learn how to touch type, but it will come with practice and there are lots of useful lessons available on the Internet for you to follow. As your touch typing skills improve, you will remember where all of the keys are on the keyboard and this really helps you to be more accurate when you are typing.
