        ### Progress or stagnate?

        A negative response to changes in working methods, products or services is likely to result in difficulties. The change process is likely to be delayed, disrupted or possibly stopped altogether. This can frustrate staff who wish to make changes and may even result in the business stagnating – not moving forwards – as a result of other staff resisting change.

        A positive response to changes in working methods, products or services is likely to result in a smoother transition. The business is more likely to be able to progress because of the willingness to accept and embrace change.

        The priority of a business is to make sure that employees respond positively to change, which in turn can provide the momentum for the change to occur.

      ### Examples of good responses
      
      A business that wants staff to respond positively to change may look for and encourage the following responses - scroll through the slides below to learn more:
    subInc: 
      type: slider
      title: 
      slides:
        -
          title: A willingness to learn
          text: This will include the willingness to learn new skills and procedures. These people have a desire to learn and then eagerly apply what they have learned in the workplace. These learners do not passively wait to be taught, they will seek out answers and ask questions.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: A willingness to teach
          text: |
            Staff are willing to share their knowledge, skills and experience with others. This will include helping co-workers to adapt to workplace changes.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: A willingness to solve problems
          text: These people tend to find solutions to problems and difficulties. When a problem occurs these people do not quit or passively wait for others to sort things out. Instead they use their best efforts to find a solution or support those who find a solution.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Maintaining a positive outlook
          text: |
            A process of change can be challenging and demanding. If staff have a happy, can-do attitude then they cope far better with the situation they find themselves in. When people are happy they don't feel stressed or anxious. Furthermore a positive outlook encourages co-workers to be positive.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Enjoying the chance to change and adapt
          text: If people actively enjoy the process of changing then they will see change in an organisation as a positive experience that is to be welcomed. These people tend to change quickly and easily.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
    copy2: These qualities may be sought during recruitment and selection processes. Attention may also be brought to them when an organisation is making significant changes.
