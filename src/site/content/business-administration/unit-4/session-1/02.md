      ### Pressure to change
      There are continuous pressures for change to be implemented in business. It is helpful to consider **reactive changes** when the business changes as a response to external pressures and **proactive changes** when the business changes due to internal demands.

    title: Case study - Political unrest
    layout: media
    theme: t-bg-primary1a i-case-study
    copy: |
      ### Consider a tour operator

      A sustained period of political unrest in a popular tourist destination will have significant implications for a tour operator. The operator will have to deal with many people wishing to cancel and/or move their holidays. There will also be less demand for holidays to a place that is experiencing civil unrest.

      Such an external change can have a dramatic impact on the operation of an organisation. In the worst case, if the organisation fails to adapt it may go out of business.

            **Change** - The use of new office IT can improve the speed, cost and quality of the work that is done.

          title: Law
          text: |
            **Change** - The Government makes changes in the law.

            **Organisational consequences of not making any changes**  
            The organisation breaks the law resulting in fines / business closure / prison sentences.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Money
          text: |
            **Change** - Consumers expect the price of certain products to fall through time.

            **Organisational consequences of not making any changes**  
            Failure to drive down price can lead to customers going elsewhere.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Production
          text: |
            **Change** - New computer aided production systems can lead to substantial cuts in production costs.

            **Organisational consequences of not making any changes**  
            Organisations unable to match the new technology improvements may decline or go out of business.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Economy - Recession
          text: |
            **Change** - The recession has drastically reduced the demand for an organisation’s services.

            **Organisational consequences of not making any changes**  
            Excess spending compared with demand for services / continued production when it is not justified by demand / losses and ultimately potential closure.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Economy - Exchange rate
          text: |
            **Change** - Changes in exchange rates make imports from competitors far cheaper.

            **Organisational consequences of not making any changes**  
            Fall in demand for products necessitates a cut in production and new efforts in sales and marketing - if this does not happen, the organisation will suffer.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Delivery
          text: |
            **Change** - A Government agency cancels a large order.
