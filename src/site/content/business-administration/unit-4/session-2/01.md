    theme: t-bg-supporting2

      ### Methods, products and services

      In session 1 you considered the influences that may create pressure for businesses to change. These can create pressures ranging from the very small to the very significant. Sometimes one small change can have a big effect on a whole operation.

      They occur all the time so businesses need to be diligent in detecting these influences and responding appropriately to them. See below to learn more.
    subInc: 
      type: accordion
      content:
        - 
          title: Impact on two areas
          copy: |
            It is usually possible for a business to assess the potential impact of change in two broad areas. These are:
            * Impact on working methods
            * Impact on the products or services provided.

            The impact of change could affect either of these options or both, simultaneously. There will be many changes that have no impact at all on a business/organisation.

            There are literally thousands of external changes happening all the time that are not relevant to an organisation or their impact is so small as to be meaningless.

      ### The bottom line
      
      In business it is important to keep up with developments and review the impact of these on a regular basis. If this is not done it can have far-reaching repercussions for the organisation. 
      
      Scroll through the slides below to see examples:

      The bottom line is that if organisations fail to make appropriate changes in their work practices, products or services then they may lose some or all of their customers. This means they risk going out of business.
      
      Most changes in products or services will necessitate changes in work practices. The structure of people’s jobs and the tasks they have to do will need to change.

    title: Examining change
    theme: t-bg-supporting3

      ### Two simple questions
      When examining the internal and external changes that impact on a business, it helps to ask two simple questions:
      1. How does [x] impact on the work methods of the business?
      2. How does [x] impact on the products or services of the business?

      It's worth bearing in mind that answers will depend on the type of business and what it sells. Select the images below to see examples:

      The aim is to answer these questions in depth and note down exactly what changes the organisation will be making. 
    subInc:

          title: For large-scale changes the detail could be...
          copy: |
            * Product by product
            * Service by service
            * Department by department
            * Team by team
            * Employee by employee
            * Task by task

            Taking the time to answer the key questions in detail will help to identify all the changes that the organisation will need to make if it is to continue to operate successfully. This is a detailed review of everywhere the proposed changes are expected to happen.
