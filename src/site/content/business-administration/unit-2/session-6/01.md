      ###  Instructions and travel arrangements
      Booking travel and accommodation is important for business.

      Often, administrative staff are expected to make all the necessary arrangements, on a budget. It's a good idea to have travel and accommodation budgets as often these costs can spiral out of control.

      With careful planning and clear thinking it is possible to successfully arrange travel and accommodation for work colleagues.

    theme: t-bg-primary1a i-case-study
    copy: |
      ### Ensure everything is covered

      Confirming arrangements ensures that no mistakes are made.

      Take a look at this scenario:
    subInc:
      type: accordion
      content:
        -
          title: Scenario
          copy: |
            Bill, a salesman, asks his colleague Sam to make arrangements for a two-night stay in St Ives (the following Wednesday and Thursday nights) and a return train ticket for a 1pm arrival on the Wednesday.

            Sam makes all the bookings. The hotel is paid for on a non-refundable basis so Sam gets a large discount. The ticket is also bought.

            On the Tuesday before the business trip Bill calls into the office to get his tickets and paperwork.

            He is shocked when Sam explains that there is not a train station in St Ives but one nearby in Huntingdon. Sam is shocked when Bill says there is a train in St Ives, Cornwall. The arrangements had been booked for St Ives in Cambridgeshire.
          img: /images/svg/ba_u2_s6_p1_accordion_1.svg
    copy2: The scenario shows how a small misunderstanding can lead to significant problems. The failure to confirm the details in this case resulted in wasted time and money. It also meant that Bill's arrangements weren't suitable for him.
