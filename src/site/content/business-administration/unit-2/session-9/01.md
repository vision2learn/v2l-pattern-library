      ###  The first point of contact

      A receptionist is often the first point of contact in a business. Therefore they often give the first impression of what a business is like.

      The challenge for a receptionist is to balance being quick and efficient with being friendly. This challenge is even greater when things are busy.  It is especially important for receptionists in service-based businesses, such as law firms, doctors' surgeries and hotels, to act professionally and be friendly at all times.

      Receptionists who don't deal with as many people will sometimes do administrative duties too.

      ###  Welcoming other people

      The visitors that a receptionist deals with depend on the type of business and the products and services it supplies.

      There are many ways that visitors may be categorised. Which characteristics are relevant will vary from one business to another and may include:
      * Private visitors or business contacts
      * Frequent visitors/one-off visitors
      * Local visitors/visitors who are not local.
      
      The type of visitor will influence the reception service that needs to be provided. For example, some receptionists in health centres dealing with large numbers of people who do not have English as a first language may be expected to be fluent in more than one language.
