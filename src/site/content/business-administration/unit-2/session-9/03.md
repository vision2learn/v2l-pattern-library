      ### Consider security for all

      In virtually all businesses the reception area is at the front entrance of the main building. This means that most receptionists have a security role. See below to learn more.
    subInc: 
      type: accordion
      content:
        - 
          title: Location
          copy: |
            The reception should be located so that it is not possible to enter the building without going through reception.
        - 
          title: Security
          copy: |
            Businesses that have serious security considerations may have a gatehouse manned by security guards controlling who enters the premises.
        - 
          title: Shared building
          copy: |
            Smaller businesses often share buildings and may share one receptionist but sometimes have their own. Many businesses have intercom systems so the receptionist can see or hear visitors before letting them into the building.
        - 
          title: Procedures
          copy: |
            There should be some appropriate procedures so that security, confidentiality and general efficiency are maintained.

    title: Following procedures
    layout: media
    theme: t-bg-primary
    copy: |
      ###  Entry and departure

      By having procedures to follow, receptionists can systematically manage and monitor visitors entering into and departing from business premises.

      They are also able to monitor where visitors go and ensure they do not go to places that are out of bounds to visitors. This makes the business a more secure workplace.

      But it's important to make sure that procedures are followed by everyone, otherwise they can be compromised.

    theme: t-bg-supporting3

      Receptionists may be responsible for keeping a record of visitors into the building or business premises where they work. Select the images below to discover the key procedures that receptionists are responsible for.
    subInc:

      content:
        -
          title: On duty
          copy: |
            **Reception procedure**  
            Always ensure receptionist is on duty.

            **Purpose of the procedure**  
            This helps to prevent unauthorised access.
          img: ba_u2_s9_p3_reveal_1.svg
        -
          title: Confidential paperwork
          copy: |
            **Reception procedure**  
            Confidential or 'sensitive' paperwork to be kept away from reception.

            **Purpose of the procedure**  
            Reception is a public area where there is scope for many people to see documents that they shouldn't.
          img: ba_u2_s9_p3_reveal_2.svg
        -
          title: Computer confidentiality
          copy: |
            **Reception procedure**  
            When using a computer, ensure that the screen is positioned so that visitors are unable to see the screen. If they have to leave their station, they should lock the computer screen.

            **Purpose of the procedure**  
            Reception is a public area where there is scope for people to see information on the screen that they shouldn't.
            
          img: ba_u2_s9_p3_reveal_2b.svg
        -
          title: Log book
          copy: |
            **Reception procedure**  
            All visitors enter their arrival and departure times in a log book.

            **Purpose of the procedure**  
            It is important for the business to know how many visitors are on the premises.
          img: ba_u2_s9_p3_reveal_3.svg
        -
          title: Car registration
          copy: |
            **Reception procedure**  
            Visitor's car registration plates are recorded.

            **Purpose of the procedure**  
            Visitor can be contacted if their car has to be moved.
          img: ba_u2_s9_p3_reveal_4.svg
        -
          title: ID badges
          copy: |
            **Reception procedure**  
            Visitors are issued with ID badges.

            **Purpose of the procedure**  
            This allows people who are on the premises without permission to be identified and removed.
          img: ba_u2_s9_p3_reveal_5.svg
        -
          title: Promptness
          copy: |
            **Reception procedure**  
            The person that the visitor has come to see is told promptly.

            **Purpose of the procedure**  
            This ensures the receptionist can verify the visitor's identity and ensure they are seen promptly.
          img: ba_u2_s9_p3_reveal_6.svg
        -
          title: Visitor badges
          copy: |
            **Reception procedure**  
            Visitor badges are collected when the visitor leaves the building.

            **Purpose of the procedure**  
            This helps to record when a visitor has left and prevents them being able to take the badge from the premises.
          img: ba_u2_s9_p3_reveal_7.svg
        -
          title: Visitor belongings
          copy: |
            **Reception procedure**  
            Visitors must take belongings with them when they leave.

            **Purpose of the procedure**  
            If items are left they may cause a security scare.
          img: ba_u2_s9_p3_reveal_8.svg

      It is important that reception procedures are followed. This can be done by:
