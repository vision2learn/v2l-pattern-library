      ###  What is a meeting?

      Meetings are a significant part of any business. A meeting occurs when a number of people assemble together to discuss a topic.

      The purpose of this information exchange will depend upon the type of meeting that is taking place. People at the meeting may belong to the same group, to different groups, or perhaps not to any group at all.

      Meetings might be called by an individual or by a team. They can be 'one off' meetings or held at regular intervals. Meetings will have a pre-agreed date and time.

      In most cases meetings are held in a specific location. But technology allows meetings to be held by people thousands of miles apart using telephone conference calls or video conferencing.

      ###  What's the objective

      Without high-quality information, not many meetings will achieve their objective – their goal.

      A meeting brief will help you to specify its purpose and focus. It should also set out vital information including:
      * Who needs to attend
      * When it should occur
      * The main resources required.
      For regular meetings, the brief may just need tweaked from one meeting to the next. For more spur-of-the-moment meetings, the brief may need to be created from scratch.

      It is important to get the brief confirmed by a senior member of staff.
