      ###  Purpose and usage

      Why is it important to use a diary?

      Think about the consequences of not using one. For example, meetings would be forgotten about and the precious time of colleagues might be wasted, or rooms could be double-booked.

      A practical solution such as a diary management system helps to record employee activity and the resources that employees need.

      As you work through this session you will find out about different types of diary systems, including personal work diaries and office diary systems.
