      ###  Computerised diary management systems

      Office diary systems are often complex or vast.

      These systems can provide:

      * Information on customer appointments and resource availability
      * Personal diary tracking for staff
      * Facilities to arrange meetings
      * Reminders for scheduled meetings
      * Prompts for future actions.
      There are several different types of office diary system.

      ### Maintaining diaries

      Diary systems can form the basis for managing key activities.  It helps to think about the information needed to maintain office diary systems accurately in terms of the 5 Ws – who, what, when, where and why.  See below for some examples of this:
    subInc:
      type: accordion
      content:
        -
          title: Scenario 1
          copy: |
            Imagine you have an appointment with a customer. You would need the following information:  
            * **Who** is the meeting with?  
            * **What** is the meeting about?  
            * **When** is the meeting?  
            * **Where** is the meeting?  
            * **Why** are you having the meeting?
        -
          title: Scenario 2
          copy: |
            A course has been arranged for staff in your organisation.
            * **Who** is going on the course? Who will be delivering it?
            * **What** will the course cover? What resources are required?
            * **When** is the course?
            * **Where** is the course being held?
            * **Why** should staff go on this course?
    copy2: |
      Failure to record this information can lead to problems.

      For example, in the case where a course has been arranged for staff, failure to record where the venue is can lead to people not turning up. If resources needed are not recorded then they may not be reserved and the course may not go ahead.

      The system is likely to fail if people don't do the following:
      * Put any information into the system
      * Update any changes in the details
      * Put only part of the information into the system
      * Put inaccurate information into the system.

      ###  Following a system

      Diary systems used by businesses will differ.

      Each business will have its own approach to diary management. When a new employee joins a business it is important that they learn how to use the system preferred in-house.

      To ensure the diary system is efficient, it's best practice for businesses to specify the information needed in the system. They should also ensure that employees are fully trained in using the system.
