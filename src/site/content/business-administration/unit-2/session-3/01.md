      Administrative tasks require a lot of equipment.

      This equipment helps workers to do tasks efficiently, and includes computers, printers, scanners and photocopiers.

    title: Computers
    theme: 
    copy: |
      ### Computers are everywhere
      All businesses are likely to use computers.

      With the right software, computers can provide a wide range of functions such as word processing, spreadsheet and database creation, accounting and publishing.

      In most offices computers are networked and connected to the Internet, which allows easy and instantaneous communication.

      There can be an over-reliance on computers and the Internet, however. Take a look at this scenario which demonstrates this.

            Imagine you use a computer every day in your job. You're a researcher who uses a specialist online package to research competitors.

            You use Microsoft Office (Word and Excel) to type reports on your competitors, and use a specialist database to record any contact with your customers. You also file paper copies of the reports you create on a regular basis.

            The network and servers fail one day, which means you are unable to access your work on your computer and you're unable to access the Internet. How would you do your job? What other tasks could you be doing, and how would you do them without IT?

            * You could use a phone book to make calls to customers.

    title: Printers
    theme: t-bg-supporting2
    copy: |
      ### Features and functions
      Printers are used to produce copies of documents from computer files.  There are two types of printer - Select the images below to learn more.
    subInc: 
      type: reveal
      content:
        -
          title: Inkjet printer
          copy: |
            These tend to be smaller, cheaper and slower than laser printers. They shoot very fine jets of ink onto the surface of the paper. In most cases they have two ink cartridges, one in black and one in colour. Often they are linked to an individual computer.
          img: ba_u2_s3_p1_reveal_1.svg
        -
          title: Laser printer
          copy: |
            These are larger and more expensive than inkjet printers. They print quickly and to a high standard. The printer can be linked to several computers. This type of printer works by shining a laser beam onto a photo-sensitive drum and then uses toner to print an image onto paper. Laser printers often need maintenance.
          img: ba_u2_s3_p1_reveal_2.svg
    copy2: |
      There are many features available on printers, including:
      * One-sided or back-to-back printing
      * Size of paper tray
      * Paper tray
      * Colour
      * Volume of printing
      * Networking ability (the ability to link with one or more computers)
      * Print resolution (this is rated in dots per inch or dpi)

      There are hundreds of printers offering all-in-one solutions which combine printing, scanning, photocopying and faxing.

    title: Photocopiers, Fax machines and scanners
    theme: t-bg-primary
    copy: |
      Photocopiers are used to make exact copies of documents. This includes letters, papers and reports. With photocopiers you can make one copy or many copies – so they're useful for making multiple copies of one document, such as a sales letter. Photocopiers have the capacity to produce copies quicker and cheaper than inkjet printers.

      They come in a wide variety of sizes, speeds and features.

      Photocopiers are often incorporated into all-in-one, multi-purpose printer/scanner/fax machines. These machines are capable of high performance and, because they're all in one, save space in offices.

      See below for more information
    subInc: 
      type: accordion
      content:

          title: Photocopiers

            Features of photocopiers:

            * One-sided and back-to-back copying
            * Speed

            * Image rotation (ability to rotate images)
            * Finishing (ability to collate paged documents/staple them/hole-punch them)
            * Copier memory (number of pages that the copier can retain in its memory
            * Colours (black and white or colour)
            * Reducing/enlarging (ability to reduce or enlarge images)

    title: Developments - REWRITE?
    theme: t-bg-primary
    copy: |
      ###  Keep pace

      Office equipment is continually being improved. New equipment and technologies, such as tablets (like the iPad) and netbooks are making their way into the office environment.

      In some cases, developments mean that old models and makes become obsolete and must be replaced by newer models.

      For this reason it is important to periodically review the equipment used and see if any changes can be made to make the equipment used more efficient or better value for money.
