      ### What do you need
      Administrative tasks require a lot of equipment. This equipment helps workers to do tasks efficiently, and includes computers, printers, scanners and photocopiers.

    title: Printers

      ### Features and functions
      Printers are used to produce copies of documents from computer files.  There are two types of printer - Select the images below to learn more.
    subInc: 
      type: reveal
      content:
        -
          title: Inkjet printer
          copy: |
            These tend to be smaller, cheaper and slower than laser printers. They shoot very fine jets of ink onto the surface of the paper. In most cases they have two ink cartridges, one in black and one in colour. Often they are linked to an individual computer.
          img: ba_u2_s3_p1_reveal_1.svg
        -
          title: Laser printer
          copy: |
            These are larger and more expensive than inkjet printers. They print quickly and to a high standard. The printer can be linked to several computers. This type of printer works by shining a laser beam onto a photo-sensitive drum and then uses toner to print an image onto paper. Laser printers often need maintenance.
          img: ba_u2_s3_p1_reveal_2.svg
    copy2: |
      There are many features available on printers, including:
      * One-sided or back-to-back printing
      * Size of paper tray
      * Paper tray
      * Colour
      * Volume of printing
      * Networking ability (the ability to link with one or more computers)
      * Print resolution (this is rated in dots per inch or dpi)

      There are hundreds of printers offering all-in-one solutions which combine printing, scanning, photocopying and faxing.

    title: Photocopiers, Fax machines and scanners
    theme: t-bg-primary
    copy: |
      Photocopiers are used to make exact copies of documents. This includes letters, papers and reports. With photocopiers you can make one copy or many copies – so they're useful for making multiple copies of one document, such as a sales letter. Photocopiers have the capacity to produce copies quicker and cheaper than inkjet printers.

      They come in a wide variety of sizes, speeds and features.

      Photocopiers are often incorporated into all-in-one, multi-purpose printer/scanner/fax machines. These machines are capable of high performance and, because they're all in one, save space in offices.

      See below for more information
