      ###  Royal Mail and others

      The best known external mail service is Royal Mail. This organisation accounts for the majority of mail and parcel delivery in the UK.

      There are smaller, private businesses that deliver parcels and larger items locally, nationally and internationally. In most cases items are delivered in 24-72 hours.  There are times when documents and packages need to be delivered on the same day, often within a few hours.

      Specialist courier mail services collect and deliver to these timescales but they are more expensive than mainstream postal delivery – it depends on the sort of service you want.

      ### Do your sums
      When calculating postage charges, it helps to ask some questions about the mail you're sending out. Then you can choose which method is best for you and work out the cost of the postage.

      Some businesses specify budgets or unit costs for mail. They can then look at the delivery options available to them and select the most appropriate one.

      See below to see some questions to ask when caluclating postage charges:
    subInc: 
      type: accordion
      content:
        - 
          title: What

            * Is the destination of the mail?

        -

            * Specific is the delivery time?
        - 
          title: Does

            * insured?

      ### What are your mail options?
      Comparing costs can mean you get a cheaper deal.  Once you understand the postal requirements for whatever needs to be sent, you can compare costs and choose the best package.

      Here are two ways you can do this:
      * Compare two or three providers
      * Use a comparision website

      It's worth remembering that some providers offer discounts for large volumes of post. This way, a business may be able to negotiate a good deal with the provider.
