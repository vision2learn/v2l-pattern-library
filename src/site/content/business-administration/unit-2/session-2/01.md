    theme: t-bg-supporting3
    copy: |
      ### What are the outcomes?
      Some of the likely consequences of poor mail handling are:
      * Lost customers
      * Lost money
      * Inefficient business

      Have a look at the table below showing how a business can be affected by the poor handling of incoming mail.

    subInc: 
      type: accordion
      content:
        - 
          title: How a business can be affected by the poor handling of incoming mail
          copy: |
            |Incoming mail affected| Potential damage caused|
            |----------------------|------------------------|
            |Requests for information about products and services| Potential lost business|
            |Orders for products/services | Lost business and income – short and long term |
            |Payments for products/services | Actual money not paid into the business – this can affect cash flow and means that effort must go into recollecting money |
            |Invoices from suppliers | Irritation to suppliers who chase up payments |
            |Important legal documents | Vital information and opportunities lost |
            |Parts, materials and equipment delivered by post | Workers unable to complete work tasks, unsatisfied customers and wasted time. |

            * Sort mail according to departments
          img: /images/svg/placeholder_icon.svg
        - 
          title: Procedures
          copy: |
            Procedure for dealing with internal mail:
            * Mail is delivered to or collected by different departments, by designated staff
            * It is put into the recipient's in-tray
