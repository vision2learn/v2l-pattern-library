    title: Plan in advance
    theme: t-bg-supporting2
    copy: |
      The advantage of making a call is you can plan it in advance.

      You also know the aim of the call. With a little thought you can make your calls far more effective.

      It is important to be clear about the purpose of a phone call.

      For example, you may be calling to:

      * Arrange a meeting
      * Have something delivered or order stock
      * Get some information
      * Provide some information.
      
      Select below for some dos and don'ts on steps to follow when making calls.

    subInc: 
      type: accordion
      content:
        - 
          title: Don't
          copy: |
            * Forget to leave space to write answers to any questions you have.
            * Speak fast or quietly. Speak at a moderate rate and be clear and concise.
            * Leave someone hanging on if you're asked a question you can't answer. Seek help.
            * Be rude or surly – people can notice this in your voice.
          img: /images/svg/placeholder_icon.svg
        - 
          title: Do
          copy: |
            * If you're ringing to get information (e.g. from suppliers), list the questions you need to ask and put them in a logical order.
            * If you're ringing to provide information (e.g. to customers), list the points you need to provide in a logical order.
            * Ask for clarification if you're unsure or can't hear.
            * Use an appropriate tone of voice. If you're ringing someone with good news, then sound happy.
          img: /images/svg/placeholder_icon.svg

    copy: |
      
      It makes sense to summarise calls.

      The longer and more detailed the call, the more important it is to summarise it. Check key facts and conclusions to overcome any misunderstandings.

      To summarise a call, run through the key points you have made with the person you're speaking to. Then check to see if anything has been left out. Keep your notes somewhere where they can be found easily.

    title: Be positive

    theme: t-bg-primary
    copy: |
      
      ### Answer with a smile
      
      How you answer the phone affects the image of the business.

      A caller can have a very positive experience, or they can have a negative experience that can put them off. This may be the difference between gaining customers and losing them.

      A positive image can be created if phone calls are 'opened' (answered) and 'closed' (ended) properly. Callers should be dealt with efficiently – this means you must know what you're doing and how to operate the phone system.

      It's not hard to create a positive image – even little things like saying please, thank you, talking in the right tone – can help.
