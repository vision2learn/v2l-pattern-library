      ###  How customer needs are met

      A business must be able to identify customer needs. One way of doing this is by collecting customer feedback. Feedback is important for businesses as it allows them to improve their products and services to customer requirements.

      See below to learn more:
    subInc: 
      type: accordion
      content:
        - 
          title: Customer feedback
          copy: |
            The feedback gathered will enable a business to determine whether their current products and services meet the needs of customers, and also to show customers that the business is thinking of them.

            There are several types of feedback – informal, focus groups, feedback forms and customer surveys, customer research and customer complaints.
            
          img: /images/svg/placeholder_icon.svg 
        - 
          title: Informal feedback
          copy: |
            Informal feedback can be collected by staff members who have contact with customers. The method of information collection tends to be informal, such as through conversations. This way staff can discover things such as:
            * Customer likes and dislikes
            * Customer preferences for the future
            * Changes the customer would like to see.

            This information needs to be collected and analysed so any themes can be identified. It is important that senior managers are informed of any trends so they can respond to them.
          img: /images/svg/placeholder_icon.svg

    title: Formal feedback
    theme: t-bg-supporting3
    copy: |
      More formal feedback is usually collected via other methods. These include the following:

    subInc: 
      type: slider
      title: 
      slides:
        -
          title: Focus groups
          text: |
            Here customers (or potential customers) are put into small groups and encouraged to discuss aspects of the products or services they buy. The aim is to identify any things that should influence current and future products or services.

            The focus group approach to collecting feedback gives customers an open forum to demonstrate their needs and expectations.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Feedback forms and customer surveys
          text: |
            A very helpful way of collecting information on customer needs and expectations is to ask them to complete a customer feedback form or to send out a survey or questionnaire. The replies given by customers can then be collated and any relevant themes can be identified. The aim is to get a flow of high quality customer information into an organisation. It is also possible to survey potential customers to find out what they need or expect.

            If the forms and surveys are properly designed they will provide the customer with an excellent opportunity to address their needs and expectations.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Former customers
          text: |
            A useful source of information on what customers need and expect may be gained from speaking to former customers. If these people now get their product or service from elsewhere it will be very helpful to know why.

            The fact that former customers have gone elsewhere is a clear demonstration that the customer no longer thinks their old supplier can meet their current needs and expectations. So the information here may be very valuable.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Customer complaints
          text: |
            In an ideal world all customers would be very happy with the products and services and they would have no need to complain.  However, when customers complain they are offering very clear insights into their needs and expectations. They will talk about what they wanted and expected and why they feel the need to complain. This information can have a significant impact on the running of the business.

            Naturally the first aim of the staff will be to correct any errors or immediate problems. They take the time to get as much useful information as possible. This information may shape the way products and services are provided.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Understanding customer needs
          text: |
            To satisfy customer needs, a business must design its systems, procedures and tasks to produce the desired outcomes. The aim is to agree outputs that the business can deliver. If delivery is below the level of customer needs there may be dissatisfaction and a loss of sales / customers or increased complaints.

            Once needs are being met, then the organisation can look for ways to become even more effective and efficient. It may be that the organisation seeks to substantially exceed customer needs. In this way, customer satisfaction may become customer delight!
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
