            ### Performance and efficiency

            All businesses strive to be the best.
            This can mean producing the best products and services while being as efficient as possible. This has implications for the organisation as a whole and for individual employees.

        theme: i-case-study
        copy: |

            Read the following scenario and then answer the questions below.

            ### The scenario

            Two businesses each make a very similar product and both businesses price the product at £5.
            Business A continues to operate in the same way, business B wants to make continuous improvements. Business B improves its performance at around 5% a year.
            After one year, the product made by business A is £5 and the product made by business B is £4.75. In five years' time, the difference is £5 versus £4. This substantial difference may cause new and existing customers to go to business B.
            In turn this leads to a fall in demand for Business A's products. Business A decides it needs to make redundancies in order to cope with the decline in demand.
            Business B can look to expand in order to cope with the extra customers and orders it has.
