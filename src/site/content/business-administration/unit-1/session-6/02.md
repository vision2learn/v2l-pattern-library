            ### How are you getting on?

            Most businesses have a formal process for reviewing performance.
            This process is most commonly known as a performance review or an appraisal.

            The process involves taking a systematic look at what the employee does in their job. This includes any tasks where an improvement in performance is necessary or desirable. Once improvement areas are pinpointed, the manager and employee will agree on ways that personal performance can be improved and the timescale for the improvements.

            These reviews tend to be done every 6-12 months so they create a framework for continuous performance review and improvement.

            ### Feedback from others

            In business you will have your work performance observed.

            This could be by team leaders, managers, supervisors and even customers.
            They may point out areas in which you can improve your performance. If their feedback is constructive and fair, you can take it on board and try to do something positive – improve your performance.

            There are many ways you can improve your performance in business – such as learning, mastering and applying new skills.

            What's out there for you?

            Once you've established yourself in a business and administration job role, you may wish to think about the work you do and your future.
            There is a wide variety of career paths available if you work hard enough.

            There are three helpful questions to ask when thinking about career pathways
            - What job do I want in the short, medium and long term?
            - Where do I want to do that job?
            - How do I get from where I am to where I want to be?
