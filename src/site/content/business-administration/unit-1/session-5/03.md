            ### Prepare to adapt
            In many cases, plans will have to be amended.
            If your plan does change, this can have repercussions for colleagues and business processes. If people have changes forced upon them at short notice, it makes it difficult for them to adapt their own plans in sufficient time.
            Ideally, you should give as much notice as possible. You should explain why the revision has occurred and what it means for your plans. If appropriate, you should apologise for the changes being made.

            ### How information is used
            Keeping people informed requires some form of communication.
            You learned about different communication methods in Session 3 of this Unit.
            One of the key things to keep in mind when deciding how people should be informed of progress is the way the organisation wants this information to be used.  Think about -
            - **What** information needs to be communicated 
            - **When** it should be communicated 
            - **Who** should receive the communication 
            - **How** it should be communicated.

            It is important to make sure you know what the formal channels are for progress reports. There may also be less formal methods for informing people about progress in many organisations.

            -
                title: Hints and tips
                copy: |
                    - Progress updates are usually targeted at a small number of people (and often only one).
                    - The information is generally provided promptly, so don't choose a method of communication that takes too long to prepare.
                    - In most cases (barring major incidents) the information on progress is usually focused upon a few specific details– such as "The delivery is on time" or "The number of units completed by 5pm will be 94 rather than 100".
                    - Often a verbal update (face to face or via the telephone) or a short note / email will be sufficient.
                    - If there are problems, there may be a need for more detailed communication.
