            ### Work standards and deadlines

            When work standards and deadlines are not met, many problems can arise. 
            
            Question 1
            Look at the list below and select the problems that you think may occur.

            Multiple answer type

            - Customers and clients of an organisation may not get the products or services that they expected

                title: Failed work standards - Example
                copy: |
                    
                    A production line in a car company is producing 4 doors (for 1 car) every 15 minutes. So the pace of work is for 4 cars an hour.
                    However, assembly lines in the company are geared up to produce one car every 10 minutes. So the pace of work is for 6 cars an hour. This means that for 20 minutes in every hour, the assembly workers can do very little.
                    If their work standard is to fit the doors on 6 cars every hour they are doomed to fail! The consequences of this difference in work standards could include the rest of the system having to slow down to match the pace of the car producers or the building of a huge backlog of 'doorless' cars.

            Organise your day
            It is important to plan your work and to be accountable to others.
            In your day-to-day life, you may make plans to do many things. If you change these plans, it may have little or no impact on other people.
            However, if you change your activities and plans in a work environment, this can have a significant impact on many colleagues and key processes. This can result in personal frustrations and poorer performance.
