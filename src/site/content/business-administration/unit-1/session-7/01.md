        title: Workplace problems
        theme: t-bg-supporting3
        copy: |
            What problems can occur?
            Problems can be divided into two categories.
            - Minor problems
            - Major problems

            Minor problems are small and usually have obvious, easy-to-implement solutions.

            Major problems are likely to have serious consequences and need careful consideration.

                    - A colleague is absent

                    - You have too many conflicting tasks and limited time.
