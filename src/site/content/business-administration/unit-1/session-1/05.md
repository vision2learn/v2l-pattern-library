<section>
  <h2>Equality</h2>

  <h3>Equal rights for all</h3>

  <p>
    In all organisations, it's important that all employees are treated in an equally fair way and are given equal opportunities.  Also, people doing the same job equally well should be rewarded fairly.
  </p>
  <p>
    Select the images below to read three key reasons for maintaining equality in the workplace.
  </p>

  <div class="l-media l-media--rev">
    <img class="l-media__img" src="/images/svg/BA_U1_S1_p5_Reveal_1.svg" alt="">
    <h4 class="l-media__title">Preventing unfair discrimination</h4>
    <p class="l-media__body">
      It is morally right to treat people fairly and to avoid unfair discrimination.
    </p>
  </div>

  <p>
    There is a moral and legal obligation to treat people on their merits and their ability to do the job. Both employers and employees should make sure that inappropriate labelling, stereotyping and prejudice do not inﬂuence the way an organisation operates.
  </p>
</section>

<section class="t-bg-primary">
  <h2>Summary</h2>
  <p>
    To ensure that people are treated fairly, an employer can:
  </p>
  <ul>
    <li>Train staﬀ on equality and diversity topics</li>
    <li>Develop policies and procedures to address equality and diversity issues</li>
    <li>Ensure that line managers appreciate diversity and promote fair treatment.</li>
  </ul>
</section>
