  <h2>Employment contracts</h2>
  
  <h3>Terms and Conditions</h3>

  <p>
    An employment contract is a very important document. It spells out the key things you can expect from your em- ployer and what your employer expects from you. Once you’ve worked for your employer for two months, you have the legal right to receive details of your terms and conditions of employment in writing.
  </p>

  <p>
    This information may come in a letter or a formal contract of employment. Alternatively, you may get a document outlining the main terms of employment, with signposts to where you can find other essential information.
  </p>

  <p>
    Select the following employment contract for an example of the information that must be included. The following additional information does not have to be included, but should be provided elsewhere:

  <h2>Key legislation</h3>

  <h3>What is covered?</h3>

  <p>
    Virtually every aspect of your employment is covered by law. In the UK, the law applies to businesses and organi- sations of all sizes and in all sectors.
  </p>

  <p>
    There are four main areas covered by legislation: 
  </p>

  <ol>
    <li>Health and safety</li>
    <li>Employment rights and responsibilities</li>
    <li>Pay and pensions</li>
    <li>Data protection</li>
  </ol>

<section class="t-bg-grey">
  <h2>Get specific!</h2>

  <h3>Sector specific legislation</h3>

  <p>
    Legislation applies to all businesses and organisations. They must find out which laws / regulations apply to them so they can follow these laws correctly. They should also ensure that each employee knows the law and how it relates to his or her job role.
  </p>
  <p>
    If a business claimed they did not know the law, it would be no defence if they got into trouble for not following the law.
  </p>
  <p>
    In many organisations, employees are told all about general and industry-specific laws during the induction process.
  </p>
  <p>
    Specific laws and regulations apply to areas such as food safety, employment agencies, private security industries and many more.
  </p>
</section>
