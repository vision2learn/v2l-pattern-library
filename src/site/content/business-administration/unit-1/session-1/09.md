## Equal rights for all

In all organisations, it's important that all employees are treated in an equally fair way and are given equal opportunities.

Also, people doing the same job equally well should be rewarded fairly.

### Preventing unfair discrimination

It is morally right to treat people fairly and to avoid unfair discrimination.  

### Laws

There are many laws created to punish unfair and discriminatory behaviour.

### Social and economic benefits

There are social and economic benefits of equal treatment.

There is a moral and legal obligation to treat people on their merits and their ability to do the job. Both employers and employees should make sure that inappropriate labelling, stereotyping and prejudice do not influence the way an organisation operates.

### In summary ![](../../images/icons/icon-summary.svg)

To ensure that people are treated fairly, an employer can:

  * Train staff on equality and diversity topics
  * Develop policies and procedures to address equality and diversity issues
  * Ensure that line managers appreciate diversity and promote fair treatment.
