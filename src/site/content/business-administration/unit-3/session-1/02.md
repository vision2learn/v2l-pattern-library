      ### Be specific

      When you do Internet research, it helps to be as specific as possible in your keyword search.
      
      If you're not specific, you might end up with results that are meaningless or lead you on the wrong path. Whichever search engine you use, try to be precise in your search criteria. Let's take a look at an example.
    subInc: 
      type: accordion
      content:
        - 
          title: Example search
          copy: |
            A student wants to know the total number of trade union members in the UK in 2018. 
            1. First she types Union membership into Google. This generates a lot of interesting results but the required information is not obvious. 2. Next, she types in British trade union membership 2018. This generates a number of results. 
            3. The forth result is Trade union membership 2018: statistical bulletin. This gives her the information she needs.

            Use [Google](https://www.google.co.uk) to try this search out and find the above pdf document.
