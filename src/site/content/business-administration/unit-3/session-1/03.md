          title: Word processing tools and software   
          text: |
            Word processing software is a common piece of IT used in business. It is used in conjunction with a keyboard to produce a full range of written documents including business letters, posters, briefing papers and reports. Errors can quickly be put right using word processing software, reducing the time spent on corrections.
            
            The most well-known word processing software is Microsoft Word. Others include Lotus Word Pro, Word Pad and Open Office.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Spreadsheets
          text: |
            Most businesses have to store, manage, manipulate and use financial and numerical information. Spreadsheets allow this to be done with ease, and can be thought of as specialised automatic calculators capable of performing tasks in seconds.
            
            The relative speed of spreadsheet calculations allow businesses to run through many 'what if' financial scenarios. The most commonly used spreadsheet software is Microsoft Excel. Other examples include Open Office and Lotus 1-2-3.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Databases  
          text: |
            A database is a structured collection of data/information that is organised in such a way that it fulfils a specific business purpose. For example, imagine you own a shop and the telephone numbers of your suppliers are stored in various locations – a supplier details card index, product information files and a spreadsheet containing order information. If a supplier's telephone number changes, you might have to update that information in all three places.
            
            In a database, however, you only have to update that information in one place – the supplier’s phone number is automatically updated wherever you use it in the database. Examples of database programs include Microsoft Access, dBase and Open Office.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Presentations  
          text: |
            Presentations can feature text, numbers, photos, scanned images, graphics, sound and video. This makes them ideal for illustrating key points or demonstrating visual data such as charts or graphs. Presentation software is used most effectively when the information included in presentations is communicated clearly and accurately with the audience. 
            
            Examples of presentation applications include Microsoft PowerPoint and Open Office. Presentation tools include Prezi and Slide Rocket.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Mobile information technology  
          text: |
            Mobile phones are becoming increasingly sophisticated and have evolved into so-called 'smartphones'. They allow you to read documents 'on the move' or away from your desk, perform calculations and communicate via email and social networks. In short, they're good for staying in touch with colleagues and contacts when you are not in the office. The newer tablet devices and netbooks are also useful for this purpose.

      ### What are the benefits?
      
      The use of IT is central to many job roles. It has become so central that it is sometimes hard to imagine what workplaces would be like without IT. To fully appreciate the benefits of IT, take a couple of moments to think about what your working life would be like without it. Specifically, think about tasks you would struggle to complete without using IT. Make a note of your thoughts in the box on the right of the page.
      
      See below to read some examples of how people struggle without IT.
    subInc: 
      type: accordion
      content:
        - 
          title: Examples
          copy: |
            1. Arranging meetings with colleagues is done by email software. Without it, many individual telephone calls would be required.   

            2. A customer wants to see the business brochure and a price list. If email was unavailable the brochure would need to be sent using a postal service and would not arrive until at least the next day.  

            3. The Internet is used for research purposes. Without it staff would need lots of desk-based reference material, or they would have to visit a library that had the reference material they needed.
