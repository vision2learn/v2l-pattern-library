      ### Purpose, layout, standards and deadlines

      It is important to know exactly what customers want – both internal and external. If this information is unknown or guessed, it's likely that customers will not get what they want.

      Time should be taken to agree on the specification of a document. This way, many potential problems can be avoided.

        -
          title: Purpose
          text: |
            **Agreement on the aspect**  
            Document can help the customer to move towards a desired objective / outcome.

            **No agreement on the aspect**  
            The document is unlikely to address the key issues. Resources will be wasted, poorer decisions made, projects may fail. Effort will have to be repeated. Time wasted.
          img: ba_u3_s4_p1_slide_1.svg
        -
          title: Content
          text: |
            **Agreement on the aspect**  
            Provides the information that is needed to get things done.

            **No agreement on the aspect**  
            Some or all of the information may be irrelevant. Some or all of the information that is needed may be absent. The ability to deliver outcomes is undermined.
          img: ba_u3_s4_p1_slide_2.svg
        -
          title: Layout
          text: |
            **Agreement on the aspect**  
            Enables the customer to readily access the information in the document. The information is presented in logical steps.

            **No agreement on the aspect**  
            Even if the document includes all the relevant information, the customer finds it difficult to follow what is being said and to find the required information as promptly as required.
          img: ba_u3_s4_p1_slide_3.svg
        -
          title: Quality standards
          text: |
            **Agreement on the aspect**  
            Enables the customer to specify key output measures. This can address simple things like the number of words and spelling accuracy.

            **No agreement on the aspect**  
            If the standards are not met then the customer experience of the document will not be good. The customer may have difficulty understanding the document or getting detail as quickly as possible.
          img: ba_u3_s4_p1_slide_4.svg
        -
          title: Deadlines
          text: |
            **Agreement on the aspect**  
            The customer can make arrangements knowing the document will be available from an agreed date / time. This aids efficient use of time and resources. The supplier can arrange his / her work in a way that delivers to the deadline.

            **No agreement on the aspect**  
            Customers are delayed as the document is not ready on time. The delay may lead to wasted resources. There may be frustration and conflict between the supplier and customer. External customers may be dissatisfied. Without an adequate deadline the person producing the document may rush it or do other things first when they should not do so.
          img: ba_u3_s4_p1_slide_5.svg

    title: Summary
    theme: t-bg-primary i-summary
    copy: |
      A failure in one area may significantly undermine all the work done in other areas. 
      
      For example, an ‘outstanding’ report that delivers on purpose, content, layout and quality standards but is three weeks late (poor on deadlines) may be worthless.   Likewise a document that is fine on purpose, layout, quality standards and deadline but misses some key content would also be of little use.
