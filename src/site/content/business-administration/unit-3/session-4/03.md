      ### Confidentiality and data protection

      Businesses should have clear procedures on data protection and confidentiality when producing documents. They are required by law to do so and it is also good, ethical business practice.

    subInc: 
      type: accordion
      content:
        - 
          title: Personal information
          copy: |
            The legal framework regulates the way that personal information is collected, stored, processed and distributed. When a business is creating and distributing documents, the rights and confidential information of individuals must be respected.

            If people thought a business could not respect confidentiality, they would withhold information or look for an alternative business. 
            
          img: /images/svg/ba_u3_s2_p3_slide_5.svg 
        - 
          title: The Data Protection Act 2018
          copy: |
            The Data Protection Act 1998 governs the use of personal information. This is done through eight data protection principles. The principles require that personal information is:

            * Processed fairly and lawfully
            * Processed for limited purposes
            * Adequate, relevant and not excessive
            * Accurate and up to date
            * Not kept for longer than is necessary
            * Processed in line with the rights of individuals
            * Secure
            * Not transferred to other countries without adequate protection.

    title: Access to confidential information
    layout: media
    theme: t-bg-supporting2
    copy: |
      The business should make it clear who has access to which items of data and for which purposes. The issues of access may apply to people other than staff – it can apply to people outside the organisation.

      Access to confidential information should be on a need-to-know basis. This means no-one should have access to information unless it is relevant to their work. Limits of confidentiality must also be set.

      You can revisit **Session 2** of this Unit if you would like to see some examples of confidentiality procedures.
