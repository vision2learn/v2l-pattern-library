      ### Author or someone else?

      The first person to check the document is likely to be the author. They should take the time to ensure the document meets the requirements of the recipient. However, it's often best to have someone else to look over the document as they won't have been working with it as closely as the author.

      On all word processing systems there are spell checking and grammar checking facilities, which can be used to check the accuracy of what has been written. However, these spell checkers don't pick up everything and it's best to use them in conjunction with reading a printed version of the document. For example, a spell checker won't recognise 'tow' as incorrect if the author meant to write 'two', as both are words.

      Technical terms should be checked thoroughly and independently if there is any doubt.
