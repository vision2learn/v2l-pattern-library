    title: The purpose of research
    theme: 
    copy: |
      ### Doing research

      Research is important in business, as it provides information that will inform business decisions. If information is not collected or is only partially collected, then this can lead to poor-quality decisions being made.

      Businesses conduct research for all sorts of reasons, including:

      * Customer research
      * Research into business equipment and costs
      * Researching market trends.
      
      If research into these areas is not done properly, then it can result in businesses spending too much money or gathering the wrong type of information for improving customer service.

    title: Case study - The research brief
    theme: t-bg-primary1a i-case-study
    copy: |
      ### Essential information

      Getting research right can be tough. It helps to have objectives and deadlines for what you want the research to achieve. Take a look at this scenario.

    subInc: 
      type: accordion
      content:
        - 
          title: Scenario
          copy: |
            A business manager asks a member of his department to look into car leasing options for six sales staff. A week later he asks the staff member for the information and receives a few details in note form.

            Some of the information is on wholly inappropriate vehicles. The business manager is very unhappy as he cannot start to make any decisions.

            It can be very easy to blame researchers if there is not sufficient information but it is best to brief them thoroughly beforehand.
          img: /images/svg/placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Handy tips on research briefs
          copy: |
             1. It helps to have a qualitative output – specific questions that can be answered when the research is completed.
             2. It can help to have a quantifiable output too – such as a research report or a word limit. This will help the researcher to understand the level of detail they need.
             3. If there is a specified deadline it helps to focus the researcher on delivering the outcomes in points 1 and 2.
             4. Points 1, 2 and 3 should be agreed by the researcher and the manager.

  -
    title: A thorough research brief
    theme: t-bg-primary
    copy: |
      Imagine the business manager handed the researcher a new, thorough brief. It would include the following items:

      1. Look at three-year leasing deals
      2. Mileage at 20,000 to 30,000 miles a year
      3. Four-door saloon cars from mid-range manufacturers
      4. Leasing firm within 20 miles of the business
      5. The report needs to show tables detailing how the leasing options compare
      The deadline is five working days.

      You can see that this brief is clearer than the original brief detailed above. The researcher is now in a position to be more precise and accurate in their work.

    title: Case study - A through research brief
    theme: t-bg-primary1a i-case-study
    copy: |
      Here's another example of the difference a detailed research brief can make.

    subInc: 
      type: accordion
      content:
        - 
          title: Brief presentation
          copy: |
            A manager asks a team member to produce a brief presentation on the work done in the department over the last twelve months. 
            
            A fortnight later the manager tells the team member that she will be making her presentation to the company’s board of directors and senior management team later that morning.

            The team member is completely shocked. She assumed that at some point she would informally present her thoughts to the manager. 
            
          img: /images/svg/placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: The team member did not know that
          copy: |
            * She was going to have to make a formal presentation
            * That the presentation would be at a specific place at a specific time
            * That the audience would be the senior people in the company
            * That the presentation would be a 30 minute talk followed by up to 15 minutes of questions and answers
            * The people attending would expect to see a PowerPoint presentation and later to have a copy of the presentation with the presentation notes
            * That the two projects for the retail sector should be the focus of the presentation
          img: /images/svg/placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Conclusion
          copy: |
            In the absence of this information the presentation was a disaster. It made the manager, the team member and the whole team look incompetent.

            Had the briefing by the manager been more specific and included all the agreed objectives and deadlines, the research and the presentation would have been of a far higher quality.
