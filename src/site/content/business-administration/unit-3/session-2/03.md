            ### What are the procedures?

            It is important that staff follow procedures concerning the information that is to be stored and the information that does not need to be stored.

            For a manual storage system for paper files, material should only be filed once it has been read and dealt with.

            For electronic systems, information is usually held in computer files. Paper files that require electronic storage should be scanned first, then the paper copy either filed or shredded.
          
          img: /images/svg/placeholder_icon.svg

          title: Retrieving information
          copy: |
            ### Requesting info

            When information is to be retrieved it is important that the person wanting it has the authority to request it. For example, confidential information should not be available to everybody.

            Some businesses use a sign-out procedure to assign the documents to the person who has requested them. This makes it possible to track the file and know its whereabouts until it is returned.

        ### What must be kept?

        When something is archived it is put into storage. This is associated with paper-based files. Filing these documents often has problems. See below to learn more.
    subInc: 
      type: accordion
      content:
        - 
          title: Problems with storage
          copy: |
            * Folders get full quickly
            * Cabinet drawers and whole cabinets become full with no space for new files
            * There is no space for new cabinets
            * There is no space for important files.
          img: /images/svg/placeholder_icon.svg
        - 
          title: Avoiding storage problems
          copy: |
            To avoid storage problems, a business can periodically review its information systems and take appropriate actions. These may include:
            * Removing and deleting duplicate documents and leaving a single copy
            * Removing and deleting unimportant documents that will never be used again
            * Archiving files that have to be retained.

            Most well-run businesses specify in their procedures when archiving and deleting can take place and who has the authority to make decisions on what can be archived.

        Businesses keep a lot of information. Some of this information is sensitive or confidential, so it can't be viewed or accessed by everyone in the business. Access to confidential information should be strictly limited and secure.

          title: Why is information confidential?
          copy: |
            Certain information is kept confidential for several reasons. These include:

            * Legal requirements, including complying with legislation such as the Data Protection Act or the content of legally enforceable contracts.
            * Commercial requirements, such as keeping confidential information secret. A lack of confidentiality can show a lack of competence and/or trust.
            * Moral requirements. There is a strong moral obligation to respect people's privacy and confidentiality. If this is broken then people will withhold information and lack trust.
            
            People need to know what they can and cannot access and the repercussions that may follow if confidence and trust are broken.
