      * Production
      * Sales
      * Functional
      * Master
      * Capital expenditure
      * Departmental
      
      See below to see some examples of these different budgets.

          title: Production budget
          copy: |
            A production budget will specify the quantities of goods to be produced over a given timescale. It will also specify the costs of direct materials, direct labour and factory overheads for the production of the goods.

          title: Departmental budget
          copy: |
            Many organisations will have budgets for specific departments. These may be classified as operational budgets (such as marketing or distribution) or support budgets (such as finance or administration).
