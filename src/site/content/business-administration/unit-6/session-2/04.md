    copy: |
        Due to some bad publicity, the board of directors at Bargainshop want training for legal compliance – such as health and safety – to be completed in the first two months of the next financial year, rather than spreading it throughout the year.

        The board have also responded to a proposal from Jorge, the training manager, suggesting that a bespoke, accredited company-wide supervisor and management programme be developed with a local college. For this reason, the training for supervisors and managers is to be paired for a minimum of 18 months, and then increased with the introduction of the new programmes.

        The training department now need to revise their budgetary work as priorities and timescales have changed.
