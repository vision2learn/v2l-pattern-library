    title: Organisational priorities
    theme: t-bg-primary
    copy: |
      An organisation may have large scale plans for what it wants to achieve. These plans and activities will be prioritised in terms of what the organisation is seeking to achieve, with the priorities of the **highest importance** being established.

      Senior managers in an organisation see the bigger picture. They will know the overall priorities of the organisation and are able to identify the importance of those priorities.

      Select the images below to see what **senior managers** can do:

    subInc: 
      type: reveal
      content:
        -
          title: Maximise the organisation’s ability to achieve things
          copy: |
            Work with budget managers to ensure that their budgets maximise the organisation’s ability to achieve things with the highest priority.
          img: ba_u6_s2_p3_reveal_1.svg
        -
          title: Ensure the work of different departments is complimentary
          copy: |
            Work across several budgets to ensure the work of different departments is complimentary and most likely to lead to high priority outcomes being achieved.
          img: ba_u6_s2_p3_reveal_2.svg
        -
          title: Understand what may be achievable at an operational level
          copy: |
            Develop a deeper understanding of what may or may not be achievable at an operational level. This may help amend current priorities and / or shape the business strategy priorities in the future.
          img: ba_u6_s2_p3_reveal_3.svg
    copy2: |
      Senior managers can improve the budgetary process by providing feedback on the priorities of the organisation. If the organisation aims to continue in the same way as the previous year, the budgets will be an excellent indicator of the priorities for the next year. However, priorities change and these need to influence future budgets.

    title: Case study - Bargainshop
    layout: media
    theme: t-bg-primary1a i-case-study
    copy: |
      Jorge, the training manager for Bargainshop, and his colleagues have been working on next year’s budgets.

      At the latest board meeting, it was decided that there would be **two key priorities** with implications for the training department.

      **Firstly**, the MD wants to introduce a new customer relations approach to 25% of stores with immediate effect.

      **Secondly**, it was agreed that theft from stores was a problem, so a company-wide course on spotting potential theft and dealing with it was deemed necessary.

      The training department must now revise their budgetary work as priorities have changed.

    img: /images/svg/ba_u6_s2_p3_casestudy_1.svg
  -
    title: Summary
    theme: t-bg-primary i-summary
    copy: |
      The priorities of the organisation influence the budget. The budget should maximise the organisation’s ability to achieve the things which have the **highest priority**.

    title: Financial resources
    theme: 
    copy: |
      Senior managers in an organisation will know what financial resources are available to facilitate the achievement of the business strategy.

      They will have an organisation-wide appreciation of financial resources, whereas budget managers will be focussed upon a narrower perspective. The senior managers will know where financial resources will be prioritised.

      Select the images below to see what senior managers can do.

    subInc: 
      type: reveal
      content:
        -
          title: Financial resources used efficiently
          copy: |
            Work with the budget managers to see whether financial resources are being used efficiently. It is possible that too many resources are being allocated to lower priority operations, or that higher priority operations are not getting the resources they need.
          img: ba_u6_s2_p3_reveal_4.svg
        -
          title: Financial resources are used to best effect
          copy: |
            Work across several budgets to ensure financial resources are being used to best effect.
          img: ba_u6_s2_p3_reveal_5.svg
        -
          title: Financial resources are used at an operational level
          copy: |
            Develop a deeper understanding of how financial resources are used at an operational level. This could lead to changes in current spending and / or help to shape the allocation of resources in the future.
          img: ba_u6_s2_p3_reveal_6.svg
