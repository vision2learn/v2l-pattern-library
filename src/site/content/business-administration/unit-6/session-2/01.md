    title: Preparing a standardised format
    theme: t-bg-primary
    copy: |
      To prepare a standardised format, the organisation may review the previous year’s activities and identify potential improvements. Organisations should use a form with a consistent and standardised layout, incorporating the following features:
      * Same layout
      * No artwork
      * Design
      * Typeface
      * Logical structure
      * Simple and straightforward
      * Cleary written 
      * Jargon free
