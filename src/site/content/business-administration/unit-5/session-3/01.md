    title: Problems that can occur
    layout: media
    theme: t-bg-primary
    copy: |
      ### Large and small
      
      In an ideal world it would be nice if every business event went by without any problems. However this is not the case. At times there will be problems for those people supporting events.

      The problem may be something minor that is no more than an inconvenience through to something that may threaten the whole event.

      A problem occurs when the proceedings of an event are impeded by a failure or underperformance of people, processes or equipment that result in the event failing to achieve its objectives.

      Typical problems that can occur include:
      * People problems
      * Equipment problems
      * Process problems
