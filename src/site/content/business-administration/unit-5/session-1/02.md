    title: Types of support activities
    theme: t-bg-primary
    copy: |
        ### Different tasks

        There is a wide variety of possible business events that you may need to support and help organise. These can range from a small in-house meeting for a few staff members through to large festivals or conferences for hundreds, even thousands, of people from inside and outside the organisation.

        Some of the most common support activities for business events include:
    subInc: 
      type: slider
      title: 
      slides:
        -
          title: Organising a venue
          text: The venues for business events may be on the organisation’s premises. In most cases these will have to be reserved to ensure they are available at the required times. Sometimes the venues will have to be sourced externally. These will have to be checked in terms of their facilities and their cost. The aim is to find cost-effective venues that will make it possible to deliver a high-quality event.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Getting the appropriate equipment
          text: |
            The equipment required will be determined by the size and type of event. Some typical examples include - computers and screens for PowerPoint presentations, sound systems, stages and portable toilets.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Arranging for speakers to attend
          text: For some events, such as conferences and seminars, the speakers are the central feature. Finding the required speakers, checking their availability and fees and arranging for them to attend are all important aspects of many business events.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Sorting out food and drink
          text: |
            For many events it is important that appropriate refreshments are provided. This may be something simple such as providing tea / coffee / water for a management meeting through to three-course meals for large numbers of people. Often people will have special dietary requirements that should be identified and catered for.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Preparing documentation
          text: The lifeblood of many business events will be the many forms of documentation. These will include things such as - promotional brochures, invitations, menus, conference packs, itineraries and minutes.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Publicising the event
          text: |
            Whether it is a small internal meeting or a large event with many people attending, it will be necessary to make sure that the right people know about the event. Furthermore these people should have reasonable advanced notice so they can make arrangements to attend.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Booking rooms and accommodation
          text: For some events it will be necessary to arrange accommodation. This could be for speakers or staff who could not reasonably travel to and from the event in a day.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Getting appropriate licences
          text: For some events it will be necessary to get appropriate licences or approval. Often local authorities and venue managers must be satisfied that the event will not harm attendees or unduly upset people not attending the event.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Ensuring there is appropriate insurance cover
          text: |
            Sometimes events will need specific insurance cover, other times the event will be insured under the organising party's insurance or the venue insurance. However it is important that the organisers check whether a proposed event is covered. If it is not then appropriate insurance cover must be arranged. It may also be advisable to insure against unforeseen circumstances that could undermine the whole event, such as very bad weather forcing an outdoor event to be cancelled.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
