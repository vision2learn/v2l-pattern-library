    title: Support after the event
    layout: media
    theme: t-bg-supporting2
    copy: |
      ### Was it a success?

        For many events it would be foolish to run them efficiently and to the satisfaction of those attending and then not follow things up.

        Certain tasks are needed to bring a business event to a close. Without them attendees may be dissatisfied and resources may be wasted. It is often the follow-up activities that determine whether the event is successful.

        These activities continue the relationship that has been established during the event. They carry the momentum of the event forward. Examples include ensuring the relevant people know their action points after a meeting and ensuring items bought at a trade fair are delivered as requested.

    title: Summary
    theme: t-bg-primary i-summary
    copy: |
      * The effort made to prepare and plan the event may be wasted if staff on the day fail to support it. Conversely if the support is done professionally the event can be enhanced and the chance of the event achieving the objectives is more likely.
      * It is important after an event to review it and highlight what went well and what may be improved. If this post-event activity is not done then mistakes may be repeated and opportunities to make improvements will be lost when the event, or something similar, is repeated.
