        ### Show you're a professional

        Every day people observe the behaviour of others and form theories and ideas about a person’s commitment, competence and character.

        They may experience the person face to face or experience the results of that person's actions. These judgements will be made whether staff like it or not.

        The challenge for businesses and staff is to manage these judgements effectively and be as positive as possible, at all times.

        Select the images below to see some examples of positive qualities.

    title: Case study - No Limits Adventure Holidays
    layout: media
    theme: t-bg-primary1a i-case-study
    copy: |
      ### Ensuring professionalism
      
      When preparing for the annual event the team at No Limits are aware that many of the attendees have specific and complex needs. 
      
      In response to this all staff supporting the event are given specialised training in how to ensure that all clients needs are met each member of the support team is given a detailed itinerary of the day and their various roles and responsibilities. 
      
      If questions or issues arise, they will be able to respond confidently as they will know how to resolve them, or if not, they know exactly who can.  this confidence allows them to remain calm and demonstrate a professional manner at tall times.
