    title: The impact of behaviour
    theme: t-bg-primary
    copy: |
      ### Consequences

      How you behave will affect the success of the event. You usually have two choices: to behave professionally and helpfully, or to behave unprofessionally and unhelpfully.

      Behaving in the latter manner can have a disastrous effect on an event.

      Select the images below to see some three examples:
      * Pre-event activity
      * During event activity
      * Post-event activity

    subInc: 
      type: reveal
      content:
        -
          title: Before - Sending out agendas
          copy: |
            **Consequences of unprofessional behaviour** Agendas are not sent out, meaning the event does not go ahead as planned.

            **Result of professional behaviour** Agenda goes out and event goes ahead as planned.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: During - Greeting attendees
          copy: |
            **Consequences of unprofessional behaviour** Attendees don't get greeted or may receive an unfriendly greeting. They may leave with a poor impression of the event/business.

            **Result of professional behaviour** People are greeted in a friendly and well-organised way. The chance of attendees enjoying the event is improved.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: After - Returning hired equipment
          copy: |
            **Consequences of unprofessional behaviour** Equipment is returned later, so the event organiser must pay a fine.

            **Result of professional behaviour** Equipment is returned appropriately, incurring no extra costs which makes it easier to facilitate an ongoing relationship with the supplier.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg

    subInc: 
      type: accordion
      content:
        - 
          title: Consequences
          copy: |
            The main consequences of unprofessional and unhelpful behaviour will include:
            * The event will not achieve its stated objective
            * The event will be less efficient and less effective
            * Internal and external customers may be dissatisfied
            * Resources wasted
            * People's co-operation will be more difficult to secure
            * More potential for conflict
            * More difficult to secure support for a similar event in the future
        - 
          title: Results
          copy: |
            Conversely the results of professional and helpful behaviour will include:
            * Achievement of event objectives
            * A more efficient and effective event
            * Internal and external customers will be satisfied
            * Resources are not wasted
            * People will be more co-operative
            * Less conflict
            * Greater likelihood of support for similar events in the future
