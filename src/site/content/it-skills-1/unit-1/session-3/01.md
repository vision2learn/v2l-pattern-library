      ### Text selection

      Whenever you want to format, move, copy or delete a section of text, you must first select the text so that Word knows what you are trying to do.

      [//]: # (To do this, point your mouse at the word you want to select - or the word at the beginning of a section of text, if you are formatting more than one word, - and then hold the left mouse button down while dragging the mouse over the text to select it. Release the left mouse button once you have made your selection – the selected text will now appear highlighted on the page. To de-select text you have highlighted, simply click anywhere else on the document with your mouse.)

      When you have applied different typefaces and font sizes to your document, you should check to make sure you have applied them accurately and consistently. A house style or style guide can help you to do this. 

      You can use a style guide to record all of the different formats you want to include in your document, for example the typeface and font size you will use for headings / sub headings / paragraphs, etc. You can then use this to help you check that you have applied the correct formatting techniques throughout your document.
