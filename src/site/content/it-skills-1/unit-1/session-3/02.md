To do this, simply click to highlight the text you want to move, then click and hold with your left mouse button and move to the position you want the text to appear. Release the left mouse button to drop the text into its new position.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg

            Place the cursor at the end of the text you want to delete, and then press the Backspace key to delete each letter / number. Or, click to highlight the text you want to delete and then press the Delete key.

    title: Paragraph breaks and line spacing
    layout: media
    theme: t-bg-primary
    copy: |
      You can format the layout of your document by adding paragraph breaks and also by changing the line spacing that is used.
      
      When inserting paragraph breaks, your mouse and cursor are very important. By clicking with your mouse at the end of a section of text, your cursor will be inserted here. Now, when you press the Return key, your paragraph break will be added at this point. So it’s important to get your cursor in the right position before inserting paragraph breaks into your document.

      To find out more about paragraph breaks and line spacing refer to session 2 of this Unit.

      Scroll through the slides below to learn more about some useful editing techniques that you can start applying to your documents:

    subInc:

        -
          title: Cut, copy and paste
          text: |
            You can move sections of text in your document using Cut: Cut, Copy: Copy and Paste: Paste – which can all be found in your Word toolbar.

            If you want to keep the original text in place while you move it, use Copy and Paste. If you use Cut and Paste, the text you highlight and cut will disappear from the screen until you paste it back into your document.

            While this is a nice easy way of moving text around your document, you need to be careful not to ‘cut’ the wrong piece of text, or paste into the wrong position in your document.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Drag and drop
          text: |
            Drag and drop is a handy way to make a quick alteration to your document, without having to cut and paste.

            To do this, first select a word, sentence or paragraph to highlight it. Then, click on the highlighted text, drag your mouse pointer to the appropriate area of the page and release the mouse button. The text will then reappear in the new position.

            One problem with using this technique is that it is easy to release the mouse button in the wrong place, therefore ‘dropping’ the text into the wrong position in your document.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Find and replace
          text: |
            If you make a mistake throughout a document, like forgetting to use a capital letter for the name of a company, you don’t need to alter all of these mistakes separately – Find and Replace is a much quicker option!

            To use the find and replace tool, use the shortcut CTRL + F. Click on the Replace tab, where you will see your highlighted word in the Find what: box. Now all you need to do is type your replacement word in the Replace with: box and then click on Replace All.

            [//]: # (One thing to bear in mind when using this tool is that Word will look for every instance of the word you have stated, and automatically change it for you. While this is fine in most cases, imagine that you have asked Word to change all instances of the word “sale” to “offer” – consider what would happen if you had words such as telesales, resale or sales team in your document.)
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Delete and backspace
          text: |
            To delete text from your document, you can either use the Delete key or the Backspace key on your keyboard, as follows:

            Place the cursor at the end of the text you want to delete, and then press the Backspace key to delete each letter / number.
            Click to highlight the text you want to delete and then press the Delete key.
            Be careful when using the backspace key – if you hold it down too long, you may end up deleting text that you don’t want to delete!

          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Undo / Redo
          text: |
            When you are working on a document, you can quickly and easily undo the last thing you have done (such as a word you have typed, format you have applied, etc). To do this, simply select Edit – Undo from the top menu bar. Alternatively, you can click on the Undo button: Undo in the toolbar or press Ctrl + Z on your keyboard.

            If you undo a particular action but then decide against it, you can redo it. To do this, you can click on the Redo button: Redo in the toolbar or press Ctrl + Y on your keyboard.
