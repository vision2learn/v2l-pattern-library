      As with saving your document, you have two options when it comes to printing your work.

      By default, documents are saved on the C: drive in the My Documents folder. If you want to save your work somewhere else *such as onto a CD*, you can click to display the Save In: menu to see your other options.

    title: Create a screenprint

    copy: |
      Occasionally, you may want to display the contents of a folder or window without actually printing the documents. To do this, you can take a 'picture' of the folder/window on your computer monitor - this is called a **screenprint**.

      This is a useful thing to do if you want to produce evidence to show that you have created files and folders on your computer.
    subInc: 
      type: accordion
      content:
        - 
          title: Printing window file contents on a PC
          copy: |
            Follow these instructions to print folder contents on a PC:

            1. Go to the screen you want to take a screenprint of and press the Print Screen key on your keyboard (you’ll usually find this key in the top right corner of the keyboard, next to the F12 key).
            2. Now open up a blank Word document. Go to the top menu bar and select Edit – Paste (Home – Paste in Microsoft Word 2007). Your screenprint will be pasted in to the blank file.
            3. To save your screenprint, select File – Save As (Office button – Save As in Microsoft Word 2007) from the top menu and give your document a suitable name.

          title: Printing folder contents on a Mac
          copy: |
            Follow these instructions to print folder contents on a Mac:

            1. Open the folder window you want to take a screenprint of.
            2. Hold the Shift and Command keys, and press 3 to take the whole desktop.  
            3. Or hold the Shift, Command and the number 4 keys, a crosshatch will appear, use your mouse to 'draw' around the window you wish to capture.
            3. Now open up a blank Word document. Go to the top menu bar and select Edit – Paste or Ctrl P. Your screenprint will be pasted in to the blank file.
            3. To save your screenprint, select File – Save As from the top menu (or Ctrl S) and give your document a suitable name.
