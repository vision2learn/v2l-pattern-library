            You have already learnt a lot about the Spell Check tool in your previous units. Here’s how to use Spell Check in Microsoft Excel: 
            1. Click on the Review tab
            2. In the Proofing group, click the Spelling icon
            3. The Spelling dialog box will appear. Here you can review, correct and ignore any errors that Excel has picked up.
          img: it2/it2_u3_s6_p1_slide2.svg

          title: Data sourcing and proofing
          text: |
            Once you have checked the text in your spreadsheet, the first thing you should do is proofread it and check the data source. By checking the source of your original data, you can make sure that you input the right information to start with.

            Proofreading the spreadsheet may uncover mistakes that Excel’s error checking tools may not have picked up. Proofreading can be useful to check all errors, spelling, formulas, and cell references. Also getting a second opinion, or someone to check it with you, can help you to spot more errors. 
          img: it2/it2_u3_s6_p1_slide3.svg
