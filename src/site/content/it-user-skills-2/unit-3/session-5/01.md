    copy: |
      There are many options available to you when it comes to displaying your data. Effective presentation of data helps the analysis of results. It may be best to keep some data in a simple range so that users have access to all data, or it may be better to put your data into a table that can be easily filtered. You could also display data in a pie chart or bar chart ready for analysis!

      Lists and tables
      
      Usually, your data will be presented as a data range. Your data will continue to look like this (in the form of a list) unless you format it as a table.
      
      To format your data as a table, you can use Excel’s predetermined Table Styles. These can help to make your data more readable.
