    theme: t-bg-primary
    copy: |
      There are many options available to you when it comes to displaying your data. Effective presentation of data helps the analysis of results. It may be best to keep some data in a simple range so that users have access to all data, or it may be better to put your data into a table that can be easily filtered. You could also display data in a pie chart or bar chart ready for analysis (we take a look at charts and graphs in the next session.)

    theme: 
    copy: |
      Usually, your data will be presented as a data range. Your data will continue to look like this (in the form of a list) unless you format it as a table.
      
      To format your data as a table, you can use Excel’s predetermined Table styles. These can help to make your data more readable. Scroll through the slides below to see how to format your data as a table.

    title: Page layout
    theme: t-bg-primary
    copy: |
      You can easily change the layout and appearance of your spreadsheet by editing features such as the margins, page orientation, page size and page breaks. This can help to make your spreadsheet more professional. When you are editing these options, you are adjusting the page setup for printing.
  -
  -
    title: Key point
    theme: t-bg-supporting3 i-keypoint
    copy: |
      By changing the orientation of your spreadsheet, you can change the Print Area. A landscape orientation will give you more space for a wider spreadsheet.
