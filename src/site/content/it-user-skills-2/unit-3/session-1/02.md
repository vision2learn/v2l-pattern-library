    title: Excel Templates
    layout: media
    theme: t-bg-primary
    copy: |
        *update to 2019 version*

        When you first launch Microsoft Excel, you will be presented with a blank spreadsheet that you can add data to. However, much like in Word and PowerPoint, Microsoft Excel gives you a number of predefined templates to help you structure and present your data more effectively.

        These templates can be used to help you create spreadsheets for a range of specific purposes such as expense reports, monthly budgets or to-do lists.  To use these templates: 
        1. Open a blank spreadsheet
        2. Click the File tab and then click on New in the left hand menu
        3. Click on the Sample templates button in the Home section
        4. In the Available Templates section that appears, select the template you want to use.
        5. Once you are happy with your selection, click the Create button.  Your spreadsheet will now be formatted according to the selected template.

    title: Worksheets and workbooks
    theme: 
    copy: |
      Worksheets and workbooks are tools to help you organise different spreadsheets. The starting point for any Excel document is the workbook, which initially contains three worksheets. Worksheets in Excel are made up of cells organised into columns and rows. 
      
      Using multiple worksheets within a spreadsheet helps you to store different sets of data that all relate to the same topic. For example, you may use Sheet1 to record your expenses for one financial year, and Sheet2 to record expenses for the next year and so on. Scroll through the slides below to learn more.
