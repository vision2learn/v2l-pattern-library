      To insert symbols or special characters into your Microsoft Word document:

      1. Place the cursor at the point you want to insert a character or symbol.
      2. Click the Insert tab.
      3. In the Symbols group, click on the Symbol button.
      4. Select the symbol that you want to use, or click More symbols for more options.
