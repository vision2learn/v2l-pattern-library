      To do this:

      1. Point your mouse arrow at the word you want to select, then either double-click with the left mouse button or hold the left mouse button down and drag the mouse over the word(s) to select the text.

      2. To de-select any text you have highlighted, simply click with your mouse anywhere else on the document.

          title: Subscript
          text: |
            This is text that is slightly lower than other text on the line. Subscript text is often used in formulas.

            To insert subscript text:
            1. Highlight the text you want to format.
            2. Click on the Home tab.
            3. In the Font group, click on the Subscript icon.

          img: placeholder_icon.svg

          title: Superscript
          text: |
            This is text that is slightly higher than other text on the line.

            To insert superscript text:
            1. Highlight the text you want to format.
            2. Click on the Home tab.
            3. In the Font group, click on the Superscript icon.

          title: Special characters and symbols
          text: |
            To insert symbols or special characters into your Microsoft Word document:

            1. Place the cursor at the point you want to insert a character or symbol.
            2. Click the Insert tab.
            3. In the Symbols group, click on the Symbol button.
            4. Select the symbol that you want to use, or click More symbols for more options.
