        -
          title: Inserting text
          text: |
            You can insert text into an existing document by simply placing your cursor at the point you would like the new text to appear, and beginning to type.

          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Selecting text
          text: |
            In order to edit text you first need to select it. To select a word / line / paragraph, click and drag around it. Alternatively; double-clicking on a word will select it, and triple-clicking a word will select the entire paragraph.

          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Moving text
          text: |
            You can use your mouse to move text from one part of your document to another. To do this, you need to:
            1. Click with your mouse to highlight the text you want to move.
            2. Click and hold the left mouse button down over the highlighted text.
            3. Move the cursor to the point in the document where you would like to move the text to.
            4. Release the left mouse button to ‘drop’ the text into its new position.

          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Copying and cutting text
          text: |
            To create a copy of some text, select the text and press CTRL + C, or highlight the text and right-click with your mouse, then select Copy from the menu that appears. A copy of the text will now be stored in your Clipboard until you are ready to paste it.
            
            To cut a section of text in order to use it in another place, press CTRL + X. The cut text will be stored in the Clipboard until you are ready to paste it.

          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Pasting text
          text: |
            To insert any text you have saved in your clipboard, move your cursor to the appropriate position and press CTRL + V. Alternatively, right-click where you want the text to appear and select Paste from the drop down menu. 

          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Deleting text
          text: |
            To delete text from your document, you can either:

            Place the cursor at the end of the text you want to delete, and then press the backspace key to delete each letter / number.

            OR

            Click to highlight the text you want to delete and then press the delete key.

          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Undo and Redo
          text: |
            Undo is a useful tool for when you want to undo your last action. It is particularly helpful when you accidentally delete something you need! To use Undo press CTRL + Z.
            
            Redo is the opposite of Undo. If you ‘undo’ an action and then realise that you need it after all, Redo will undo the undo action! To use Redo press CTRL + Y.

          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Find and Replace
          text: |
            To search through a document for a particular word, press CTRL + F. A box will appear where you can enter the word you want to find.

            CTRL + F also has a replace function, which you can use to automatically replace one word or phrase in a document with another. This is very important for ensuring consistency for specific words or phrases in a long document.

          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Sorting text
          text: |
            Sorting text in your document is most useful when you are working with a list of text. To sort any data that you have in a list, all you need to do is:

            1. Use your mouse to highlight the text you want to sort.
            2. Click the Sort button in the Paragraph group in the Home tab. The Sort Text dialog box will appear.
            3. In the drop down list at the top of the dialog box, Paragraphs and Text will be automatically selected. Simply choose whether you want to sort your text in ascending (A to Z) or descending (Z to A) order.
            4. Once you are happy with your selection, click OK and your selected text will be sorted according to your sort criteria.

          img: placeholder_icon.svg
