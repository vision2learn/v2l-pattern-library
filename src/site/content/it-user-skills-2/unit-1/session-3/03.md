          title: Spacing, tabs and indents
          text: |
            Making changes to Line and Paragraph spacing is easy when you use the line spacing, decrease and increase indent tools. You can also make smaller, more specific changes to Line and paragraph spacing.

            As well as setting a consistent spacing between your paragraphs, you might also need to consider what you will do with headings in your document.

            For example, you might choose to insert two paragraph breaks after main headings, and one paragraph break after sub-headings.

            Whatever you decide, you should ensure that this formatting is applied consistently throughout the document in order to make it look professional.

          img: placeholder_icon.svg

          title: Borders and shading
          text: |
            Borders and shading add visual interest to what can otherwise be a boring page of text. They help to emphasise important information by making it stand out from the rest of the text.

            Borders and shading are best used sparingly, not every document will benefit from them. A 50 page report may use borders and shading to help key information stand out, but a letter to the tax authorities probably doesn’t need them.

            You can use the shading tool in Word to apply a background colour to a page or table. Shading is often useful as part of Forms or to create an impressive visual effect in a plain Word document.
