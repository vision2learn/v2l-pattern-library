    title: Before you start a task
    theme: t-bg-primary
    copy: |
      Before you start a task, you need to find out as much information about the task as possible. By making sure you understand the context of your task, this ensures you can successfully plan and complete the task.Scroll through the slides below to learn more.

    subInc: 
      type: slider
      title: 
      slides:
        -
          title: Who are you doing the task for?
          text: |
            Before you start a task, it’s really important that you work out who you are doing the task for.

            You might be completing a task for yourself, or someone else may have asked you to do it (such as a manager at work, a colleague etc.). Knowing your audience can help you work out how much detail you need to go into when completing the task.
          img: it2/it2_u1_s1_p3_ipu_slide_1.svg
        -
          title: When does it need to be completed?
          text: |
            By knowing when a task needs to be completed, you can work out a goal that you can work to. Goals and deadlines are important because if they are missed, they can impact on the workflows of other people. 
          img: it2/it2_u1_s1_p3_ipu_slide_2.svg
        -
          title: What information is required?
          text: |
            What information do you need to include? What is the information for?

            If your task was to write a letter to clients advertising an event, you might need information like names and addresses. If you already had this information, you could plan how you are going to use it.

            If you didn’t have this information, you can think about how you could get it! Who do you need to ask?
          img: it2/it2_u1_s1_p3_ipu_slide_3.svg
        -
          title: Where will the information be used?
          text: |
            Information used as part of a task could be used on screen, printed out or sent to others via email! By knowing where your information will be used, you have a clear understanding of the context of the task.
            
            Planning a task effectively before you start it can really save time and improve the quality of your work when it is finished!
          img: it2/it2_u1_s1_p3_ipu_slide_4.svg
