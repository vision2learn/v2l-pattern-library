            You can also get a list of recent documents and view the Properties about your document.

    title: Using the Ribbon
    theme: 
    copy: |
      The tools and options that each Ribbon contains relate to the function of that Ribbon.  Each Ribbon is accessed by clicking on the relevant tab and the commands in each Ribbon are organised into groupings. For example, the **Home** Ribbon in Word has all of the formatting and editing options.

      Some Ribbon tabs are shown only when they are needed. For example, the Format tab only appears when a picture is selected.  
      
      In the bottom right corner of some Ribbon groupings, you may see a small button. You can select it to open a dialog box containing further options.

      Now, let’s take a look at the Word Home ribbon. This provides shortcuts to some of the most frequently used tasks found in the drop down menus. You do not need to use all of them at this stage but we’ll take a look at some of the most useful ones.

      Select the icons on the toolbar to find out more.

          title: Screenprint on a PC
          copy: |
            Follow these instructions to take a screenprint on a PC:

            1. When your computer screen is displaying the content you want to take a print of, press the *Print Scrn* key on your keyboard. This key is normally towards the top right corner of the keyboard. 
            2. This will create a Copy of your screen content and now all you need to do is Paste into a word document.
            3. Open up a Word document.
            4. Position your cursor at the point in the document where you would like the screenprint to appear.
            5. Right click at this point, then select Paste from the menu that appears.
            
            Once you have pasted a screenprint, you can use the Format tab to crop it into the right shape and size. You will learn more about cropping and resizing as part of this course.
            
          img: /images/svg/it2/it2_u1_s1_p3_accordion1.svg
        - 
          title: Screenprint on a Mac
          copy: |
            Follow these instructions to print folder contents on a Mac:

            1. Open the folder window or program you want to take a screenprint of.
            2. Hold the Shift and Command keys, and press the number 3 to take the whole desktop.  
            3. Or, hold the Shift and Command keys, and press and the number 4, a crosshatch symbol will appear, use your mouse to 'draw' around the window you wish to capture, let the mouse go to take the shot.
            4. Now open up a blank Word document. Go to the top menu bar and select Edit – Paste or Control V. Your screenprint will be pasted in to the blank file.
            5. To save your screenprint, select File – Save As from the top menu (or Control S) and give your document a suitable name.

    copy: |
      In many of the Microsoft Office programs, you’ll find a very useful tool to help you check your spelling and grammar.

      To check your work in a particular Office program:

      1. Open up a document that needs to be checked
      2. Click the Review tab to open up the Review Ribbon. On this Ribbon, Select either the *Editor* or *Spelling* button.
      3. Office will begin checking your document for errors and a dialog box will appear to highlight any suggested corrections.
