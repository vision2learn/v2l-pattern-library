          title: Applying borders and shading
          copy: |
            Simply highlight the table (or part of the table) that you want to format, then click on the Design tab of the menu and choose the appropriate design option.
          img: it1/it1_u1_s2_p2_reveal2.svg
        -
          title: Inserting / deleting rows
          copy: |
           Move your mouse pointer to the left of an existing row and then click to select the entire row.
           
           Once selected, right click with your mouse over the row. On the menu that appears, you’ll see two options – Insert Rows and Delete Rows. Select one of these options depending on the change you want to make to your table.

          img: it1/it1_u1_s2_p2_reveal3.svg
        -
          title: Inserting / deleting columns
          copy: |
            Move your mouse pointer to the top of an existing column (the mouse pointer will change to a black, downward pointing arrow) and then click to select the entire column, right click with your mouse over the column. On the menu that appears, you’ll see two options – Insert Columns and Delete Columns. Select one of these options depending on the change you want to make to your table. 
          img: it1/it1_u1_s2_p2_reveal4.svg
        -
          title: Adjusting column width
          copy: |
            To change a column width in your table, first click a cell in the column you want to adjust. Now select Table – Table Properties from the top menu bar, then click the Column tab and select the size you want.

            Select the column you want to adjust then click on the Page Layout tab of the menu. Select Columns in Page Setup.
          img: it1/it1_u1_s2_p2_reveal5.svg
        -
          title: Selecting rows and columns
          copy: |
            The key thing to remember when *selecting* a row or column is if you're:
            Deleting a row or column, the selected row or column will be the one that you delete. 
            Adding - the new row will be added *below* the selected one; the new column will be added *to the right* of the selected one.
