          title: Page breaks
          text: |
            When you reach the end of a page, Word automatically inserts a page break to take you to a new, blank page. If you want to insert a page break before the end of a page, you can insert a manual page break.

          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Paragraph breaks
          text: |
            To create a paragraph break in your document, simply press the Enter key twice when you reach the end of a paragraph. This will move your cursor down onto a new line and you can begin to type a new paragraph.

            By using this technique, you can space your document out so that it is easy to read and use paragraphs to group related text together.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Orphans and widows
          text: |
            Good Word documents will not contain widows or orphans!

            A widow is a page that begins with the last line of the paragraph before.

            An orphan is a page that ends with the first line of the next paragraph.

            To help you avoid these scenarios, you can use Widow and Orphan Control. 

          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Section breaks
          text: |
            You can use section breaks to change the layout or formatting of a particular page (or pages) in your document. For example, you might want one page of your document in portrait orientation and the second page in landscape orientation – a section break would allow you to do this.
