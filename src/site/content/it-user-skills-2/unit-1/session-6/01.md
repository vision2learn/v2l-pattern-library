    title: Proofreading and correcting mistakes
    theme: t-bg-primary
    copy: |
      When creating documents, it is important to make sure that your content is accurate. Your documents should always be professional, and any errors or inconsistencies will not give this impression.

      While you may take great care when entering text and numbers in your document, you should always check the accuracy of your finished document. The best way to do this is to proofread your work. Remember! Proofreading is a specialist skill. See below to learn about some of the ways that you can improve your own proofreading skills.

          title: Set enough time and use a checklist
          text: |
            Often, people don’t set enough time aside to check their work, and as a result it contains a number of mistakes. When you check a document, it’s important to set aside enough time to check every single element before it gets sent out or goes to print.

            It can be useful to use a proofreading checklist to make sure you don’t forget anything.

          title: Numerical information
          text: |
            It’s really important to double-check numerical information such as the results of equations and calculations. It is also important to double-check numerical symbols, such as decimals points. By checking these things, you can ensure that a document is as error-free as possible.
            
            The best way to check calculations in your work is to use a calculator or Excel. Making sure the numbers are correct is really important for making sure your documents are accurate and professional.
          img: it2/it2_u1_s6_p1_slide4.svg
        -
          title: Layout and style
          text: |
            The final layout and style of your document may need to conform to particular guidelines set by the organisation that you are typing the document for. This is known as a House Style. (You learned about house styles in session 3.)

            House styles are really useful when checking the layout of documents, as they give you a point of reference to remind you of the style and structure you need to follow. When producing a document for an organisation, it’s really important to check it against the House Style guidelines before you print it off.

            By following the House Style, you can help to ensure consistency in the formatting and layout of your documents.
          img: it2/it2_u1_s6_p1_slide5.svg

          title: Print preview
          text: |
            Check that your document is set at the right size and page orientation to be printed by clicking on the Page Layout tab and using the options in the Page Setup group.

            To preview your document:
            1. Click the File button.
            2. Click the Print option.
            3. Look to the right of your screen where you can preview each page of your document before you print it.
