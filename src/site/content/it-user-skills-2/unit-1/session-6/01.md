    title: Proofreading and correcting mistakes
    theme: t-bg-supporting3
    copy: |
      When creating documents, it is important to make sure that your content is accurate. Your documents should always be professional, and any errors or inconsistencies will not give this impression.

      While you may take great care when entering text and numbers in your document, you should always check the accuracy of your finished document. The best way to do this is to proofread your work.

      Remember! Proofreading is a specialist skill. See below to learn about some of the ways that you can improve your own proofreading skills.
    subInc: 
      type: accordion
      content:
        - 
          title: Print and read your work
          copy: |
            The best way to proofread your work is to print it and read through it carefully, checking for spelling, spacing, punctuation or grammatical errors. Take a look at some useful hints for reading through your work:

            * Use a blank piece of paper to cover all of the text apart from the line you’re reading. This will stop you from being distracted by other text and allow you to concentrate.
            * Try reading your document backwards! This might sound strange but it will take meaning away from the content and allow you to check the spelling of each word on its own.

          img: /images/svg/placeholder_icon.svg
        - 
          title: Check the design
          copy: |
            As well as checking the content, be sure to check the design of your document. Make sure that everything looks right on the page – from the layout to the text and images.

            If you are checking a very long document, try putting a ‘style guide’ together. This could contain words and phrases that you have used a lot and want to make sure that they appear in the same format throughout. For example, whether you use ‘website’ or ‘web site’.

    theme: t-bg-primary
    copy: |
      When you check a document, it’s important to set aside enough time to check every single element before it goes to print. Often, people don’t set enough time aside to check their work, and as a result it contains a number of mistakes when it goes to print!

          title: Checking numbers
          text: |
            t’s really important to double-check numerical information such as the results of equations and calculations. It is also important to double-check numerical symbols, such as decimals points. By checking these things, you can ensure that a document is as error-free as possible.
            
            The best way to check calculations in your work is to use a calculator or Excel. Making sure the numbers are correct is really important for making sure your documents are accurate and professional.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Checking layout
          text: |
            The final layout and style of your document may need to conform to particular guidelines set by the organisation that you are typing the document for. This is known as a House Style. (You learned about house styles in Session 3.)

            House styles are really useful when checking the layout of documents, as they give you a point of reference to remind you of the style and structure you need to follow. When producing a document for an organisation, it’s really important to check it against the House Style guidelines before you print it off.

            By following the House Style, you can help to ensure consistency in the formatting and layout of your documents.
