          text: |
            When you add a new slide, you can choose the structure and layout for that slide based on the information that it needs to contain. These pre-set slide structures provide you will a range of different ways to display your information. To create a new slide:
            1. Click on the arrow next to the New Slide button in the Slides group of the Home tab.
            2. From the menu that appears, select the slide structure that you want to use.
            3. The new slide will be inserted at the end of your presentation.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Slide order
          text: |
            Sometimes, you may want to change the order of your slides so that your information is in a more logical sequence. To change the order of your slides:
            1. In PowerPoint, open up the presentation that you want to edit.
            2. On the left hand side, you will see the Slides tab.
            3. In the Slides tab, left click on the slide you want to move and drag the slide to its new position.
            4. When you are happy with the position of the slide, release the mouse to ‘drop’ the slide into a new position.

            You will see that the chosen slide now appears in a different point in your presentation and that your slides have all been renumbered to account for this change in order.

          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Slide orientation
          text: |
            To change the orientation of the slides:
            1. Click on any of the slides in your presentation.
            2. Click on the Design tab and then click on the Slide Orientation button in the Page Setup group.
            3. From the menu that appears, select either Portrait or Landscape Orientation.
            4. All of the slides in your presentation will change to your chosen orientation.

    title: Hiding slides
    theme: t-bg-primary
    copy: |
        Sometimes, you may have information in your presentation that is sensitive or that you don’t want to display to certain groups of people. To remove this information from your presentation without deleting your slides, you can hide your slides.

        For example, you may be showing a presentation to two groups of people, one group may need to see all of the information, but the other group may only need to see parts of it. Hiding slides allows you to keep all of the information without showing it to everyone.  Scroll through the slides below to learn more.

    subInc: 
      type: slider
      title: 
      slides:
        -
          title: Hiding slides
          text: |
            1. Open up the presentation that contains the slides you want to hide.
            2. In the Slides tab on the left hand side, click on the slide that you want to hide:
            3. Click on the slideshow tab, and then click on the Hide Slide button in the Set up group.
            4. The hidden slide will remain visible in the Slides tab; however the slide number is crossed out to indicate that the slide is hidden.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Reset a hidden slide
          text: |
            1. In the Slides panel, right click on the slide that is hidden.
            2. From the menu that appears, click on the Hide Slide button.
            3. The hidden slide will now be visible again in your presentation.

          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: View a hidden slide during a slideshow
          text: |
            Sometimes, you may be half way through a slideshow and realise that the information on hidden slides is actually relevant to the group you are giving the presentation to! To view a hidden slide during a slideshow:
            1. While you are running the slideshow, right click on the screen.2. From the menu that appears, click on the Go to Slide option.
            3. A list of slides that make up your presentation will appear – hidden slides appear in this list as well.
            4. Select the slide that you want to go to.
