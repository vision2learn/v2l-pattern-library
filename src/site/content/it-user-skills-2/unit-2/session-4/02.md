            Templates are documents that are formatted so that the ‘look’ of the document is already in place. This includes elements such as colour schemes, text styles and slide structure.

            Templates are very useful as instead of spending time saving the same kind of presentation over and over again, you are presented with a ‘clean slate’ that you can enter information into.

            By default, PowerPoint applies a plain, blank theme to new presentations. However, there are a number of different themes that you can apply to your presentation.

            These themes are pre-formatted slides that include colour schemes, backgrounds, fonts and styles that change the overall look of your presentation.
          img: it2/it2_u2_s4_p2_slide2.svg
