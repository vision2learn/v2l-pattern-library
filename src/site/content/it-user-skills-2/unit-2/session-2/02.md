title: Reducing file size
intro: |
  PowerPoint presentations can quickly become very large files – especially if they contain objects like photographs, charts, videos and audio files!

  Reducing the size of the file you are using will make the presentation faster to load. This is very useful when you have a large amount of data on your computer or a limited amount of disk space.

    title: Economising on disk space
    theme: 
    copy: |
      The amount of storage that your computer has will depend on what kind it is, so saving on disk space is really important if you need to save a large number of documents.

      Sometimes you may want to archive a group of files in one file so that they can be easily backed up to a network drive or cloud storage, moved to another folder or transferred to a different computer. It is also sometimes useful to compress lots of files into one so that they use less disk space on your computer.

      To save disk space, there are a number of things you can do:

    title: Image resolution
    theme: t-bg-primary
    copy: |
      The resolution of your images will depend on how you are using your presentation. If you want to print your presentation then you will need a high quality image. However, if the presentation will only be seen on screen, then the images can be lower resolution without compromising the quality.

      You can change the resolution and size of your images before you add them to your presentation, but it is often easier to compress images within PowerPoint.

    title: Compressing images
    theme: t-bg-supporting3
    copy: |
      When you are trying to reduce the size of your presentation, one of the first things you can do is to check the size of the images that you have used within it. This is really useful for a variety of images, including photographs and logos.

      Scroll through the slides below to see instructions.

    subInc: 
      type: slider
      title: 
      slides:
        -
          title: Step 1
          text: |
            Click on the image that you want to format.

            Click on the Format tab, and then click on the Compress Pictures icon in the Adjust group.
          img: it2/it2_u2_s2_p2_slide1.svg
        -
          title: Step 2
          text: |
            The Compress Pictures dialog box will appear

            Under Compression options, you can click to select two compression options:
            * Apply only to this picture – if this box is checked then only the selected image will be compressed, if unchecked then all images in your document will be compressed
            * Delete cropped areas of pictures – if this box is checked then the areas left over from image cropping will be deleted

          img: it2/it2_u2_s2_p2_slide2.png
        -
          title: Step 3
          text: |
            Select a Target output based on whether you are going to print your presentation, view it on screen or send it via email.

            Once you are happy with your selection, click OK.

            Your image has now been compressed! This will help reduce the overall size of your presentation.
          img: it2/it2_u2_s2_p2_slide3.png
