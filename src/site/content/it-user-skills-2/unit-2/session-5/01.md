title: Animating your presentation
intro: |
    If you are creating a presentation that will be viewed on-screen as a slideshow, then you have the option to add animated features to your slides.

    The animation tools in PowerPoint give you the opportunity to decide how to animate every object in your presentation. You can also decide on the transition from one slide to another, and set timings for your slides animation.
