title: Animating your presentation
intro: |
    The animation tools in PowerPoint give you the opportunity to decide how to animate objects in your presentation. You can also decide on the transition from one slide to another, and set timings for your slides animation.  These are useful if you are creating a presentation that will be viewed on-screen as a slideshow.

            Before you begin to add effects to objects on your slides, it is useful if you view the Animation Pane so that you can see the effects you add.

        -
          title: Adding effects to slides
          text: |
            There are three different kinds of animation that you can apply to objects on your slides. You can change how your object enters the slide, how it appears on the slide and how it will leave the slide.

            If you only want to add one kind of animation to an object, such as an Entrance, then you can select the animation effect from the Animation Pane once the object is selected.

            
          img: it2/it2_u2_s5_p1_slide2.svg
        -
          title: Choosing effect options
          text: |
            Once you’ve chosen an object to animate and the effect you want to use, you can now set various options for the effect.

            Once you have added an effect to an object, you will see it appear in the Animation Pane.  You can see the effects options window by clicking on the arrow next to the animation.  On a Mac this is shown in the same pane below the animations list.

          img: it2/it2_u2_s5_p1_slide3.png
        -
          title: Removing effects from slides
          text: |
            If you are unhappy with your chosen animation, or you want to delete some of the effects that you have applied to your slides, simply right click on the effect that you want to delete in the Animation Pane and select *Remove* from the menu that appears.

            On a Mac select the animation from the list and click the red cross in the upper right corner.
          img: it2/it2_u2_s5_p1_slide4.png
        -
          title: Transitions
          text: |
            A slide transition is the way in which one slide of your presentation moves on to the next. Rather than slides just appearing one after one, you can choose a range of effects as each new slide appears. Using transitions can add a really professional feel to your presentation and can be used on every slide.
          img: it2/it2_u2_s5_p1_slide5.svg
