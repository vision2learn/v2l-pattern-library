title: Animating your presentation
intro: |
    If you are creating a presentation that will be viewed on-screen as a slideshow, then you have the option to add animated features to your slides.

    The animation tools in PowerPoint give you the opportunity to decide how to animate every object in your presentation. You can also decide on the transition from one slide to another, and set timings for your slides animation.

    title: Adding effects to slides and Slide transitions
    theme: t-bg-primary
    copy: |
      There are a number of different options that you can choose from when it comes to adding effects to slides. Scroll through the slides below to learn more.

      A slide transition is the way in which one slide of your presentation moves on to the next. Rather than slides just appearing one after one, you can choose a range of effects as each new slide appears. Using transitions can add a really professional feel to your presentation and can be used on every slide!

      Be careful when adding animation – too much animation can distract readers from the main message of your presentation and make presentations appear unprofessional.

          title: View the Animation Pane
          text: |
            Before you begin to add effects to objects on your slides, it is useful if you view the Animation Pane so that you can see the effects you add. To view the Animation Pane:
            1. Click on the slide that contains the objects you want to animate.
            2. Click on the Animation tab.
            3. In the Advanced animation group, click on the Animation Pane button
            4. The Animation Pane will appear to the right of your presentation. Once you animate objects on your slides, the animations will appear here.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Adding effects to slides
          text: |
            There are three different kinds of animation that you can apply to objects on your slides. You can change how your object enters the slide, how it appears on the slide and how it will leave the slide.  You need to add these effects in order. To do this:

            1. Select the object that you want to add an effect to.
            2. Click on the Animation tab.
            3. Click on the Add Animation button in the Advanced Animation group.
            4. From the menu that appears, select an Entrance effect for your object.
            5. You will notice that your animation now appears in the Animation Pane.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Adding effects to slides 2
          text: |
            When you are happy with your entrance animation, simply repeat steps 1 – 4 for Emphasis and Exit effects to apply these to your object.

            If want to see more animation effects to choose from, click the More button More button in the Animation group.

            If you only want to add one kind of animation to an object, such as an Entrance, then you can select the animation effect from the Animation Pane once the object is selected.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Choosing effect options
          text: |
            Once you’ve chosen an object to animate and the effect you want to use, you can now set various options for the effect.

            Once you have added an effect to an object, you will see it appear in the Animation Pane. Click the drop down arrow next to each animation and you will see the Animation Pane.
            * Start – you can select when to start the effect.
            * Effect options – this will open a dialog box that allows you to enhance your effect through extra Timing, Sound, Text Animation and Effect options.
            * Timing – here you can change how quickly or slowly you want the effect to take place.

          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Removing effects from slides
          text: |
            If you are unhappy with your chosen animation, or you want to delete some of the effects that you have applied to your slides, simply right click on the effect that you want to delete in the Animation Pane and select Remove from the menu that appears.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Adding slide transitions
          text: |
            To add a transition to a slide in Microsoft PowerPoint:
            1. In the Slides panel, select the slides that you want to apply the transition to.
            2. On the Transitions tab, in the Transition to This Slide group, click a slide transition effect.

            If want to see more animation effects to choose from, click the More button.

            You can select multiple slides by holding down CTRL and clicking on multiple slides in the Slides Panel.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Transition effects
          text: |
            When you are adding a transition to a particular slide in your presentation, you can click Effect options in the Transition to This Slide group on the Transitions tab for further effect options such as direction.

            On the Transitions tab in the Timing group you can choose how you want to move from this slide to the next – either by clicking your mouse button or you can set a time after which PowerPoint will automatically move on to the next slide for you. You can also choose to apply a sound to the transition.

            If you want to apply transition effects to all slides, just click the Apply to all slides button in the Timings group located on the Transitions tab.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
