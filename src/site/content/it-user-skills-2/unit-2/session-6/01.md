    copy: |
        Once you think that your presentation is finished, the first thing that you should do is review your aims. Two key questions that you should consider include:

        * Who is the intended audience of the presentation?
        * What is the main message you want to communicate?

          title: Checking content
          text: |
            When checking the content of your slides, you need to ask yourself three key questions:

            1. Have you included all the essential content?
            2. Does the content follow a clear structure?
            3. Does your presentation communicate information clearly?

          img: it2/it2_u2_s6_p1_slide1.svg
        -
          title: Checking design
          text: |
            When you check the design of your presentation, there are three key areas that you need to look at:

            1. Font and background colours – can you read what is written on the slide?
            2. Placement of images, graphs, tables and charts – are they clear? Do they add anything to your slideshow?
            3. The amount of information on each slide – Is there too much information? Does it appear confusing?

          img: it2/it2_u2_s6_p1_slide2.svg
        -
          title: Changing the order of slides
          text: |
            Sometimes, when you are putting together a particularly detailed presentation, you might not realise until the end that some of your slides should be arranged in a different order.

          img: it2/it2_u2_s6_p1_slide3.svg

          title: Rehearsing timings
          text: |

            It’s really important to check the timings of your slideshow so that you know that PowerPoint will move through your slides at a reasonable speed. If your presentation moves too fast or too slow then it may be less effective. To rehearse timings, you need to use the Rehearse Timings feature.

    title: Checking animation and effects
    theme: 
    copy: |
      It’s important to check your animation and slide transitions. The best way to do this is to click on the Slide Show tab and click the From Beginning button in the Start Slide Show group. You can then look through your slideshow and see if there are any transitions or animations that need to be changed.
      
      Make sure that you display the Animation pane by clicking the Animation pane button in the Advanced Animation group on the Animation tab.  Select the images below to read more.
