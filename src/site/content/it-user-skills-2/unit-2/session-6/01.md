          title: Checking content
          text: |
            When checking the content of your slides, you need to ask yourself three key questions:

            1. Have you included all the essential content?
            2. Does the content follow a clear structure?
            3. Does your presentation communicate information clearly?

          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Checking design
          text: |
            When you check the design of your presentation, there are three key areas that you need to look at:

            1. Font and background colours – can you read what is written on the slide?
            2. Placement of images, graphs, tables and charts – are they clear? Do they add anything to your slideshow?
            3. The amount of information on each slide – Is there too much information? Does it appear confusing?

          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Changing the order of slides
          text: |
            Sometimes, when you are putting together a particularly detailed presentation, you might not realise until the end that some of your slides should be arranged in a different order. To change the order of your slides:

            1. In PowerPoint, open up the presentation that you want to edit.
            2. On the left hand side, you will see the Slides tab.
            3. In the Slides tab, left click on the slide you want to move and drag the slide to its new position.
            4.  When you are happy with the position of the slide, release the mouse to ‘drop’ the slide into a new position.

            You will see that the chosen slide now appears in a different point in your presentation and that your slides have all been renumbered to account for this change in order.
          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Rehearsing timings
          text: |

            It’s really important to check the timings of your slideshow so that you know that PowerPoint will move through your slides at a reasonable speed. If your presentation moves too fast or too slow then it may be less effective. To rehearse timings, you need to use the Rehearse Timings feature.

            Once you have rehearsed your timings, the way you view your presentation may have changed to Slide Sorter view. To change back to the Normal view, simply click on the View tab and click on the Normal button in the Presentation Views group.

          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        
        -
          title: Rehearsing timings - instructions
          text: |
            1. Click on the Slide Show tab and then click on the Rehearse Timings button in the Set Up group
            2. The slideshow of your presentation will be launched and a timer will appear on screen.
            3. Start delivering your presentation as you would to a real audience. When you are ready to move on to a new slide, click the mouse anywhere on the screen – the timer will capture the length of time that the slide was on screen.
            4. Continue working through the slideshow, clicking every time you are ready to move to the next slide.
            5. When you have worked through the entire slideshow a Confirmation message will appear; if you are happy with the timings, click on Yes. If you wish to discard the timings, click on No.
