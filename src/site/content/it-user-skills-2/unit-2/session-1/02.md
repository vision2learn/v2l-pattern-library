        One of the most useful programs on your computer is Microsoft PowerPoint. You can create a wide range of presentations including reports, proposals and revision notes – you can quickly and easily make changes to these presentations without wasting any paper!

        PowerPoint presentations often incorporate images and graphic elements such as lines, borders, charts or other objects. They can also include hyperlinks that link your presentation to an external source (such as a website), or another slideshow.

        Before you get started, it is important that you are familiar with tasks such as opening Microsoft PowerPoint, starting a new document, taking screenprints and saving your work. Please see the section on [Microsoft Office]() if you need to refresh your memory.

        - 
          title: How will the presentation be used?
          copy: |
            You can use PowerPoint to create your presentation in a number of different formats. These formats include:
            * An on-screen slideshow
            * Handouts
            * Notes

            Once you know how the presentation is going to be used, you can customise your slides to suit this purpose. For example, some background colours may work well for on-screen presentations, but will not work when printed as handouts.
        - 
          title: Who is the presentation for?
          copy: |
            You must ensure that the information you include in your presentation communicates clearly and accurately with the audience. By knowing who your audience is, you can ensure that the information is presented in the most effective way.

            Your audience will influence many features of your presentation, including:
            * The language
            * The content
            * The formatting and design
            * The images
