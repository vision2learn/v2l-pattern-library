          title: How will the presentation be used?
          copy: |
            You can use PowerPoint to create your presentation in a number of different formats. These formats include:
            * An on-screen slideshow
            * Handouts
            * Notes

            Once you know how the presentation is going to be used, you can customise your slides to suit this purpose. For example, some background colours may work well for on-screen presentations, but will not work when printed as handouts.
        - 
          title: Who is the presentation for?
          copy: |
            You must ensure that the information you include in your presentation communicates clearly and accurately with the audience. By knowing who your audience is, you can ensure that the information is presented in the most effective way.

            Your audience will influence many features of your presentation, including:
            * The language
            * The content
            * The formatting and design
            * The images

            Have you been given a timescale of when the task must be finished? The time that you have available is one of the most important things you have to bear in mind throughout your work.

            For example, if you don’t have very much time to complete something, you might have to leave out some of your more detailed ideas to make sure that you can meet your deadline.

          img: placeholder_icon.svg

          title: Resources
          text: |
            Do you have the resources that you need to complete the task effectively? If not, you might have to use some of the budget to buy additional resources or gain extra help from professional experts or other staff.

            This is particularly important when completing a task using IT. For example, if you needed to create a large financial report, not having access to a computer with Excel would have a huge impact on the task and how quickly it could be completed.

          img: placeholder_icon.svg
        -
          title: Audience needs
          text: |
            Before you start any kind of IT work, you should always think about who you are producing the work for and what their needs are. Different audiences would mean totally different outcomes.

            For example, if you were preparing two café menus, one for adults and one for children, they would be very different. A menu aimed at children would be more colourful and probably have less text. A menu for adults would have more text and be formatted in a more sophisticated manner.
