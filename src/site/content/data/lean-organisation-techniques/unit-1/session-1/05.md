  There are many benefits of a lean work environment. At the heart of it all is an effort to improve the quality of service: making processes more efficient, improving employee morale and saving money.

  Of course, these benefits can be felt across a business from those at the very top to employees, customers and everyone else who comes into contact with the business.

          title: Case Study 1
          copy: |
            Michael works in the personnel department at a call centre. A large proportion of his job role is desk-based which involves working with a lot of spreadsheets and word processing files. Up until a few months ago, Michael and his team found their work was hampered due to the chaotic and confusing folder layout of the company’s computer network which hadn’t been updated for a number of years. Throughout each workday they would waste minutes at a time looking for the correct files while the state of the folder structure meant there was no motivation for anyone else to keep it in good order. After a review process, time was spent streamlining the folder system and overhauling the structure of the network. In addition, staff were trained and given guidance on how to keep folders neat and tidy. Michael and his team found that their work was more streamlined and efficient. This saved precious minutes which, over the course of weeks and months, really add up!
          img: /images/svg/leanmanagement/LM_U1_S1_p5_caseStudy_1.svg
        -
          title: Case Study 2
          copy: |
            The administration department of a large manufacturing company has prided itself on the flexible working arrangements for its employees where, as long as a set amount of hours are met per month, workers can pick their own hours. Previously, there was little communication across teams about who might be staying late and how long they would be staying for. This often meant that lights and workstations were left on overnight at great expense to the company. To remedy this, senior management introduced a rota system where one team leader every week was responsible for keeping track of when other employees would be staying late and team building days were held to encourage more cross-team communication. The changes were a success. Lights and workstations were no longer left on overnight and within three months the company had already experienced a noticeable reduction in its electricity bills.
          img: /images/svg/leanmanagement/LM_U1_S1_p5_caseStudy_2.svg
        -
          title: Case Study 3
          copy: |
            Employees were asked to complete long, drawn out process forms and deliver them by hand upstairs for them to be processed by the administration department. The process was so drawn out, that employees would often avoid doing work that would mean having to fill the forms in. By instating a much swifter, entirely electronic form for employees to fill in, the process could now be completed a lot quicker. Not only did this speed up the process, it also improved worker productivity and motivation as it removed a major barrier that was affecting their workflow.
          img: /images/svg/leanmanagement/LM_U1_S1_p5_caseStudy_3.svg
