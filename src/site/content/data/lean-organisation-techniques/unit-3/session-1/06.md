        - 
          title: Teams in the workplace
          copy: |
            **Effective teams**
            * Informal and relaxed atmosphere
            * Relevant discussion and high level participation
            * Team objective understood and commitment to achieve it
            * Team members listen to one another
            * Conflict is not avoided but dealt with in the open
            * Decisions are made through consensus
            * Ideas are shared and expressed openly
            * Leadership is not overly formal, but there is a focus on consensus
            * The team examines and monitors its own progress and behaviour
            * There is low employee turnover and absenteeism
            
            **Ineffective teams**
            * Bored and tense atmosphere
            * Limited relevant discussion and participation
            * No clear objective or commitment
            * Team members tend not to listen to one another
            * Conflicts often fester or lead to destructive interactions
            * Decisions are often not made through consensus and there is indecisiveness
            * Ideas are withheld or may be subject to open criticism
            * Leadership is focused on a formal leader
            * There is little or no examination of progress or behaviour
            * There is higher staff turnover and absenteeism
