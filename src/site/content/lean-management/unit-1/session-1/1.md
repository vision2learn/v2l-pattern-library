The three Sessions in this Unit will help you to gain an understanding of the concept of a lean environment in the workplace.

## Your learning goals

In this first Session, you will work towards the following learning goals.

You will:

  1. Understand the concept of a lean environment.

At the end of the Session you can:

  1. Describe the *principles* of lean organisation techniques.
  2. Explain the *benefits* of a lean environment.

### Printing work

As you work through your Lean Organisation Management Techniques course, it might prove useful to print some pages to keep for future reference.

  1. Go to the web page you want to print
  2. From the top menu, select **File** and then **Print**.
  3. A print box will appear, in this select the **Preferences** button.
  4. Click on the Paper tab and then click to select **Landscape**. **Once you have done this, click OK**.
  5. Once you're satisfied with your print format, select **Print** to send the page to your printer.

### Altering the font size

If you use Internet Explorer, you can change the size of the text that appears on your web page by selecting **View** and **Text size** from the top menu in your browser. You will be given the following five options: 

  * Largest
  * Larger
  * Medium
  * Smaller
  * Smallest

Simply click one of these options to select a font size.

Alternatively, you could scroll your mouse wheel while holding down the Ctrl key on your keyboard.
