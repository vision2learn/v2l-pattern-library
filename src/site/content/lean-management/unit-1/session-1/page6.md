title: Benefits of eliminating waste
---

In a workplace that is focused on continuous improvement, the main aim is to identify and eliminate waste. The idea is that by always looking for ways to improve and lower costs, improved outputs will inevitably occur.

ClickTap the slider to see how a traditional organisation compares to a lean organisation.

  * ![Ian talking to a patient, and Jane standing with arms crossed](../../images/unit01/session01/pg6-slider.svg) Traditional organisation Lean organisation
  * #### Traditional organisation – business focus

More concerned with the product or service. Traditional organisations are more interested in keeping things stable and predictable.

#### Lean organisation – business focus

Far more customer centred. Geared toward identifying potential improvements and changes that help fulfil what the customer wants and needs to a higher standard.

  * #### Traditional organisation – organisation structure

Hierarchical with a more traditional power pyramid. Bosses at the top for a more top-down form of decision making and problem solving.

#### Lean organisation – organisation structure

For lean organisations, the power structure is flatter with less emphasis on those at the top dictating choices and decisions. Flexibility is encouraged, as are individual initiatives and suggestions. 

  * #### Traditional organisation – operational activities

Usually have a set and rigid structure that has remained the same over a number of years as there are few problem solving techniques that attempt to improve how things are done or get to the bottom of why certain things aren’t working.

#### Lean organisation – operational activities

Employees participate on work activities with greater fluidity. Greater emphasis on experimentation and testing out hypotheses about improving activities.

__

In comparison to more traditional organisations, businesses that implement lean management techniques are more fluid in the way they work – providing more opportunities for people to express ideas and opinions about the way things could be improved.

As lean organisations are continually looking for ways to improve, they are less likely to grow stale and are better able to identify and iron out any problems with their processes.
