    title: Potential workplace problems
    theme: t-bg-supporting2
    copy: |
      There are many potential problems and difficulties that can influence team performance. Some of the most common problems can be divided into four categories: 

      1. Purpose
      2. Roles
      3. Relationships
      4. Processes

      Scroll through the slides below to learn more. 

    subInc: 
      type: slider
      title: 
      slides:
        -
          title: Purpose
          text: |
              The purpose of the team is expressed through goals and objectives. Problems occur when people do not understand or accept the team purpose or key objectives. 

          img: lm_u3_s2_p1_slide_1.svg
        -
          title: Roles
          text: |
              Roles provide details about what people must do to achieve team goals and objectives. Problems occur when there is confusion about what team members should expect from each other, when there are inconsistencies between team objectives and individual objectives, and when roles overlap. 

          img: lm_u3_s2_p1_slide_2.svg
        -
          title: Relationships
          text: |
              Relationships between team members can sometimes be problematic and this can negatively affect team performance. Problems occur when team members have negative feelings about each other, when there are personality clashes or when team members do not believe their needs are being met. Problems can also occur when there is a lack of communication. 

          img: lm_u3_s2_p1_slide_3.svg
        -
          title: Processes
          text: |
              If there are problems with processes, outcomes may not be delivered. Problems occur when there is confusion about who has authority to make decisions, when cross-team communications fail, when processes for conflict resolutions are substandard and when co-ordination and monitoring are not carried out. 
          img: lm_u3_s2_p1_slide_4.svg
  -
    title: Team member responsibility
    theme: t-bg-primary
    copy: |
      Each team member, including the team leader, has a **responsibility** to help the team achieve its purpose. Each individual should ask themselves whether any of their actions are undermining team performance and, if so, how they can change their actions. Whenever problems occur, the people involved should aim to **understand the facts** and focus on achieving the desired team outcomes.

    title: Effective working relationships
    theme: t-bg-supporting3
    copy: |
      When dealing with conflict, teams can help prevent problems and difficulties by creating and maintaining effective working relationships. There are many interactions in a working environment every day. Agreeing targets, liaising with other departments, meetings, setting quality standards and many more are communicated through employee interactions. When positive relationships exist in the workplace, these interactions are made easier.

      Remember, effective working relationships between team members and team leaders can be supported by the following attitudes and relationships.
