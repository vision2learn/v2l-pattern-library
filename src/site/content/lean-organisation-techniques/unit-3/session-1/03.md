    title: Team effectiveness - Clear instructions and effective communication
    theme: t-bg-supporting2
    copy: |
        In order for teams to be effective, it is very important that team members know exactly what their **goals** are and what they must to do achieve them.

        Teams can ensure that they do this by adhering to **seven key steps.**

        Scroll along the slides below to learn more about each step.
    subInc:
      type: slider
      title: 
      slides:
        -
            title: 1. Defining precise goals
            text: |
                If the team’s intended outcome is vague, it is difficult to decide what needs to be done and who needs to be doing what. A specific outcome makes the allocation of tasks easier. It may help the team to set SMART (Specific, Measurable, Agreed, Realistic and Timed) goals. 

                Once the team have precise objectives, these can be broken down into milestones. These smaller milestones will identify individual tasks and activities for team members.
  
            img: lm_u3_s1_p3_slide_4.svg
        -
            title: 2. Creating plans for the achievement of goals
            text: |
                Once the team has decided upon their SMART goals, they must make a plan to achieve them. They need to answer the following questions: 
                * What tasks need to be done?
                * When do the tasks need to be completed?
                * Who should carry out the tasks?
                * What resources are required to carry out the tasks? 
            img: lm_u3_s1_p3_slide_5.svg
        -
            title: 3. Having clear instructions for work requirements 
            text: |
                If outcomes are clear and well-planned, it is much easier to provide clear instructions about work requirements than if the outcomes are unclear. 
                
                It is also important that each team member knows what they are expected to do and when tasks should be completed. Care should be taken to ensure that team members have the skills and resources to do their tasks. 
            img: lm_u3_s1_p3_slide_6.svg
        -
            title: 4. Having clear parameters on individual authority 
            text: |
                The authority of each team member should be clear. The team member should know exactly how much freedom they have to make decisions and changes. They should also know what to do in the event that a situation goes beyond their individual authority. In these situations, it is likely that they should speak to a team leader, supervisor or manager. 
                
                If the parameters of authority are unclear, team members may make decisions that may have negative consequences.

            img: lm_u3_s1_p3_slide_7.svg
        -
            title: 5. Checking understanding
            text: It is important that team members fully understand instructions. Understanding can be enhanced if team members are actively encouraged to ask questions. It is also helpful to ask team members some questions to make sure that they understand their instructions. 

            img: lm_u3_s1_p3_slide_8.svg
        -
            title: 6. Seeking support
            text: When working within a team, it is important to recognise any problems and difficulties, then consider asking for assistance. The ability to ask for support and assistance helps to strengthen teams and enhances their effectiveness.

            img: lm_u3_s1_p3_slide_9.svg
        -
            title: 7. Defining procedures for dealing with conflict
            text: |
                Conflict can lead to problems in the workplace. Therefore, it is important to have procedures for dealing with conflict straight away. Procedures can also prevent the conflict situations becoming emotional charged and worsening.
                
                ‘Informal procedures’ should make it clear that if conflict occurs, the parties involved should seek out a person in authority as soon as possible and ask him or her to intervene. Formal procedures, such as grievance and disciplinary procedures, can also be used to deal with conflicts. 

            img: lm_u3_s1_p3_slide_10.svg

    copy2: |
        ### Effective communication

        It is also important that any communication between team members and teams is effective. Information may be communicated in various different ways depending on the scenario. It may be communicated numerically, verbally or visually. At all times, the effectiveness of communication is evaluated against the objectives it is supposed to achieve. It is also important to continuously look for ways to improve communication.
