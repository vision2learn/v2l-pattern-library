          title: Planning an observation
          copy: |
            #### Steps in the planning process
            * Define workplace environment
            * Check any health and safety requirements
            * Establish a ‘clear-out’ area
            * Consider categories for observation
            * Consider the people who will be involved
            * Gather necessary equipment
            * Decide on a timescale for the observation

            #### The 5C/5S technique
            * Seiri – Clear out and Sort
            * Seiton – Configure and Simplify
            * Seiso – Clean and Sweep
            * Seiketsu – Conformity and Standardise
            * Shitsuke – Custom and Sustain  

            #### CANDO
            * Clear up
            * Arranging
            * Neatness
            * Discipline
            * Ongoing improvement
        - 
          title: Stages 1, 2 and 3 of the 5C/5S technique
          copy: |
            #### Clear out (Sort)
            * Separating essential items from non-essential items
            * Essential items should be checked that they are safe and fully operational
            * Non-essential items should be disposed of appropriately
            * ‘Red tags’ should be attached to any abnormal conditions

            #### Configure (Straighten/Simplify)
            * Effective use of workplace, equipment and inventory
            * Minimising unnecessary reaching, walking and bending
            * Stored items should be accessible, visible, classified and labelled

            #### Clean and check (Sweep/Shine/Scrub)
            * Clean all essential items and equipment
            * Identify any abnormal conditions
            * New standard conditions are established
            * Creation of a check sheet

            * Quality critical items
            * Broken items
            * Homeless items
            * Inventory
