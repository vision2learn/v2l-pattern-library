      JMC used the process map to move towards improving productivity.

      This process map provided a helpful visual representation to improve staff understanding of the mailing process and a starting point for understanding and improving the way JMC do business.
      
    subInc: 
      type: accordion
      content:
        - 
          title: Staff questions
          copy: |
            The mapped mailing process enabled JMC staff to start asking a series of questions about the process that may result in an improved service:

            * Were mailers ready in good time and the mailing service responsible for the delays?

            * Were there any delays in the production of documents that made it difficult to get mailers out on time?

            * Were there any problems with the supply of envelopes that resulted in time delays?

            * Was the folding equipment and manual folding faulty?

            * Was the folding equipment and manual folding too slow?

            * How many documents are damaged during folding?

            * Was the stamping process too slow?

            The questions listed here are not exhaustive – they are just some of the questions JMC staff could have asked in order to improve their mailing service. The question and answer process would continue until the correct mailing process was sufficiently understood so that appropriate actions could be taken.
