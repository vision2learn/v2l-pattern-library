          title: Stakeholders
          text: A stakeholder is someone with a vested interest in an organisation. These may include managers, board members, shareholders, employees, suppliers, government and community.

        -
          title: Customer voice 
          text: As well as staff and stakeholders, customers are another fundamentally important group of people who may be involved in the PNA process. It is important for a lean organisation to ensure that the voice of the customer is given the highest priority.

          text: For example, a publically funded hospital may have some very specific targets on waiting lists, quality of treatment and budgets set by politicians (a key stakeholder).
        -
          title: Example 2
          text: A private sector organisation may have targets set by an owner (a stakeholder) on profits, market share and dividends.
