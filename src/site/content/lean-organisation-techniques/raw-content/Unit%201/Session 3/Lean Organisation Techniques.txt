## Understanding work processes

__

__

__

__

In a workplace an **action** or **activity** does not take place in isolation. Instead, there is a **sequence** of interconnected and interrelated activities that lead to an outcome. These linked activities form a **process**. 

However, it can be difficult to get an understanding of how the various activities are linked. One of the most effective ways to understand and improve workplace performance is to get a ‘picture’ or **visual representation** of what is happening.

In this Session you will gain a better understanding of processes through the use of visual representations.
