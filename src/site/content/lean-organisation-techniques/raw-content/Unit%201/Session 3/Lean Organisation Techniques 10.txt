### Actions

The creation of the flowchart showed you a clear overview of the whole process. As a result, you can question the process and identify areas where waste can be reduced and productivity can be improved.

For example, to meet the demand of popularity, the pizza counter may choose to store more ingredients and special sauce. This could be represented visually in a new flowchart by introducing more storage symbols which would add value to the process.
