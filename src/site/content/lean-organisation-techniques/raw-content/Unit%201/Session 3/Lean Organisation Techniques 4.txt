### In summary ![In summary](../../images/icons/icon-summary.svg)

__

__

__

__

This is a basic but effective example of a completed process map to serve a purpose. The process map shows the actions Rajesh took to meet the purpose of attending a conference. The **oval** shapes represented the **start** and **end** of the process and the **rectangles** showed the **activities** within the process.

By the end of this Session you will have to produce a process map for a process relating to your own work or experience; your work on this page is a great start!
