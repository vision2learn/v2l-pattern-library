In this Session, you will learn about **problems and difficulties within teams. **

### Your learning goals

__

__

__

__

In this Session, you will work towards the following **learning goals.**

You will:

  1. Be able to work effectively in a team. 

At the end of the Session you can: 

  1. Know how to deal with any conflict or difficult situations as a team member. 
  2. Know how to deal with any conflict or difficult situations as a team leader. 
  3. Review the team’s overall effectiveness and own contribution to achieving goals. 

### Your Assessment

The information and exercises in this Session will help you to complete **Section 2 of your Unit 3 Assessment.**

Teams often face problems or difficult situations that they must overcome as a collective. In order to do this, team members and team leaders must know how to work together and work effectively so they can deal with any problems and achieve their goals.
