# Potential workplace problems

Pages:

  * __

There are many potential problems and difficulties that can influence team performance. Some of the most common problems can be divided into four categories: 

  1. Purpose
  2. Roles
  3. Relationships
  4. Processes

TapClick each tile in the grid to learn more. 

Reveal all / Close all

#### Purpose

The purpose of the team is expressed through goals and objectives. Problems occur when people do not understand or accept the team purpose or key objectives. 

![Purpose](../../images/unit03/session02/pg2-graphic1.svg)

#### Roles

Roles provide details about what people must do to achieve team goals and objectives. Problems occur when there is confusion about what team members should expect from each other, when there are inconsistencies between team objectives and individual objectives, and when roles overlap. 

![Roles](../../images/unit03/session02/pg2-graphic2.svg)

#### Relationships 

Relationships between team members can sometimes be problematic and this can negatively affect team performance. Problems occur when team members have negative feelings about each other, when there are personality clashes or when team members do not believe their needs are being met. Problems can also occur when there is a lack of communication. 

![Relationships](../../images/unit03/session02/pg2-graphic3.svg)

#### Processes 

If there are problems with processes, outcomes may not be delivered. Problems occur when there is confusion about who has authority to make decisions, when cross-team communications fail, when processes for conflict resolutions are substandard and when co-ordination and monitoring are not carried out. 

![Processes](../../images/unit03/session02/pg2-graphic4.svg)

* * *

Each team member, including the team leader, has a **responsibility** to help the team achieve its purpose. Each individual should ask themselves whether any of their actions are undermining team performance and, if so, how they can change their actions. Whenever problems occur, the people involved should aim to **understand the facts** and focus on achieving the desired team outcomes. A key question to ask is:

![What changes will enable us to achieve our desired outcomes?](../../images/unit03/session02/pg2-graphic5.svg)

"What changes will enable us to achieve our desired outcomes?" 

  


Both team members and leaders share the responsibility of helping the team achieve its purpose. Each individual should ask themselves:

![Are any of my actions or behaviours undermining team performance? ](../../images/unit03/session02/pg2-graphic6.svg)

"Are any of my actions or behaviours undermining team performance?" 

"How can I change them? " 

  


Over the next few pages we will look at how team members and team leaders can deal with the issues outlined above.
