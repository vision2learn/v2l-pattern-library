# Effective working relationships

Pages:

  * __
  * __

When dealing with conflict, teams can help prevent problems and difficulties by creating and maintaining effective working relationships. There are many interactions in a working environment every day. Agreeing targets, liaising with other departments, meetings, setting quality standards and many more are communicated through employee interactions. When positive relationships exist in the workplace, these interactions are made easier.

Remember, effective working relationships between team members and team leaders can be supported by the following attitudes and relationships.

Reveal all / Close all

#### Shared knowledge

The whole team must understand the aims of the team and the contributions that everyone must make.

![Shared knowledge](../../images/unit03/session02/pg3-graphic1.svg)

#### Mutual respect

All team members should treat one another with respect, especially when there are differences of opinion.

![Mutual respect](../../images/unit03/session02/pg3-graphic2.svg)

#### Trust

Team mates should not have to worry about lies, deception or manipulation. They should be confident that fellow workers will be reliable, dependable and keep their word. 

![Trust](../../images/unit03/session02/pg3-graphic3.svg)

#### Openness

A free flow of information within and between teams is very important. 

![Openness](../../images/unit03/session02/pg3-graphic4.svg)

#### Co-operation

Individuals should cooperate with colleagues by encouraging them, supporting them, remaining calm, not being overly judgemental, being good humoured and generally being helpful. 

![Co-operation](../../images/unit03/session02/pg3-graphic5.svg)

#### Evidence 

When issues or problems arise it is often helpful to provide proof or signs of the issue. For example the number of sales over a period of time or emails from colleagues.

![Evidence](../../images/unit03/session02/pg3-graphic6.svg)

#### Team objectives

If everyone on the team is focused on and working towards the same team objective this will have a positive effect on the team relationship, efficiency and production. 

![Team objectives](../../images/unit03/session02/pg3-graphic7.svg)

#### Listening

It is vitally important to listen to both team leaders and team members. Good listening skills are vital for efficient team working.

![Listening](../../images/unit03/session02/pg3-graphic8.svg)

#### Communication

Constructive feedback should not involve personal feelings or judgements. Instead, it should be based on real information, evidence and/or observations.

![Communication](../../images/unit03/session02/pg3-graphic9.svg)
