# Team roles and the Belbin approach

Now you know about the roles, processes, purposes and relationships and how to resolve issues relating to them, let’s look at the different **roles** that team members need to fulfil in order for teams to be productive.

In addition to team leaders and team members, individuals may carry out different roles within their team. In [Session 1](../../unit03/session01/pg7.asp) you were briefly introduced to TMS and Belbin roles. To see the positive and negative characteristics associated with each of the nine Belbin roles tapclick along the slider below.

#### The Belbin approach 

There are nine team roles within the Belbin approach. TapClick on each role to learn about the positive and negative characteristics of the team member that may carry out the role.

  * ![](../../images/unit03/session02/pg8-graphic1.svg) Plant
  * #### Positive characteristics 

The plant will be creative, imaginative, unorthodox and a problem-solver.

#### Negative characteristics

They may ignore details and may fail to communicate effectively.

  * ![](../../images/unit03/session02/pg8-graphic2.svg) Resource investigator 
  * #### Positive characteristics

The resource investigator will be an extrovert, enthusiastic, communicative and will explore opportunities and develop contacts.

#### Negative characteristics

They may be over optimistic and can lose interest after early enthusiasm passes. 

  * ![](../../images/unit03/session02/pg8-graphic3.svg) Coordinator 
  * #### Positive characteristics

The coordinator will be mature and confident, a good chairperson and goal clarifier, and will promote decision making. They will delegate. 

#### Negative characteristics

They may delegate personal work and can appear manipulative. 

  * ![](../../images/unit03/session02/pg8-graphic4.svg) Shaper 
  * #### Positive characteristics

The shaper will be challenging and dynamic. They will thrive on pressure and will overcome obstacles. 

#### Negative characteristics

They can be seen as provocative and may hurt feelings of others. 

  * ![](../../images/unit03/session02/pg8-graphic5.svg) Monitor evaluator 
  * #### Positive characteristics

The monitor evaluator will be strategic and discerning. They will see options and judge accurately.

#### Negative characteristics

They may lack observable drive and be overly critical. 

  * ![](../../images/unit03/session02/pg8-graphic6.svg) Team worker 
  * #### Positive characteristics

The team worker will be cooperative, perceptive, calm and diplomatic. They will be a listener, builder and avoider of friction. 

#### Negative characteristics

They may be prone to indecisiveness and may be easily influenced. 

  * ![](../../images/unit03/session02/pg8-graphic7.svg) Implementer
  * #### Positive characteristics

The implementer will be conservative, disciplined, efficient and reliable. They will turn ideas into practical actions. 

#### Negative characteristics

They may be inflexible and may loathe to respond to new options. 

  * ![](../../images/unit03/session02/pg8-graphic8.svg) Completer
  * #### Positive characteristics

The completer will be detailed, conscientious and painstaking. They will search out errors and deliver on time.

#### Negative characteristics

They may worry, dislike delegating and may be a ‘nit-picker’.

  * ![](../../images/unit03/session02/pg8-graphic9.svg) Specialist
  * #### Positive characteristics

The specialist may be single minded, dedicated, knowledgeable and skilled in a particular area of skill shortage. 

#### Negative characteristics

They may have a narrow focus on specialism and loiter on technicalities. They may not see the ‘big picture’.
