# Benefits of team working

Pages:

  * __
  * __

Many organisations use teams as a means of delivering desired outputs. In **lean organisations**, teams are often tasked with the **elimination of waste.**

There are many potential benefits that team working can bring to organisations and their customers. 

TapClick to see some of the most commonly found benefits of teamwork.

  * ![](../../images/unit03/session01/pg9-graphic1.svg)

#### Increased efficiency

Team collaboration allows each employee to focus on their own specialist skills. Any team that takes the time to establish working patterns and perseveres is likely to achieve increased **efficiency** and **productivity**. 

  * ![](../../images/unit03/session01/pg9-graphic2.svg)

#### Improved performance 

When employees focus on what they do best and don’t have to deal with tasks that are beyond their skills or capabilities, they produce higher quality work.

  * ![](../../images/unit03/session01/pg9-graphic3.svg)

#### Increased competency 

While collaboration enables team members to focus on their core competencies, it can also help to increase competences. By collaborating with others, team members learn more about what they do, and gain a broader understanding of the system they work within.

  * ![](../../images/unit03/session01/pg9-graphic4.svg)

#### Trust is built 

Strong teams cultivate a foundation of trust. Trust maintains open lines of communication between all team members which encourages people to share information. 

  * ![](../../images/unit03/session01/pg9-graphic5.svg)

#### Reduced stress

When employees try to do too many tasks, it can negatively affect their productivity and health. In a team, employees can support each other, reducing the likelihood of burnout. 

  * ![](../../images/unit03/session01/pg9-graphic6.svg)

#### It enables management focus on the big picture

An organization may struggle to be successful if its leaders are forced to spend hours dealing with task or process level administrative or operational matters. Having capable teams to handle these tasks frees up senior managers to guide the medium and long term strategy.

  * ![](../../images/unit03/session01/pg9-graphic7.svg)

#### It leads to innovation 

Most innovation comes as the result of the effort of a group of people, rather than an individual. Most lasting innovations start with the creative process, which then moves through development, refinement, execution, and revision phases. Innovation usually involves input from many employees. The different experiences and perspectives of different teams can be utilised. 

  * ![](../../images/unit03/session01/pg9-graphic8.svg)

#### Improved service

In any successful organisation, there tends to be a strong **team spirit **that is appreciated by its internal customers, external customers and supporters. Positive team working leads to positive attitude and a strong sense of commitment to the organisation. 

  * ![](../../images/unit03/session01/pg9-graphic9.svg)

#### It brings mutual support

When teams work well together they are also supportive of one another. Mutual support can encourage people to achieve goals they may not have realised they could reach on their own. Furthermore, teams offer an environment that provides a sense of belonging. 

  * ![](../../images/unit03/session01/pg9-graphic10.svg)

#### It brings job satisfaction

The advantages of teamwork may lead to improved organisational efficiency and customer satisfaction. It also contributes to employees’ job satisfaction, through learning, reduced stress, more support and better use of skills. When employees are more satisfied, they tend to be motivated, less prone to absenteeism and more productive.

### Case study ![case study](../../images/icons/icon-case-study.svg)

Jennifer works for an advertising agency. The creative department of the agency is made up of teams of people who work together to fulfil projects. Jennifer is skilled in developing others’ ideas, and feels that she can focus on this particular skill because she is part of a team. She also finds that the agency’s work is innovative and of high quality because it is produced by teams. She enjoys her job because her team members are supportive of her work.
