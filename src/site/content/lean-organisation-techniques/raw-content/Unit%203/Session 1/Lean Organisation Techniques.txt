The three Sessions in this Unit will help you to gain an understanding of **working in business teams**.

### Your learning goals

__

__

__

__

In this first Session of Unit 3, you will work towards the following **learning goals**.

You will:

  1. Understand what makes an effective team.

At the end of the Session you will be able to:

  1. Describe the characteristics of an effective team.
  2. Describe the different types of team structure, giving examples.
  3. Explain the benefits of team working.

### Your Assessment

The information and exercises in this Session will help you to complete **Section 1 of your Unit 3 Assessment**.
