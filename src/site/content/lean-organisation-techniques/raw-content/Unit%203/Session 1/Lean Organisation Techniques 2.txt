# Teams in the workplace

Pages:

  * __

Organisations are made up of teams of employees who work together. Teams are often more effective at completing tasks than individuals, particularly when the tasks are complex or require creative thinking. 

Before beginning to learn about teams and the factors that make them effective, it is important to make a distinction between groups and teams.

ClickTap each image to learn more.

![oneshow](../../images/unit03/session01/pg2-graphic1.svg) ![twoshow](../../images/unit03/session01/pg2-graphic2.svg)

#### Groups

A group is a number of persons or things that are considered to be related in some way.

A group has:

  * Two or more people
  * Interaction between those people
  * Psychological awareness among group members
  * A perception of itself as a group. 

#### Teams

A team is a group of individuals who work together to achieve a common goal or task.

A team has:

  * Members who work together
  * A degree of ‘loyalty’ among members
  * An interdependence working towards goals
  * Goals and objectives it works towards 
  * A perception of itself as a team.

### Case study ![case study](../../images/icons/icon-case-study.svg)

James owns a shop that builds and sells computers. When he originally opened the shop, he was the only employee and had to carry out all the tasks himself. Often customers would be left waiting in the shop when wanting to buy something and he would often miss deadlines for computer repairs. He now has three employees who all chip in with different aspects of the business. By working in a team the shop is able to deal with more customers with even greater efficiency. James has also started offering computer workshops in the community after a suggestion by one of his employees.
