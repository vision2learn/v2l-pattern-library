# Effective working relationships

  * __

If teams are to be effective, it is essential to create and maintain **effective working relationships**. This will help the organisation flourish. 

There are huge numbers of interactions in a working environment every day. The success or failure of these interactions has a significant impact on an organisation’s ability to use **business improvement techniques**. 

Interactions can include anything from agreeing on targets, liaising with other departments, meetings and much more.

ClickTap each image below to learn more about the basics of effective working relationships and effective team working.

![Icons](../../images/unit03/session01/pg4-graphic4.svg) ![Icons](../../images/unit03/session01/pg4-graphic5.svg)

#### Basics of effective working relationships

Effective working relationships are based on: 

  * Shared knowledge and mutual understanding
  * Mutual respect
  * Complementary attitudes/values
  * Trust
  * Openness
  * Cooperative behaviours.

#### Basics of effective team working

Effective team working is based on: 

  * Common goals
  * Shared language
  * An agreed action plan
  * Support and trust
  * Good communication.

* * *

Let’s look at how some important organisational activities are affected by good and bad working relationships. TapClick the slider to learn more. 

  * ![Achieving team objectives ](../../images/unit03/session01/pg4-graphic1.svg) Achieving team objectives 
  * #### Impact of good working relationships

There will be clear, agreed objectives and prompt agreements. 

#### Impact of bad working relationships

Team members’ negative attitudes can lead to vague goals, little commitment to goals and dissent. 

  * ![Satisfying customers](../../images/unit03/session01/pg4-graphic2.svg) Satisfying customers
  * #### Impact of good working relationships

High quality information leads to improved products and higher levels of customer satisfaction. 

#### Impact of bad working relationships

An unhappy customer may refuse to explain why a product has been rejected, leading to the continued production of substandard products and loss of customers. 

  * ![Adding value to business](../../images/unit03/session01/pg4-graphic3.svg) Adding value to business
  * #### Impact of good working relationships

Openness and sharing of improvement information and internal co-operation (not competition). 

#### Impact of bad working relationships

Leads to withholding improvement information, preventing improvement.

When there are good working relationships within an organisation there is more likely to be camaraderie between groups of employees meaning employees work well with each other and are more likely to pull together when problems arise.

Furthermore, employees are more likely to be motivated and have greater job satisfaction which can only have a positive impact on an organisation’s effectiveness.
