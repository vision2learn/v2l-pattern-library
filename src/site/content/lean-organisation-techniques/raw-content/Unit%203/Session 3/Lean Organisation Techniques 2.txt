There are many models which seek to show the managerial tasks required of all team leaders. They often operate as a cycle where each task creates the foundation for work on the next task. If the work completed on the early tasks is low quality then later tasks are likely to suffer.

It is important that team leaders display competence in these important areas. A team leader who does not have these competences may cause the team to fail to achieve its objectives.

### 1\. Setting objectives

The team leader must ensure that the team objectives are clearly specified and accepted by senior managers and team members. Once this has been done plans can be made to achieve the objectives.

### 2\. Planning

The objectives specify where the team wants to go and the plans are the means of getting there. Everyone in the team needs to know their role and the activities to be completed if the objectives are to be achieved.

### 4\. Motivating

If the team is to succeed the team members must be motivated to do their tasks efficiently and effectively. A team leader must find ways to ensure team members are motivated. Later in this session motivation will be explored in greater depth.

### 5\. Communicating

A team leader needs to communicate regularly and effectively with team members and people outside the team. This includes consulting with people, listening carefully and getting information to those who need it.

### 7\. Evaluating

A process of evaluation will include comparing actual team performance with team objectives and individual team member performance with individual objectives / targets.

### 3\. Organising and coordinating

The team leader must ensure the activities are organised in a way to ensure the team operates smoothly. The activities of several team members needs to be coordinated to ensure things are done efficiently. Any problems must be dealt with promptly. 

The team leader will also coordinate activities with other teams and parts of the organisation.

### 6\. Monitoring and controlling

In a team the objectives may be clear, the plans prepared and staff may be organised and coordinated. Team members may be motivated and well informed. However, even at this point the team leader’s job is still not done. As work is carried out it needs to be monitored to ensure that things are progressing as planned.

A team leader should have control systems that identify any underperformance and enable corrective action to be taken.

## Functions of team leaders

One of the most influential experts on leadership is Professor John Adair who wrote _Effective Leadership: How to Be a Successful Leader_ (Pan Macmillan, 2009). He identifies eight functions of leadership. Adair has studied leaders in all walks of life and at different levels in organisations and institutions. He believes that the functions are integral to high quality leadership.
