### Your Assessment

The information and exercises in this Session will help you to complete **Section 2 of your Unit 2 Assessment.**

A **workplace** may exist in many potential environments, such as factories, garages, warehouses and offices. 

In each case, people, machinery, materials, processes and energy are combined to create **outputs** – usually in the form of products and services. Outputs are created for internal customers (colleagues) or external customers. Therefore, a work environment can be described as a place where work activity, which aims to create outputs for customers, takes place. 

Any number of people can work in a work environment, from one to over a hundred.
