# Workplace organisation 

Pages:

  * __
  * __

It is important that any workplace is organised. When a workplace is clean, orderly and logically arranged, it lends itself to a good working atmosphere. People are able to get the job done, and the whole place is safer, healthier and more efficient. 

However, this is not always the case. 

ClickTap to see some examples of problems that can make a workplace disorganised. 

![Clutter](../../images/unit02/session02/pg2-clutter.svg) ![Defective machinery](../../images/unit02/session02/pg2-defectivemachinery.svg) ![Absent equipment ](../../images/unit02/session02/pg2-absentequipment.svg) ![Poor quality](../../images/unit02/session02/pg2-poorquality.svg) ![•	People working chaotically](../../images/unit02/session02/pg2-people.svg)

The consequences of a disorganised workplace can undermine the efficiency of the organisation.

Some consequences of a disorganised workplace include: 

  * An unsafe environment
  * Unpleasant working conditions
  * Poor maintenance and more frequent breakdowns
  * Lost time
  * Lower output
  * Higher wastage
  * Poorer quality outputs.
