# Reduced costs, problem elimination and health and safety

Pages:

  * __
  * __

In addition to improving reliability and eliminating waste, standardisation is also used to **reduce costs**, **eliminate problems** and aid in **health and safety**

The process of standardisation can make a significant contribution to **quality**, **efficiency** and **safety** in the workplace. It may be used in all types of organisation and for all types of business functions.

#### Reduced costs

One aim of standardisation is to **reduce costs**. In a manufacturing environment, one way to reduce costs is to use the same component for several models. This creates demand for a larger volume of the component and makes it possible to get economies of scale and volume discounts. It also makes it possible to carry lower overall inventory (over the full range of models). 

#### Problem elimination

In both product and service industries, standard procedures can help to eliminate the likelihood of problems recurring, and provide the basis for a consistently delivered process. A standard procedure makes it possible to track the process and identify anything that may cause undesirable outcomes. 

#### Health and safety

Most organisations have clear instructions procedures for the operation of equipment and handling of potentially hazardous substances. If instructions are ignored, people’s health and safety can be put at risk. There are legal, moral and commercial reasons for maximising health and safety. It is logical to reduce variation and standardise health and safety processes. For example, organisations may emphasise the need for appropriate protective clothing for carrying out certain tasks. 

* * *

#### Stats and facts ![](../../images/icons/icon-stats.svg)

There is a tension between standardisation and **customisation** Whilst customers want to have the benefits of standardisation, they often like to have things that express their individualism. Often, organisations seek to standardise as much of the production process as possible and then make provision for some customisation at the end. 

#### Assessment key point ![](../../images/icons/icon-assessment-flag.svg)

As part of your Assessment, you must be able to (4.1) describe the benefits of standard working methods. Remember that some of these benefits include improved reliability, the elimination of waste, the improved quality of outputs, reduced costs, the elimination of problems and improved health and safety procedures.
