### Your learning goals

__

__

__

__

In this Session, you will work towards the following **learning goals**. 

You will:

  1. Know ways to eliminate variance from processes in the workplace. 

  1. Describe the benefits of standard working methods. 
  2. Prepare a standardised work document suggesting how to eliminate variance to process. 

### Your Assessment

The information and exercises in this Session will help you to complete **Section 4 of your Unit 2 Assessment.**

One of the most significant influences in business performance is standardisation. However, it is something that is rarely recognised. Organisations strive to achieve consistency and predictability and to eliminate variance. In order to do so, they must establish greater standardisation.

In this Session, you will be introduced to **standardisation** and **standard operating procedure.**
