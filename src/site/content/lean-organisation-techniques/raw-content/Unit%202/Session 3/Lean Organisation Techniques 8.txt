Unit 2: Business improvement tools and techniques

# Preparing and implementing VMTs

Pages:

  * __
  * __

When an organisation is committed to visual management techniques they will tend to be company-wide initiatives. Within this context, specific teams will participate in the visual management techniques in their work area. Usually, a designated team member will be nominated to prepare and oversee the introduction of the new visual management activity.

To begin with, they’re likely to consider the following five factors:

  * What information needs to be displayed
  * Who will make use of the information
  * How often it will need to be updated
  * The method of updating data
  * How the information will be displayed.

* * *

#### Case study 1

A team leader in a call centre is carrying out a review of the visual management techniques in her department. She has discovered that inbound call handlers often struggle to forward calls on to the correct departments as extension numbers change all the time due to the business expanding. She decides that there should be a visual display of the correct extension numbers and who they connect to in the business. As the information is changing so often, she decides that the information should be displayed on a wipe clean whiteboard that is updated every week. To ensure that the extension codes are accurate, the team leader nominates an employee who is in at the start of the week to be responsible for keeping the information up-to-date.

![Case study 1](../../images/unit02/session03/pg8-graphic1.svg)

#### Case study 2

A local football club has decided to reform how its finances work. Currently, any money brought in on a match day is pooled together and counted in total. With the new system each part of the club, from the kiosk to the bar and club shop, will submit its intake which will be counted separately. There will then be running totals for each section of the club along with an overall total. For this job to run smoothly, the cash office must have clearly defined compartments for the money from each section of the club so that the staff who collect and count the money can keep track of it. The club secretary gets laminated signs made up for use in the cash office as the different parts of the club are unlikely to change any time soon. To ensure that the system is still working, there will be a review at the end of every season.
