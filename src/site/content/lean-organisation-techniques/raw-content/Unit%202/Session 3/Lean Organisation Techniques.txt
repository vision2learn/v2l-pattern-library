### Your learning goals

__

__

__

In this Session, you will work towards the following learning goals. 

You will:

  1. Be able to use visual indicators to improve the work environment.

At the end of the Session you can:

  1. Identify elements that contribute to good visual management in the workplace
  2. Prepare appropriate visual indicators

### Your Assessment

The information and exercises in this Session will help you to complete **Section 3 of your Unit 2 Assessment.**

![Visual indicators](../../images/unit02/session03/pg1-graphic1.svg)

In our day-to-day lives, we are constantly picking up visual information. It could be anything from a person’s smile to the colour of traffic lights. We can see visual information and immediately know what it means.

Visual indicators help to express complex information quickly and clearly in the work environment. The images show some examples of visual indicators that are commonly found in a workplace and in our everyday lives. 

In this Session, you will develop an understanding of what is meant by visual management and what benefits can be gained by having visual management systems in place. You will also discover how the principles of visual management can be applied in the workplace.
