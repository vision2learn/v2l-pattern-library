# LEAN ORGANISATION TECHNIQUES

Unit 2: Business improvement tools and techniques   
Session 3: Use visual indicators to improve the work environment 

# Making further improvements 

Pages:

  * __
  * __

Many businesses make the mistake of introducing new visual management techniques and then stopping there. They think that merely introducing new visual indicators is enough. However, those committed to continuous improvement should constantly be looking for ways to improve what’s already there.

### How can we make the system more user-friendly?

Making sure that those who use and rely on the visual indicator can understand it is of the utmost importance. VMTs are all about making the job easier and more efficient for employees. If they’re difficult to understand or unclear they can actually be counter-productive and work as a hindrance.

![How can we make the system more user-friendly?](../../images/unit02/session03/pg12-graphic1.svg)

When reviewing and looking for ways to improve, it is a good idea to include employees in this process. Get their opinions and feedback on how well (or poorly!) the current system works. 

### How can we make the system more customer-friendly?

Businesses that have VMTs which are visible to customers must also consider how customer-friendly their visual indicators are when looking to make improvements. The focus of any business should be improving customer service – the customer here is anyone who makes use of the service provided by a business.

![How can we make the system more customer-friendly?](../../images/unit02/session03/pg12-graphic2.svg)

Being customer-friendly also means ensuring that the visual indicators in place also have a beneficial effect on customer experience. If VMTs are having a negative impact on employee performance then they’ll affect customer experience as a consequence and so should be improved.

### How can the data be simplified?

Simplifying data is all about making visual indicators more straightforward and easier to understand. The simpler the data, the more likely employees and customers are to understand it at a glance. For example, recycling bins that have different compartments for different types of waste usually have an image and consistent colour coding so that people know exactly, at a glance, what refuse should go in which bin. 

![How can the data be simplified?](../../images/unit02/session03/pg12-graphic3.svg)

Of course, data like manufacturing statistics and numbers can only be simplified so far, but visual indicators like signs and stock levels can always be improved.

### In summary ![In summary ](../../images/icons/icon-summary.svg)

__

__

__

__

As you’ve already learned, lean businesses should always be looking for opportunities to improve. Forward-thinking visual management techniques can show a business’ commitment to continuous improvement. However, allowing visual indicators to stagnate or persevering with visual indicators that don’t work can actually give the opposite impression.

For truly effective visual indicators in the workplace, businesses should review indicators currently in place in a timely fashion and always be looking to improve them where necessary.
