# Quality, cost and delivery (QCD)

Pages:

  * __
  * __

**Continuous improvement** has an impact on three broad categories. These are **quality, cost** and **delivery**. 

These three categories are usually abbreviated to **QCD**.

### The seven QCD measures

The **Department of Trade and Industry** has developed a pack of seven measures of competitiveness related to QCD (quality, cost and delivery). Click the buttons below to find out what they are. 

NRFT 

DSA 

PP 

ST 

OEE 

VAPP 

FSU 

### NRFT

Not right first time (NRFT) _defects per million_

### DSA

Delivery schedule achievement (DSA) _percentage on time_

### PP

People productivity (PP) _output per operator hour_

### OEE

Overall equipment effectiveness (OEE) _equipment specific measures_

### ST

Stock turns (ST) _ratio of total inventory to sales_

### VAPP

Value added per person (VAPP) _output value minus input value_

### FSU

Floor space utilisation (FSU) _sales per square metre_
