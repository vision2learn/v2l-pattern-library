# Continuous improvement

Pages:

  * __
  * __

In this Session, you will acquire the essential knowledge and understanding of the principles and benefits of **continuous improvement deployment**. 

You will discover why **continuous improvement** is so important to a working environment. You will also learn about the different types of improvement that are often made in the workplace.

### Case study ![Case study](../../images/icons/icon-case-study.svg)

Consider two companies called Company A and Company B. They produce very similar products of comparable quality and price.

Company A consistently delivers its products year after year.

Company B seeks to continuously improve its production and delivery. As a result of this, the products improve by 5% each year and their prices come down by 5% each year.

If all other things are equal, what are the consequences for Company A and Company B? ClickTap ‘consequences’ to learn more. 

#### Consequences

It is most likely that more and more customers will go to Company B and fewer to Company A. This is because Company A’s product remains unchanged, whilst Company B’s product gets continually better and cheaper.

The consequences of these differences are not only experienced by customers. They will also have an impact on security and future employment prospects. An expanding company is better placed to offer promotion opportunities, higher earning potential, better pensions and other similar benefits. As organisations grow, they provide the basis for a stronger and more competitive national economy.

There are many ways that Company B may have achieved its improved performance. Two common methods are through **innovation** and **continuous improvement**.

### Stats and facts ![Stats and facts](../../images/icons/icon-stats.svg)

Toyota, Motorola and FedEx are all examples of companies which use continuous improvement techniques to improve their products and services.
