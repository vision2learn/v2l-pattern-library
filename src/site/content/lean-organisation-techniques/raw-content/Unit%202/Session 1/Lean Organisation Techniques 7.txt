# New thinking approaches

Pages:

  * __
  * __

Traditionally, organisations are hierarchical – all important ideas and changes come down from those who have power and authority to those who have none. 

The Kaizen approach is a new thinking approach. It challenges many of the traditional and common ideas about how organisations operate. 

  * #### Traditional thinking organisations

In traditional thinking organisations there is a flow of information from one department to another. There is a limited flow of information between customers and suppliers.

#### New thinking organisations 

A new thinking organisation may group work activities together into processes, and link processes into systems. They are committed to the Kaizen principles and continually seek to improve their processes.

* * *

TapClick each heading to find out more about the different ways information may flow within an organisation.

#### Information and bottom-up flow

![Bottom-up flow](../../images/unit02/session01/pg7-diagram1.svg)

As you can see, the information flows up and down (vertically) through the organisation. It is not simply a top-down flow. There are large quantities of important information at activities and processes level. Senior managers at the top of the organisation may, otherwise, be unaware of this information. Therefore, bottom-up flow is important.

This information flow does have some limitations. For example, there will still be many potential improvements at the level of activities and lower processes, and it makes little sense for the organisation to wait for a senior manager or director to decide on a small change in an activity. 

#### Information and decision-making flow 

![Decision-making flow](../../images/unit02/session01/pg7-diagram2.svg)

This diagram shows another type of information flow called a decision-making flow. It shows how decisions about day-to-day activities may be made by the people doing the activities, so that information flow and decisions are made in both directions. 

#### Information and product/service flow

![Product service flow](../../images/unit02/session01/pg7-diagram3.svg)

This diagram shows that information also flows vertically in a new thinking organisation. The flow extends from the customers, back through the organisation, and through to suppliers. The information flows in both directions.
