# How is waste eliminated?

Pages:

  * __
  * __

Waste costs money and can lead to poor quality products and services. Once waste has been identified, the company must look for ways to eliminate it. 

Companies use a three-step approach to eliminate waste: 

  1. Identify
  2. Categorise
  3. Eliminate

Identify 

Categorise 

Eliminate 

First, the company must decide upon which activities and processes to investigate. Employees who have hands-on experience in the chosen area should participate in the investigation. The process may be videoed for review. 

![](../../images/unit02/session01/pg10-identify.svg)

Sometimes, investigators ‘walk through’ the process and identify sources of waste as they do so. This is called a **waste walk**. They may record each individual waste source on a sticky note and place the note near the source of waste.

During the ‘identify’ step, the participants should resist the temptation to discuss solutions and counter measures. This is a distraction and people may miss further sources of waste.

Once the ‘identify’ stage has been completed, the company must consider solutions. For each source of waste, they should list as many solutions as possible. Sometimes, organisations use specific brainstorming techniques to help. The solutions may be listed alongside each source of waste.

The potential solutions are then categorised using criteria such as:

![](../../images/unit02/session01/pg10-categorise.svg)

  * **Time** – Can they be tackled short term, medium term or long term?
  * **Cost** – How much will the solution cost to implement?
  * **Benefit** – What will be the saving?

The organisation can then prioritise its elimination actions.

Finally, the organisation must take action to eliminate the sources of the waste. Once this is done, it is important to **check** and **evaluate** the effectiveness of the elimination actions. 

When waste is produced by an organisation, it is a clear indication that there are potential inefficiencies in some way. 

![](../../images/unit02/session01/pg10-eliminate.svg)

The effort to identify and eliminate waste shows that the organisation is seeking to improve its performance. By continuously identifying and eliminating waste, the organisation continuously improves its performance.
