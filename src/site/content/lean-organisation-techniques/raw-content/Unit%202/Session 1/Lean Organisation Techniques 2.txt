# Using business improvement techniques 

Pages:

  * __
  * __

The philosophy of **lean production**, and its relevance as a basis for improvement within the workplace, has been identified through productivity research. Its development has been applied to the public sector and back office functions of organisations.

  * ![Slide 1 image](../../images/unit02/session01/p2-slide1.svg)

The **Gershon report** is one good example of research which supports the philosophy and relevance of lean production. The report established **operation improvement targets** within local authorities which have been achieved through the application of **lean environment principles**. These lean environment principles can also be applied throughout the public sector. 

  * ![Slide 2 image](../../images/unit02/session01/p2-slide2.svg)

This Unit has been designed to give you the understanding and confidence to employ world-class techniques within your own working environment. These techniques can be applied to improve a wide range of operations, such as payroll and room usage. 

  * ![Slide 3 image](../../images/unit02/session01/p2-slide3.svg)

The Unit will enable you to investigate the **continuous improvement of process management** and will equip you with **Kaizen tools and techniques** which can be applied within the workplace to facilitate **Lean Systems**. 

  * ![Slide 4 image](../../images/unit02/session01/p2-slide4.svg)

You will also develop your knowledge and understanding of the **principles of workplace organisation**, implementing 5S/5C improvements, and visual management techniques.
