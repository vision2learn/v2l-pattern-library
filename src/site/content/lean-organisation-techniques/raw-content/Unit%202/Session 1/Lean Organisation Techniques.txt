### Business improvement techniques

To achieve a lean organisation, many businesses strive to continually improve their systems and processes. In this Unit you will look at some research which has been implemented by organisations. For companies to be successful and keep up with their competitors, it is important that they are not satisfied with their current way of operating but are always striving to improve the quality and efficiency of their operations.
