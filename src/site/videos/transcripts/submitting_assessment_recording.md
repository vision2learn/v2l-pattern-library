When you complete an assessment, you will need to send it to your tutor for marking. Login to your vision2learn account and visit the my study page. Each time you start a new assessment, it will appear under your assessments. You'll find the title of your assessment under the black assessment tab. When you have found the name of the assessment you wish to submit click on it. A new V-Mail message will appear on screen. Notice that the name of your tutor and the name of the assessment you are submitting have been added automatically.

To attach your assessment document, click the top Browse button. Use the file window to locate your assessment within your documents. Left click the file so it becomes highlighted and the name of the file appears in the file name box. Click Open to add your file to your V-Mail message. If you have any additional documents or files to submit, select the next Browse button and repeat the previous steps. If you would like to add a message to your tutor, you can type it in the message box.

Finally, click on the Send assessment button. Your V-Mail message and attachment or attachments will be sent to your tutor. Once you have submitted an assessment, it will be removed from your assessments on the my study page. You can now track the progress of your assessment on your My Progress page. To visit this page, click on the My Progress icon in the Tasks tab. This page shows a summary of the progress you are making through your vision2learn course. If you need any more help with this or any other key features, click the Help button located at the very top of the page in the black vision to learn banner.
