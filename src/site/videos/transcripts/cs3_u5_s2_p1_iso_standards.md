**Nicole:** So how do the ISO standards support cyber security?

A cyber security professional will probably find the ISO/IEC 27001 standard details most of the requirements that a business needs to follow to comply with ISO 27000 standards.

**Abraham:** It sets out the specifications that an Information Security Management System [ISMS] must meet to become certified. An ISMS is the set of policies and procedures including legal, physical and technical controls involved in the information risk management process. 

**Nicole:** Those organisations that meet the standards can become certified as ISO 27001 compliant by an accredited certification body.

**Abraham:** ISO 27001 requires organisations to:

• Systematically examine information security risks looking at threats, vulnerabilities and impact.

• Design and implement security controls to address unacceptable risks.

• Develop a comprehensive management process to regularly review and update the ISMS taking into consideration the threat environment and other developments.

**Nicole:** The ISO 27001 also includes guidelines for developing controls in a range of areas such as information security policies, human resource security, asset management, operations and communications security and compliance amongst others. 

**Abraham:** By aligning an organisations ISMS to the ISO 27001 standard it can also help to show that measures have been taken to comply with GDPR. 

**Nicole:** In the event of a data breach happening an ISMS that conforms to ISO 27001 will demonstrate to the regulator that they have done all they can to prevent and recover from an attack.

**Abraham:** The ISO 27032 standard is usually used together with ISO 27001 and includes guidelines on information and data protection to make sure there are no gaps in security policies and procedures

**Nicole:** It focuses on information systems and guidelines to prevent data leaks, like encrypting communication channels. The emphasis is more on network and internet security and critical information infrastructure.

**Abraham:** The ISO 27000 family as we have said covers a range of different standards that can help organisations to develop different aspects of their security controls and ISMS systems.

**Nicole:** ISO standards provide best practice guidance so having ISO 27000 certification can help organisations to show that they use effective security practices and procedures. This can in turn give employees and clients assurance that their data is in safe hands. 

**Abraham:** Some organisations will also have to show that they are ISO certified as a condition of doing business, this is especially true when working with government bodies.
