Problems with time perception can make life more difficult generally, as it is an essential part of the way people make sense of the world around them.

JOE: Problems with time perception can also cause delayed reaction to certain stimuli, such as speech and this can present communication problems such as not replying to questions or requests, or saying things later out of context.
