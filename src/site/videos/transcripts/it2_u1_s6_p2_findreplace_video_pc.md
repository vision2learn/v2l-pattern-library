A useful tool within Word is the Find and Replace tool. This allows you to search through a document for a specific word, and then replace it with another word. This could be very useful if you have a large document and you need to find instances of a word that may appear 100, 200 times and it needs to be changed quickly. 

As an example, I have a letter on the screen, and where I'm going to use the find replace tool to change the word "data" to "information". There are several instances of the word "data". And so I click on the editing tool, select Replace. And the word I'm looking for is "data". And I'm going to replace that with "information". 

There are three options here so you can Replace and that will replace each instance one at a time. So you can just confirm that this is okay. Or you can select Replace All, this will automatically search through the whole document and replace every instance of the word "data" within "information". It's good to be careful with this, because there may be certain words that are part of other words. 

An example of this is nighttime/nightmare. So if you are looking to change the word "night" into something else, and those words were present in that document, it would automatically change the first part of that word into whatever you had decided to change it into. So for this one here, I'm just going to change "data" with "information". And I'll go through one at a time, so if I select Replace. As you can see, it's highlighted the word "data". So I click Replace, the second instance has appeared, click Replace, and Replace, and Replace. Word has now finished searching the document, and I can close that. And every instance of the word "data" has been replaced with the word "information"..
