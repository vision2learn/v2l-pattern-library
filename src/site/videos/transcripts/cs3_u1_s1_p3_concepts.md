So what are the key concepts of Cyber security?

**Abraham:** Confidentiality…Integrity…Availability…Authentication.

Confidentiality means controlling access levels of data and sensitive information. It should only be available to those who have right to view, store or transmit it, and if someone doesn’t need to know, then they shouldn’t have access. 

Integrity means that all information is maintained safely as it should be with no modifications. Sensitive data should not be tampered with or compromised by any means.

**Nicole:** Availability means that sensitive information and data should not be readily available but should be accessible to those who have the appropriate authority at a time the time they need it.

**Abraham:** Authentication- means controls need to be in place to authenticate the identity of users and that access is given to the appropriate people. To help to make this more secure multifactor authentication should be used.

**Nicole:** We often talk about risk but what is risk? In simple terms risk means something bad happening. In terms of cyber security, risk is the potential for loss, damage or destruction of assets or data. Risk can be affected by a vulnerability, which is a weakness that can create exposure to a threat or a hazard. Having adequate cyber security can help can minimise that risk.

**Abraham:** Hazards are the probability of an occurrence of something potentially damaging.

Threats show more intent and are more likely to harm and vulnerability is how susceptible you are to that hazard or threat.
