Let’s look at how adults and parents can help children and young people make good lifestyle choices to help with Self Esteem.

 
RHIANNON:  
If a child or young person feels unimportant or worthless, it will likely lead to sad emotions and create poor self-esteem. This is a common factor in children and young people experimenting with alcohol or drugs and other destructive or risky behaviours. 

 
Having strong self-esteem is essential as a child or young person grows up, as they will face many challenges and having a healthy self-esteem is fundamental in making safe and healthy choices. a child or young person should be shown lots of love and positivity and told why they are special as a person. Children and young people remember the positive things and they make them feel loved. 

 
LEAH:  
An adult or parent who shows positivity, when faced with challenges can implant that on a child or young person and show them how to handle themselves when they too are challenged. They can also learn to not be afraid to show opinions and ideas and it’s ok to disagree sometimes as long as They consider the opinions of others.

 
RHIANNON:  
All children and young people need verbal discipline from time to time, but if the criticism is directed at the child as a person, rather than being directed at their inappropriate behaviour or actions, it can have a negative effect, causing them to feel ridiculed or ashamed. 

 
A parent should approach their role in this aspect of their child’s development by acting as a life coach or a teacher, rather than a disciplinarian or punisher. If a parent is unhappy with their behaviour, they should tell them, but make clear they still love them.

 
LEAH:  
When an adult is commenting on, or correcting the behaviour of a child or young person, it is important that they are fair and firm, as this will help the child or young person to accept responsibility for their bad behaviour. All children need to accept responsibility for their behaviour, and learning self-discipline is a very important part of growing up. 

 
RHIANNON:  
Valuing effort over perfection can encourage Children and young people to take on new challenges and by not being over-anxious about success, they might try more activities and set themselves more goals and plans for things they’d like to accomplish. Also getting praise for any improvements or successes can help even more.

 
LEAH:  
Children and young people need to be guided to make good lifestyle changes for the benefits of their mental well-being. 

 
This could involve encouraging them to discover and develop their talents, through clubs, groups and activities or to express themselves creatively, through art, drama or music. finding something they are good at can provide a huge boost to their feelings of self-worth and their self-esteem.
