**Nicole:** So, what motivates bad and good actors?

**Abraham:** Bad Actors or threat actors have malicious intent and are constantly looking for ways to infiltrate an organisations networks and systems. 

**Nicole:** Cyber criminals or black hat hackers are the most common threat actors, and their aim is to exploit human security vulnerabilities. Attacks may involve hacking into devices to gain password details and stealing or making data inaccessible to an organisation by holding it to ransom. Financial gain is usually the motive, and their weapons of choice are often phishing attacks, ransomware, and malware. 

**Abraham:** Hacktivists are activist groups or collectives who instead of being motivated by purely financial or personal gain, carry out 'attacks' in support of ideological or political causes and may target industries or specific organisations, who don't share their political views or causes. By disrupting organisations, they feel that they can alert the media and public to their cause, but they do it by criminal means. One of the most well-known Hactivist groups is Anonymous, known for their cyber attacks on governments, corporations, and the Church of Scientology.

**Nicole:** Not only do threat actors come from outside of an organisation they also exist within. They may be former or current employees who attempt to exploit vulnerabilities using ransomware, malware and hacking tools but with legitimate access to networks and systems. Their motivation may be financial, but it may also be for espionage reasons or sabotage, or even just for revenge, to get their own back, especially if they have been dismissed.

**Abraham:** And then there are those who are motivated by personal reasons, just to have fun, create chaos or seek attention. These are threat actors known as script kiddies, and are able to do a lot of damage, just because they can!

**Nicole:** So, what about good actors or white hat hackers? Are they bad actors who have seen the light? For some this is actually true! 

Check out Kevin Mitnick, who was arrested in 1995 for black hat activities by the FBI, now a trusted security consultant with his own security company.

**Abraham:** To know how to make systems cyber secure and thwart the hackers, cybersecurity professionals must first think like a hacker, then act like a hacker. But the difference is this is all done with the permission and authorisation of the system owner, in order to beat the cyber criminals at their own game. They use all the tools a black hat hacker would use but their motivation is to be a force for good.

**Nicole:** White hat hackers or ethical hackers come in a range of different forms. They perform roles of security experts and analysts, penetration testers and cybersecurity researchers.

But their motivations are not just about doing good, they can also make a good living out of being a white hat hacker or good actor. Afterall saving an organisation money by improving its security can be worth a lot!
