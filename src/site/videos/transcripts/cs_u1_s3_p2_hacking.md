**Abraham:** 'Hacking' is the process of compromising and gaining unauthorised access to a computer system or systems, whole networks or digital devices and taking control and inserting malware. This can be motivated by financial gain, protest, information gathering or just for the challenge! Hackers can be from a range of backgrounds and have different motives and may include self-taught 'kids' or rogue IT programmers and professionals skilled at modifying computer hardware and software.

Hacking can involve technical skills which can create “malvertising” or malicious advertising which uses online advertising to spread and install malware. Hackers inject infected ads into legitimate advertising networks that display ads on trusted websites. But hackers also use psychology to trick users into clicking on malicious attachments or providing personal data also known as social engineering.

Hacking is an umbrella term which covers a range of different cyber attacks and has evolved from a lone individual working from their bedroom to a widely established criminal enterprise with turnkey hacking tools available to those with lesser skills.

So, what motivates hackers to commit their crimes.

It may be financial gain such as the theft of credit card details or defrauding individuals and banks. Also holding companies to ransom.

It may be for gaining a reputation in the hacker subculture by pulling off hacks on sites and leaving their 'marks'

It may be for corporate espionage where one company may hack another to steal their information and gain a marketplace advantage or individuals will offer their services for sale.

It may be state sponsored with nations stealing business, military or national intelligence secrets and trying to destabilise other nations.

It may also involve Hacker Activists or Hacktivists who are politically or socially motivated and may wish to raise public awareness and focus public attention on something or someone.

It’s important to remember that an individual or a business may not always be the end target. You may even think why would anyone want to hack me? 

But they may be a step along the way. An individual or business may be hacked because of the established connections they have to a higher value target.
