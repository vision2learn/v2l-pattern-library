**Nicole:** Threat status is the current level of malicious cyber activity and the potential for damage or disruption to a computer network or system. Threat status can include information regarding attacks from malware or DoS and will determine whether software needs to be updated and or extra training is needed within an organisation or business to bring everyone up to speed.

The higher the threat status the greater the threat. Organisations and businesses may have their own system for labelling this by using colours or numbers etc.

**Abraham:** Threat modelling can be used to determine a threat status and identify potential security threats and any vulnerabilities. They can also help to determine how serious the threat is and what it’s impact would be.

So how do security professionals determine threat status? Let’s look at some of the processes that they can use.

**Nicole:** Gathering threat intelligence using OSINT and other reliable sources to gather information on current threats, vulnerabilities tools and processes and the motivations for threat actors.

Keeping up to date with all assets that are in use within the business or organisation. This can help them track down any flaws or vulnerabilities that may need to be addressed. Therefore, it is important to get alerts if any unauthorised downloads are made by staff.

**Abraham:** Keeping up to date with the ability to mitigate an attack by making sure the technology is able to counter an attack, such as upgrading the firewall.

Doing regular risk assessments especially after expanding any assets, to determine how attacks may take place and develop plans for addressing any vulnerabilities.

**Nicole:** Threat mapping the potential paths of threats and how attackers may get from one resource to another. This can help identify weak areas and where extra resources or training may be needed.

After a risk has been assessed the current threat status is determined and then recommendations and actions will take place. The exact recommendations will always depend on the type and level of the threat.
