**Nicole:** Let's have a look at some threats and how they can be protected against.

**Abraham:** A DoS or denial of service attack is where a computer is used to flood or crash a system due to too much internet traffic causing them to slow down and eventually stop.

This attack can be managed by deploying firewalls at key points in a network to filter out unwanted traffic. This means that the potential victim doesn't receive or respond to them.

**Nicole:** A DDoS or distributed denial of service attack involves multiple systems orchestrating a synchronised attack to a single target, overloading a server. These attacks can be seriously disruptive as the location is difficult to detect due to the random nature of the distribution of the attack. This form of attack uses malicious network traffic packets.

Modern security technologies have developed to defend against DoS attack, but the DDoS is more of an elevated threat and of greater concern to cyber security

**Abraham:** Botnet software

A bot is a piece of malware that infects a computer to carry out the commands of the attacker remotely. A botnet is a number of internet connected devices that are running one or more bots. The attacker controls the botnet to hijack networks of computers to carry out scams and cyberattacks such as data theft, server crashing and malware distribution. While botnets can be an attack in themselves, they can also be used to execute secondary attacks.

**Nicole:** A trojan is a type of malware often disguised as legitimate software. Users are often tricked into loading and executing trojans on their systems. They enable cyber criminals to spy and steal sensitive data and gain backdoor access to a system.

A trojan must be executed by the victim to gain access to the system via a phishing or social engineering attack by opening an infected email or clicking on a link. This type of threat can be prevented by not clicking on anything unsolicited.

**Abraham:** Ransomware is malware that encrypts and holds a victim’s information for ransom. They subsequently cannot access files, databases or applications and the attacker demands a ransom to provide access.

If a system is infected with ransomware an appropriate decryption tool will be needed to regain access. Antivirus software should be able to protect systems but also having back up data will help in the short term.

**Nicole:** Spyware is a form of malware that secretly infects a computer to gather information such as the sites visited, usernames, passwords, payment information and email activity. It runs quietly in the background monitoring activities. Spyware can enter your computer through freeware, software bundles and phishing and malvertising and it can also attack smartphones.

If spyware is suspected or to protect against it a good cybersecurity program or a robust antivirus software can be used to clean up the system.

**Abraham:** Worms are designed to spread from computer to computer and have the ability to reproduce themselves to do this. It operates by inserting itself into a legitimate programme or document that supports macros in order to execute its code. To do this it can create unexpected and damaging effects and corrupt or destroy data. It can remain dormant until the device executes its code and once that is infected it can infect others in the network. It can steal passwords, corrupt files and spam email contacts amongst other things. Antivirus software protects against more than viruses and is an older term. We now tend to refer to it as antimalware software because it protects against all types of malicious software including viruses.
