**Abraham:** The main form of unethical conduct we think about within cyber security is hacking, but there are other forms of unethical conduct that may take place. 

It may involve deliberately copying information and data to use or supply to others for unethical activities. 

**Nicole:** Unethical behaviour can involve sabotage, disclosing or misusing confidential information or maliciously injuring the reputation or prospects of an individual or business.

Let’s look at this in a little more detail.

**Abraham:** Sabotage- is rather an obvious form of unethical conduct and this can be seen when an employee intentionally damages a system. This may be for spite or to show that they can fix an ‘existing’ problem to showcase their skills or gain a ‘bug bounty’. This may also be done by a disgruntled employee who wants to get their own back if they have an axe to grind or have lost their job.

**Nicole:** Disclosing of misusing confidential information- cyber security professional have access to a great deal of personal confidential data and may feel that it might be worth something to pass it on to someone else or use it for blackmail.

**Abraham:** Maliciously injuring the reputation or prospects of an individual or business. Maliciously spreading information or images can seriously damage the reputation of a colleague or business. Cyber security professionals are in a trusted role having access to confidential data and should never use this against someone as this is totally unethical.

**Nicole:** But unethical conduct doesn’t always have to be on a large scale it can also be something as simple as leaving a 'post it' around with log in details for anyone to access a computer system.

**Abraham:** It may be thoughtless or show a lack of judgement, but it can lead to so much more as this can open a system up to unethical security issues. 

**Nicole:** It’s also important to remember that cyber security professionals are highly trusted members of staff who have a great deal of access to important, sensitive and highly confidential data. They should never use and abuse this high level of access and trust placed in them by displaying unethical conduct.
