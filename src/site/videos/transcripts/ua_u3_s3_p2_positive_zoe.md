Zoe: I had the experience of working with a child with autism who really enjoyed anything related to messy play. And through messy play, we were able to help them to learn to communicate through asking for different items, and it was always a lot of fun. It was always very messy. I'd always be covered in whatever it was they were interested in - from like flour, to hot chocolate powder, marshmallows, whatever it was. Paint anything. And it was always great fun, and it really helped the student to learn how to communicate because they got a lot of enjoyment out of it, and they were always very excited to  engage in anything messy.
