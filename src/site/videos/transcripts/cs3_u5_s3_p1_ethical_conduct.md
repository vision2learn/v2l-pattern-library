**Nicole:** Ethical conduct refers to standards of behaviour that are appropriate and shows understanding of what is right and what is wrong. 

**Abraham:** In cyber security unlike other industries there is no specific code of ethics though many employers and organisations will have their own codes of conduct or ethics covering other aspects of their organisations, as well as security that need to be complied with by all staff.

**Nicole:** It is also very important that all organisations that handle data do so in an ethical way to make sure that they abide by legislation and regulations that we have already looked at relating to the use of computers and data.

**Abraham:** All organisations and companies will have an IT policy in place, and some will also have a specific cyber security policy. These will be regularly reviewed and updated to keep in touch with ever changing laws and regulations. 

**Nicole:** Most new employees must sign up to a policy agreement as part of their induction to say that they will follow all guidelines including ethical ones especially where specific sensitive data is concerned and GDPR rules need to be adhered to.

There are a range of ethical rules that link to legislation when it comes to using computer systems that apply such as: 

**Abraham:** • Not using computer equipment to harm others. 

• Not interfering with other’s computers and work

• Not looking through other’s files without their permission.

• Not using a computer to steal or cause mischief.

• Not copying software.

• Not using other people’s resources without permission.

• Acting without regard for consequences.

• And having respect for data security.

**Nicole:** With remote working, more employees work from home, but, all of these rules should still apply. 

And remember ethical conduct goes both ways employees may sync their work emails to their smartphones, and employers may come to expect workers to check emails outside office hours.

**Abraham:** It is not ethical of a business to expect employees to be available at all hours unless this is part of their role.
