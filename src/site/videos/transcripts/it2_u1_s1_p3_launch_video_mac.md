To access your Microsoft Office programs on a Mac, there are three ways you can do this. First way is you can select the Finder.  And from there select Applications, and then you can scroll down to find the relevant program. So Word, PowerPoint orExcel. 

Alternatively, you can do your Launchpad and on the Launchpad screen, you can then navigate to where the program icons are, which is Excel, PowerPoint, and Word.

And finally, you can attach the shortcuts to the programs on your tool bar at the bottom of the screen. As you can see, I've placed Word, Excel and PowerPoint on this bar for easy access, which will then automatically launch the programs.
