To access your Microsoft Office programmes on a Mac, there are three ways you can do this: first way is you can select the Finder. And from there, select Applications. And you can scroll down to find the relevant program: Word, PowerPoint, or Excel. Alternatively, you can do your Launch Pad and on the Launch Pad screen you can then navigate to where the program icons are, which is, Excel, PowerPoint, and Word. And finally, you can attach the shortcuts to the programs on your toolbar at the bottom of the screen [the Dock], as you can see I've placed Word, Excel, and PowerPoint on this bar for easy access, which will then automatically launch the programs.
