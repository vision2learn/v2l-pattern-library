**Leah:** So maybe you have always had an interest in computers and dabbled in coding. Let's have a look at how you could take this interest forward and develop a career in coding and programming.

There are plenty of different jobs out there for programmers in the world of technology. Remember all those computer games and apps you like playing, software developers created them. Think of all the industries that use different software and it all must be programmed. 

So, let's have a look at some of those jobs and career paths that you could follow and what they involve and what qualifications you need to apply for a role.

**Jack:** Software application developers are responsible for creating and enhancing applications for mobile devices and the most common programming languages for this role are Java, Java script and Python. 

**Leah:** Web developers work on the look and function of websites. They listen to the client’s needs and problem solve to give them the best website for their business needs, they will work with a range of other creatives to optimise the best fit for a client. Most common languages used for creating website are Java, Java Script, and HTML.

**Jack:** Computer systems engineers are responsible for taking complex application problems, system administration issues or network concerns and identifying and creating solutions. They work closely with the client and collaborate with developers. Most common languages are Python, Java, and C++

**Leah:** Database administrators are responsible for securing, organising, and troubleshooting large amounts of data storage for companies or organisations. This is a great role for those who like to analyse, and problem solve. Most common languages are Python, Java, and SQL.

**Jack:** Computer systems analysts are strategists who are responsible for merging a company's business and IT initiatives. The evaluate and propose upgrades to meet the growing business needs. As well as coding, a good knowledge of business is useful. Most common languages are Python, Java, and JavaScript

**Leah:** Software quality assurance engineers are involved at the creation of software, documenting defects and designing tests and scenarios and creating manuals. They review functionality and potential problems. Most common languages are Python, Java and JavaScript.

**Jack:** For a Business Intelligence analyst, programming is a bonus but not always necessary, as they determine trends and gather facts about software products. This is an ideal career for those who don't always want to code. Most common languages are Python and Java.

**Leah:** Computer Programmers write and rewrite programmes until they are free of errors. They use workflow charts and coding formulas and show a good attention to detail and patience. Most common languages are Java and JavaScript and C#

**Jack:** Network system administrators maintain computing environments in their networks and prevent disasters by backing up data. They also provide network security and make sure that codes are free of errors. Most common languages are Python and Java.

**Leah:** Most people go into this career path after graduating after doing a degree in IT, Computing or other STEM subjects. There are also courses available in coding and specialist events and bootcamps. Some organisations also offer apprenticeship routes into a coding career with on-the-job experience. Once a programmer has their foot on the ladder then the opportunities for progression are there, depending on their abilities to expand their knowledge base and experience and those with advanced skills are likely to be in great demand.
