**Jack:** So, what exactly is a project manager and what do they do?

Project management has always been important in business and is getting more so all the time as project management skills are in more demand. 

Project managers are responsible for overseeing all the processes we have just looked at, Initiating, planning, executing, monitoring, and controlling and closing. These are also known as the ‘project life cycle’. 

They don’t do this on their own but instead are involved in all the different stages in one capacity or another and they may not even be assigned to the role until the project is underway. 

It is their responsibility to make sure that the right people are assigned to the right roles and that all planning is carried out properly to achieve the target outcome. 

They will also protect the team from any distractions and lead them through the executing of the project. They will also be on top of monitoring the progress of the project at all stages and addressing any issues that may arise. 

At closing they will be responsible for making sure everything has been finalised and working with the client or stakeholders to get everything signed off and reviewing the process.
