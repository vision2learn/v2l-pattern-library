JOE: Life events and transitions indicate change, and as we have discussed previously, change is often very difficult for individuals on the autism spectrum. An individual with autism needs to be prepared for any changes caused by life events and transitions making the process easier for them to handle. 

BETH: The planning process to prepare an individual with autism, for a life event or transition, should cover every aspect of that change and how it will impact the individual, making the process easier to deal with.

Also, the needs of everyone involved and those supporting the individual must be considered, considering how they are impacted by the life event and the emotional needs of all affected by the change in the situation. 

JOE: Seeking the support of others especially professionals can help to support everyone concerned and they can become a good asset to have on board when in the process of transition.

BETH: A psychotherapist or counsellor or another trusted professional such as a social worker may be able to offer guidance and support and a range of strategies that may help with change associated with a specific life event or transition that may impact the family.

JOE: Support groups and online communities may also be a good source of help offering their own experiences and guidance, giving insights into how to approach the transition process. 

BETH: Informal support networks can also be helpful directly and indirectly helping with more general support that might be needed. Families should not be afraid of seeking help when they need it to make the changes associated with life events and transitions progress more smoothly.
