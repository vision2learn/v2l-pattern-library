Hello and welcome to this video in which we will look at how you can carry out a Kaizen activity and establish measurable improvements. Carrying out a Kaizen activity involves a systematic approach to identify, implement, and measure improvements in various aspects of an organisation. For example, you may wish to establish measurable improvements across areas such as costs, health and safety, quality improvement, and customer satisfaction, as well as many others.

So how do we do this? The first step is to clearly define the objective of the Kaizen activity, focusing on a specific area or process that requires improvement. At this step, you will set specific and measurable goals for each improvement area. In this video, we will look at how to reduce the setup time for a manufacturing process as an example.

Assemble a cross functional team. Create a team comprising individuals from different departments or areas who have expertise or are directly involved in the targeted improvement area. This cross functional team brings diverse perspectives and ensures representation from all relevant stakeholders.

Analyse the current state. Conduct a thorough analysis of the current state of the process targeted for improvement. Map out the current setup process, identify steps that contribute to setup time, and document the time taken for each step. Use tools like value stream mapping or process flowcharts to visualize the process utilisation.

Generate improvement ideas. Conduct a brainstorming session with a team to generate ideas for reducing setup time. Some ideas could include improving tool organization, standardising setup procedures, or implementing quick change tooling. Prioritise improvement opportunities. Evaluate the generated ideas based on their potential impact and feasibility.

Prioritise the ideas that are likely to have the most significant impact on reducing setup time. Establish measurable metrics. Define a metric to measure setup time, such as the total time taken from stopping production to restarting production after setup. Set a specific target for reducing setup time, for example, by 50%.

Develop an action plan. Create a detailed action plan outlining the steps, responsibilities, and timelines for implementing the selected improvement ideas. Assign team members to each task, such as designing and implementing a tool organisation system, or creating standardised setup instructions.

Implement the improvements. Execute the action plan by implementing the identified improvements. For example, create labeled and organised tool storage areas, train operators on the standardised setup procedures, and implement quick change tooling systems. Measure and track results. Measure the setup time before and after implementing the improvements.

Collect data on the time taken for each step and calculate the overall setup time. Compare the results to the target set in step five to assess the effectiveness of the Kaizen activity. Review, adjust, and sustain. Review the results and gather feedback from the team. Identify any further opportunities for improvement and make adjustments to the setup process as necessary.

Develop standard operating procedures and provide training to sustain the improvements. Celebrate achievements. Celebrate the successful reduction in setup time and acknowledge the efforts of the team. Share the results and lessons learned with the wider organisation to inspire others and promote a culture of continuous improvement.
