JOE: There are a range of different initiatives, organisations and agencies and companies that can help individuals with autism into employment and some also offer employment directly focusing on the skills individuals with autism can offer.

BETH: ‘The National Autistic’ society provides resources and a variety of work programmes, training and resources for jobseekers with autism and can help employers looking to benefit from hiring a more diverse workforce.

JOE: ‘Ambitious about autism’ is a national charity that has a website covering a range of things including getting into employment. They run employability programmes acting as a bridge to employment for autistic people by removing barriers. 

They work with employers giving them access to talented and innovative autistic employees and promote paid work experience for individuals. They also provide training resources and mentorship. 

BETH: ‘Auticon’ is an international information technology consulting firm company that exclusively hires individuals with autism. It employs their cognitive diversity and specific talents to supply services for many blue-chip organisations throughout the world.

It embraces the extraordinary abilities that individuals with autism can have and their unique approach to problem solving.

JOE: The way individuals with high functioning autism think can make them more valued in the workplace, and as companies can see the opportunities to employ neuro-diverse individuals, then this may help others on the autistic spectrum.

BETH: Neuro-diverse people are wired differently and can bring new perspectives to a company and can make up for the skills shortages in technology companies and other digital industries.

JOE: More companies are looking to recruit individuals who offer technical talents and have attention to detail and systematic approaches to tasks. In a world that relies more on digital development this can only expand further.
