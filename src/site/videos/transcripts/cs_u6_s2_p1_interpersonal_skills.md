**Abraham:** Interpersonal skills are the skills required to effectively communicate, interact, and work with individuals and groups alike and these skills are important for success especially in cyber security. It is important to remember that cyber security does not exist in isolation. There is a strong human factor involved just consider how many breaches are initiated by ‘phishing.’ 

**Nicole:** Cyber security professionals must be able to effectively interact with all levels and departments within an organisation and educate other members of staff about cyber security threats that affect the whole organisation including themselves. 

So, what exactly are interpersonal skills?

**Abraham:** Interpersonal skills include —

• The ability to listen. 

• Good verbal and non-verbal communication abilities. 

• The ability to question others and give information in response to questioning. 

• Respecting the views and opinions of others.

• Putting across personal views clearly and appropriately. 

• Giving and receiving feedback 

• The ability to clarify thoughts and views.

• Working well and collaborating with others 

• Being self-aware 

• And having empathy 

**Nicole:** Cyber security is not just an IT problem it affects everyone in a company and organisation. As more departments rely on digital communication and technologies, then cyber vulnerabilities will increase, and cyber security professionals will be called upon to resolve issues across a range of areas making good use of their interpersonal skills.
