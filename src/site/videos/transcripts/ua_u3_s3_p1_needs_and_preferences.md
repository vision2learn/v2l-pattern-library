JOE: How can we adapt our communication style to meet the needs and preferences of each individual with autism.

BETH: When communicating with individuals with autism it is important to remember each person is unique and has unique needs and preferences and these are not going to be obvious straight away but there are different approaches we can take.

People should be aware of their communication style and how much spoken language they use. By modifying this using clear, simplified instructions, or giving extra processing time before repeating something or expecting an answer can help.

JOE: Being specific with questions can make answering easier or describing objects specifically such as including the size or the colour of something make it easier to identify.

BETH: Giving instructions in order, can help to make the process of doing something much clearer, for example when talking to a child by saying ‘First put your shoes on, then pick up your bag’ this helps them to understand the order in which to do things.
