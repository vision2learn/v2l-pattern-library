So what is a Standardised Operating Procedure? A Standardised Operating Procedure, or SOP, is a documented set of instructions that outlines how to complete a specific task or process. SOP are commonly used in various industries, including manufacturing, healthcare, and food service, to ensure that tasks are completed consistently and efficiently.

A SOP typically includes a clear and concise description of the task or process, a list of necessary materials and equipment, step by step instructions, safety precautions, and quality control measures. These instructions provide employees with a detailed roadmap of how to complete a task or process, reducing the likelihood of errors and increasing efficiency.
