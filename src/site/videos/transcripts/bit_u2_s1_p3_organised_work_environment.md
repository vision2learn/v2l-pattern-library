Hello and welcome to this video in which we'll look at why having an organised working environment is beneficial to you, the team and the organisation. Arguably the most important reason for having an organized working environment is health and safety which we looked at in Unit 1. We also learned about how having a cluttered and disorganized workspace can increase the risk of accidents and injuries by trips or items falling.

Imagine a workspace that has boxes, cables, and items piled up. There's no order for where to find anything and some items can't be reached without moving or climbing over things. By simply keeping everything clear, giving all items a place and tidying up regularly, many hazards can be avoided. Once we have a clean and tidy workplace, we can find what we need without needing to hunt for it.

We can move around faster and more safely, all of which increases productivity. Staff can easily access what they need. This includes each other as well as equipment. Collaboration between staff and teams will be easier. So an organized working environment has a huge impact on you and everyone else in the workplace.

Let's look at these benefits in a little more detail. When your workspace is organized, productivity increases, as you know exactly where everything is and you spend less time searching for things. This saves you valuable time and helps you focus on the task at hand. An organized workspace reduces stress and anxiety, which can have a positive impact on your mental and emotional well being.

When everything is in its place, you can approach your work with a clearer mind and less stress. By having an organized workspace, you're better able to prioritize your tasks and manage your time effectively. You'll be able to focus on what's most important and avoid distractions. When you work in an organized environment, it's easier for you and your colleagues to collaborate.

You can share information and resources more easily and communicate more effectively. A cluttered workspace can often lead to a cluttered mind. An organized workspace can help to create a calm and productive environment, which can lead to improved creativity and problem solving skills.
