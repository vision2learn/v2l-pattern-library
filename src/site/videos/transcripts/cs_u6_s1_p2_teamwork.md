**Abraham:** What is the advantage of working in a team to working alone?

Working with others in a team is often better than working alone for solving problems. It can increase creativity and allows for brainstorming ideas and discussing and sharing solutions to difficult tasks. 

**Nicole:** By working together, teams can find solutions to problems that work best by discussing the issues from different perspectives and different points of view. Productivity can therefore improve as more ideas can be developed and collaboration can increase. 

Teams can also compensate for any weakness in each other’s ideas or theories by offering a range of solutions which can be thoroughly discussed and reviewed.

**Abraham:** Conversely working alone an increase a person’s independence and help an individual to make their own decisions. It can increase an individual’s self-motivation, initiative and resourcefulness and help develop their problem-solving skills. 

There are less distractions like team meetings and the reliance on others and not everyone feels comfortable working in a team preferring to work with minimal direction and supervision.

**Nicole:** But working alone can be limiting, no one to bounce ideas off or offer advice at times of indecision and teamwork can be beneficial for everyone even those who prefer to work solo. 

When individuals get together with other independent thinkers to share ideas and by approaching ideas from different perspectives, they can bring a broader range of solutions to a problem.

**Abraham:** So, let's look at how team members can work together to make use of individual strengths.

A strong team should have a good team ethic which in turn makes it successful. Successful teams have members with a range of individual strengths and qualities. 

These include good communication, focus on the task in hand, contributing to the workload evenly, diverse members and good leadership.

**Nicole:** However, this is not to say all members need all the same qualities and strengths for a team to be successful. Team members bring different attributes to the team. But remember a team is only as good as it's component parts and if one of those parts is not functioning correctly then the whole team will suffer.

**Abraham:** Strong and responsible leadership is one of the key aspects of maintaining a successful team. A good leader should be respected and trusted. They are the glue that holds the team together. 

**Nicole:** The person at the top should be fully aware of everyone’s strengths and to some extent weaknesses and what they can bring to the team. They should be responsible for setting the pace and offer encouragement and motivation. They also need to be aware if anyone is not performing at their best, the reason why this is so. It maybe that they need more support, or that too much responsibility has been put on one person.
