**Leah:** Communication in business can be broken down into different types such as formal and informal.

Formal communication is the flow of information through formal channels and can be verbal or written. It is often hierarchical in nature and controlled by managers. Lines of communication may go down and up or even horizontally but are generally controlled via management and follow a systematic approach.

Informal communication is based on personal interactions and relationships. It can be unstructured and unplanned and does not follow the formal channels established by management. 

We all communicate in different ways at different times whether formally or informally so let’s have a look at these in a little more detail.

**Jack:** Verbal communication involves speaking with others. It may be face-to face, over the telephone or via digital media and the internet. It may also be on a one-to-one basis or with a group. Verbal communication can also be formal or informal in many different ways from presentations, meetings, interviews, discussions, to one-on-one conversations, both personal and for business. Verbal communication can be impacted by tone and language and non-verbal communication elements and may be backed up by other means of communication including visual imagery or written communication. 

**Leah:** Non-verbal communication includes facial expressions, posture, eye contact, hand movements and touch and can be used on its own or alongside verbal communication. A listener can communicate their response non-verbally without saying anything. Non-verbal communication is often used formally in meetings and interviews but is also used informally in social circumstances and personal relationships.

**Jack:** Written communication can traditionally be in the form of a letter, but often now involves emails, texts, memos, reports, Tweets, contracts etc. In the short form they should be clear and concise, but in the long form may include more complex information. However, the speeds and methods of communication today in the digital age, can often lead to poorly spelt and constructed sentences and careless errors. Written communication can be used both formally and informally.

**Leah:** Visual communication is all around us from posters and ads on a wall, to images on a TV screen. We are bombarded by social media with visual imagery on sites such as Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube. It can also be used in conjunction with written communication and verbal communication and often also involves non-verbal communication. Visual communication can often be used to back up verbal communication within a formal setting such as work presentations and advertising, but often it is used informally.
