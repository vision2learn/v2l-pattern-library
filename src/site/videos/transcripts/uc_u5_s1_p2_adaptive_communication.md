**Jack:** One of the ways to communicate effectively is to tailor the communication to the audience. If done correctly it should result in engaging the audience with good results. In business communication will involve people at different levels of ability and understanding, some may be colleagues who understand what is being communicated, some may be customers who might not be fully conversant and need to be informed. 

There are also different channels of communication that must be considered when communicating to different audiences, from giving out information generally, via social media or contacting individuals directly via email.

**Leah:** Adapting communication style can depend on whether the communication and interaction is face to face or digital. In person communication gives the opportunity to observe body language and can help in deciding if a change of tone is needed.

The tone should also suit the audience, respecting their views and not being too preachy or condescending.

Over email or digital communication, the message should be more considered to be able to put it across clearly and concisely.

**Jack:** Working with a team and presenting ideas face to face, will give opportunity for interaction and active listening and the opportunity for feedback, questioning and further discussion.

Vocabulary used should always fit the audience the communication is aimed at, and any complex elements should be explained in a way that others can understand.
