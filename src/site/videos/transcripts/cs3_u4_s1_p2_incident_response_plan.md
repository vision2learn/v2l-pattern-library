**Nicole:** What exactly is a cyber security incident response plan?

A cyber security incident plan consists of instructions on how to respond to and recover from network security incidents. 

**Abraham:** Incident plans may be drawn up to address issues such as malware detection, data theft and service outages, but it also should include all areas as cyber attacks can affect any part of an organisation.

So, when is a cyber security incident response plan used? 

**Nicole:** As we have previously mentioned every organisation whether large or small should have a Cyber Security Incident Response Plan or IR and this usually comes into play when a breach is detected.

It can also help to protect a system by planning for backups to secure data, security alerts to detect any malicious activity and efficient patch management. Identity and access management should be used to avoid insider threats.

**Abraham:** Incident response planning can protect an organisations reputation by helping to show that they are prepared. It can also be a more cost-effective way of handling cyber security as breaches can be expensive due to loss of data, downtime of systems, costs involved with investigating and restoring systems.

**Nicole:** Having an incident response plan is an important part of the whole cyber security process. It can help set out the role that everyone should play and the processes that should be followed at all times and not just during an attack.

**Abraham:** A plan should consist of a series of actions to follow but should also provide a checklist approach so that actions are consistent across all areas appropriate to the plan.
