JOE: Well, it is a plan that contains a range of strategies which not only focus on the behaviour that challenges of individuals with autism, but also includes ways to ensure that they have access to things that are important to them.

A behaviour support plan is a document that is created to help understand and manage behaviour in individuals with autism to show behaviour that others find challenging. It provides parents and carers with a step-by-step guide to make sure the individual has a good quality of life. 

BETH: It also enables parents and carers to identify when intervention is needed to prevent behaviour that challenges and help with positive reinforcement.

A good behaviour support plan is based on the results of a functional assessment and uses a ‘Positive Behaviour Support’, a holistic approach which considers not only the behaviour itself, but also the circumstances and characteristics of the individual exhibiting the behaviour and the people and situations surrounding it. It’s proactive and preventative, focusing on the teaching of new skills to replace behaviours that challenge.

These strategies used are referred to as proactive strategies and reactive strategies and a good behaviour support plan has more proactive strategies than reactive ones. 

Proactive strategies are tools that are used to prevent or avoid problem behaviour from occurring. They are introduced before any behaviours that challenge start and help to reduce the chances of them occurring.
