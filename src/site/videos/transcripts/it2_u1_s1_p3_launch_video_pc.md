To access your Microsoft Office programs, you would need to go down to the start menu, which is in the bottom left hand corner. If you click on the Windows icon, that will bring up a list of programs that are installed on your computer. Everything is installed alphabetically, so as you scroll down, you will see the programs that have been installed such as Excel, PowerPoint and Word, which are the three programs that you will use during this course. 

To access the program, you would simply click on the Word icon, and then that would open up, Microsoft Word.

To make it easier to access your software without having to go through the start menu. You can right click on that. And then select the more option and pin to task bar.

This will then place the icon on your task bar. So by clicking on that, that will open the program directly without having to go through the start menu. You can then do this for your PowerPoint and your Excel, which will just speed things up a little bit.

If I click on the icon now that will launch Word and take me to the Good Morning welcome screen.
