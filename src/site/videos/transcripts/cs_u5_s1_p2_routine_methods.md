Businesses large and small can use different methods to maintain cyber security to keep their data safe online. These can give a basic level of security against the most common risks. 

Intrusion detectors can be used to monitor a system and any unusual activity. Also raising awareness of all employees and their role in keeping a business cyber secure.

As we have mentioned previously, using strong passwords such as those using a combination of capital and lower-case letters, numbers, and symbols and multi factor authentication are important and having a password policy can help staff follow best security practice. 

Controlling access to data by making sure that individuals only have access to data and services for which they are authorised. This may involve physical restrictions to certain premises or application controls limiting access to networks which we covered in the last unit. 

Firewalls are effective gatekeepers and a major barrier to cyber threats, software and firmware updates should always be kept up with. Having suitable anti-malware software installed and regularly updated is essential to protect your system.

Ensure that data is consistently backed up using any protected back up devices, so that if a system is hacked or corrupted the information can be retrieved.

Remember make sure that your system is not vulnerable to the risk of cyber security attack as much as you can, by being aware of how cyber secure you are.
