**Nicole:** The Cybersecurity Tech Accord which works to foster collaboration amongst global companies to defend themselves from threat.

**Abraham:** The Global Commission on the Stability of Cyberspace which includes international governments, businesses and organisations helps to promote awareness and understanding on issues of cyber security.
