Mental health services are free on the NHS, but in some cases a GP referral is needed to access them. However, there are some mental health services that allow people to refer themselves.

 
At school or college, mental health care can be arranged for individuals through services such as CYPMHS which we have discussed previously. 

 
LEAH:  
Some mental health problems can be managed without the need of approaching a GP and there are a variety of materials available and local organisations offering help. 

 
RHIANNON:  
A GP will assess an individual’s circumstances and offer appropriate advice or treatment and can refer an individual on to a psychological therapy service or a specialist mental health service for further advice or treatment.


 These services may be provided by a GP surgery, a large local health centre, a specialist mental health clinic or a hospital. 

 
Treatment may be provided on a one-to-one basis or in a group with others with similar difficulties, and therapy can also sometimes involve families.

An Individual has the legal right to choose which provider and clinical team they are referred to by their GP for their first outpatient appointment and in most cases, have the right to choose which mental health service provider to go to.
