RHIANNON: How can we tell if someone is being bullied?

LEAH: If a child’s belongings are forever getting 'lost' or damaged or they never seem to have any money, this may indicate bullying. 

If they are suffering from physical injuries or unexplained bruises, maybe they don’t want to go to school or feign illness, or they are not doing well in class, this may also be an indication. 

If they generally become nervous, lose confidence and become withdrawn, or have problems with eating and sleeping this can be an indication that the child or young person could be being bullied. 

RHIANNON: Any child can be bullied for any reason. If a child is seen as different in some way or seen as an easy target, they can be even more at risk. 

This might be because of their race or ethnic background, gender, sexual orientation or could be just because they have low self-esteem, seem shy or introverted or appear anxious and lack assertiveness. 

LEAH: They may be bullied for the way they look, the colour of their hair, if they wear glasses or if they have a disability. 

RHIANNON: Some may be a target because of their interests or hobbies, they are new to an area, they have changed schools, or due to their family circumstances. 

LEAH: Sometimes people can even be bullied for things we wouldn’t associate with bullying, such as being good looking, popular, clever or having nice things.

RHIANNON: Bullying is all about causing hurt to others repeatedly, with ongoing damaging effects and the bully gains satisfaction and a sense of power, from treating others badly. 

LEAH: A young person who becomes visibly upset at name-calling may increasingly become the focus of attention and may be seen as a soft target, someone who is "easy to wind up” but not every at-risk person will be subject to bullying.

RHIANNON: Maybe there isn’t a bully around or maybe the person is more self-assured and self-confident and not as susceptible to bullying in general. In these cases bullying may not be the course and we need to look to other factors, but it is always worth being mindful of the possibilities.
