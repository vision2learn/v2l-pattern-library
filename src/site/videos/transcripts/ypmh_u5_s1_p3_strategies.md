When a child or young person shows signs of experiencing mental health concerns, usually the first line treatments include psychological therapies or medication. 

 
However, there are other approaches that can help such as changing lifestyle factors that influence mental health.

 
Simple positive lifestyle changes that can be made by Improving diet, increasing exercise, prioritising sleep and rest, and reaching out for help when needed.

Children and young people can be encouraged to improve their fitness by just getting out more walking or playing as short bouts of activity can provide an immediate elevation of mood.

 
Encouraging children and young people to limit exposure to mobile phones, computer and TV use or any other substance if its excessive. Helping them to regulate their actions can have positive benefits for sleep and mood, and therefore their mental health.

 
LEAH:  
Spending time with nature such as having a pet has many positive effects and may also boost feelings of well-being. Exposure to sunshine helps produce serotonin and boosts vitamin D levels, which also has a positive effect on mental health.

 
Positive lifestyle changes aren't a replacement for medication or psychological therapy but something people can undertake themselves, on top of their treatment.

Social support is often identified as an important component of good relationships and strong psychological health and involves having a network of family and friends that can be turned to in times of need. Social support involves advice, care, empathy and concern anytime or when most needed.

 
Others can help take care of physical needs especially when people have immediate needs that must be addressed for example when a child has worries about their workload for school.

 
Some can provide informational support providing guidance, advice, information, and mentoring especially important when making decisions or big life changes.

Being surrounded by people who are caring and supportive helps children and young people to deal more readily with the stresses that life brings.
