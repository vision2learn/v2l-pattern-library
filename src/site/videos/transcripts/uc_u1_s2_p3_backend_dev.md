**Jack:** A Backend developer is a person who builds and maintains the technology needed for to make sure that data or services requested by the front end system or software, are delivered. They use their understanding of programming languages and tools to solve problems and their responsibilities may involve creating, maintaining, testing, and debugging the ‘back end’ which allows the software or hardware to function.

To be a Backend developer it is often a requirement to have a BA in computer science or another closely related field and have worked their way up from a more junior role. Backend developers need good technological skills and are often well versed in several different programming languages.
