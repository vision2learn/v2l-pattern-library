**Nicole:** So, let’s look at some of the key organisations that are involved in cyber security in the UK today.

Let’s first look at the National Cyber Security Centre also known as the NCSC.

The NCSC is an organisation launched by the government in 2016, set up to support the most critical organisations in the public sector as well as industry and the general public. Their aim is to 'help make the UK the safest place to live and work online'. They offer practical guidance and will respond to any cyber security incidents they are made aware of, to minimise the harm that they may cause. 

They also work collaboratively with law enforcement, defence and intelligence and security services and overseas partners. Their website is a source of information for individuals and families to large organisations, including the self-employed, small, and medium businesses, the public sector and cyber professionals. 

The information they give can range from advice about what to do with spyware, like a scam message about a fake parcel delivery, to how to defend organisations against malware and ransomware attacks. They also have a wealth of information on all different aspects of cyber security and resources and opportunities for schools and students who are interested in cyber security.

For more information and guidance their website is well worth checking out. 

Next let’s look at Government Communications Headquarters also known as GCHQ

GCHQ are the UK's intelligence, security, and cyber agency and their 'mission is to keep the country safe in the real world and online'. They work with the secret intelligence agencies, police, and the private sector and with overseas partners to do this. They have always been at the heart of the British intelligence and security agencies. The NCSC is part of GCHQ and together they aim to protect the UK citizens and business from global attack, that may impact the country's security and prosperity.

For more information on GCHQ check out their website.

The Information Commissioners Office or ICO is the UK's independent body set up to uphold information rights. It works in the public interest and promotes openness by public bodies and data privacy for individuals. It shows clear guidelines about individuals rights regarding their data and its use and the individual’s right to access their data or correct it if necessary. 

For more information on the ICO check out their website.

And finally let’s look at the UK Cyber Security Council who state that

“The UK Cyber Security Council is the self-regulatory body, and voice, for UK cyber security education, training and skills, with a mandate from the UK government to be the focal point through which industry and the professional landscape advise, shape and inform national policy on cyber security professional standards”. 

They support the UK Governments National Cyber Security Strategy to make the UK a safe place to live and work online. They are an umbrella body for the cyber security profession and aim to be a reference point for cyber security professionals.

For more information on them check out their website.
