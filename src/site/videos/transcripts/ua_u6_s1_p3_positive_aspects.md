BETH: Every individual with autism is unique. They may share traits with others, but the autism spectrum is wide, and each individual will have their own outlook and mix of behaviours, many of which can be really positive.

An individual with autism can be constantly searching for a reason or an answer. This can help them throughout their education and into their working life.

JOE: Individuals with autism can have good concentration levels and may focus on a specific activity for hours. They can be thorough and have a high degree of accuracy in what they do with acute attention to detail. 

Individuals with autism’s obsessions and special interests make them inclined to thoroughly research things and find out all they can on a subject.

BETH: They are mainly visual learners and get used to being able to see things and reproduce them. They can spot patterns and repetitions in things and this can help with subjects such as science, maths and music which all are made up of patterns. 

JOE: They may also be good at organising and categorising, and have good memory for facts and processes, and once they learn to do something they can repeat it over and over again without making mistakes.

BETH: They can have creative and distinctive imaginations and expression of ideas, and a unique way of thinking outside the box.

They are often plain speaking and honest and may be less likely to judge others, seeing people for what they are.

JOE: Thinking about the positive aspects of autism can often make life easier for a parent and change the opinion of a teacher or potential employer. It can open doors to new opportunities and change how individuals with autism are perceived in the home, school, employment and in the community.  

BETH: Rather than focussing on the weaknesses associated with autism, people should focus on the strengths and the positive aspects of autism. A positive approach will help to build good supportive relationships, which can help with improved development and quality of life. Embracing and celebrating a different way of thinking can help to improve and add to the world in general and hopefully make it a more interesting place.
