When working with larger documents, you may wish to insert page numbers, the date, a company logo or other information into a section of the letter, or document. If you want it to appear on all pages, you would primarily use the Headers and Footers tool for this. 

To access the Headers and Footers tool, if you go to the Insert tab and move your mouse along, you will see the Header drop down. Now there are a range of pre formatted templates for the headers or footers. But if you just wish to edit a blank header or footer, you would just simply go down to the bottom and click Edit Header.
