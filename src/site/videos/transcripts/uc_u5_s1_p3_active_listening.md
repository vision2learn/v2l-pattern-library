**Leah:** Listening does not always get classed as a communication skill, but it can be one of the most important skills, which can have a major impact on job effectiveness and relationships with others. We use listening to obtain information and to learn, so that we can process and understand that information and utilise our skills in interpreting and acting on it. 

However, we don’t always hear everything that is be said to us especially if we are distracted. 

**Jack:** The way to improve our listening skills is by ‘active listening’ where a conscious effort is made to take on board the complete message that is being communicated, as well as the words that the person is saying. It is a way of listening that keeps a person involved with their conversation partner in a positive way.

In order to practice ‘active listening’ a person needs to pay careful attention to what is being said making the other person feel that they are being listened to and not get distracted by anything else around them. They must also keep focussed on what is being said without getting bored or losing interest. 

**Leah:** ‘Active listening’ also serves the purpose of earning the trust of others and having the opportunity to offer support and empathy to the speaker.

‘Active listening’ is particularly important when a person is in a supervisory position or interacting with colleagues. It helps with understanding problems and collaboration on projects.
