In this video we are going to explain the benefits of innovation and change for an organization.

Change is a fact of life and organisations at the forefront of their industry or line of service won’t stay that way if they can’t adapt to change. 

Innovation is when an organization or business makes positive changes to maintain its competitive edge and continue to deliver quality products or services. 

It’s important that organisations and businesses embrace the fact that they need to change and innovate, as the world around them is constantly moving, and it’s necessary to innovate and change to keep pace. If not, they’ll find themselves falling behind.

Let’s look at some of the benefits that can come from an organization or business undergoing change and innovation.

Embracing new technology

Every day, new technologies are being invented or developed and new scientific discoveries are being announced. Although sometimes it can take a while for an important new kind of technology to establish itself, many organisations and businesses keep an eye out for the next game-changing technological advance. By embracing new technology through innovation and change, businesses and other organisations can outcompete their rivals and satisfy their customers’ needs more efficiently.

Cost savings

By changing and innovating, organisations can keep down costs especially through adopting new work or management practices where inefficiencies are identified and removed. However sometimes this can involve redundancies, not always a popular change, especially when certain job roles are found to no longer be needed.

Increased profits

This is the flipside of cost savings. By innovating and adapting, businesses and organisations can increase the profits they’re bringing in, which they can in tur, re-invest into the business and reward their people.

Boosting efficiency

Innovation can help an organization or business discover more efficient ways to deliver its product and services. This can also help improve the customer experience, making it more likely current customers will stay loyal and spread the good word about their experience. It also frees up time and resources for the organization to achieve even more.

Competitiveness

For businesses, it’s always important to be as competitive as possible, striving to do better than other businesses in their field or industry. Innovation is very important in staying competitive. If you’re not innovating, you can always guarantee that one of your competitors is. This is also true of non-business organisations who want to maintain a good reputation and continue to deliver their services and products effectively.

Willingness to experiment

It’s true that not all innovations and changes achieve what they set out too. But by embracing innovation and change, and recognizing that they’re inevitable parts of life, you can inspire a spirit of experimentation in your people, making them more open minded and more willing to try new ideas in the future.
