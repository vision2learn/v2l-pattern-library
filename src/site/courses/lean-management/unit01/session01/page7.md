# Benefits of a lean environment

Pages:

  * __
  * __

There are many benefits of a lean work environment. At the heart of it all is an effort to improve the quality of service: making processes more efficient, improving employee morale and saving money.

Of course, these benefits can be felt across a business from those at the very top to employees, customers and everyone else who comes into contact with the business. 

ClickTap the information on this page to see some of the benefits of limiting the seven different types of waste and creating a leaner environment.

Reveal all / Close all

#### Transport

  * Efficient access to materials
  * Reduced handling costs
  * Less internal movement of materials
  * Reduced risk of handling damage and health and safety hazards

![Transport](../../images/unit01/session01/pg7-transport.svg)

#### Inventory

  * Frees up resources / money
  * Easier movement and transportation
  * Frees up workspace
  * Lower potential hazards

![Inventory ](../../images/unit01/session01/pg7-inventory.svg)

#### Motion

  * Less chance of injury and absences
  * Increased productivity
  * Improves environment and morale

![Motion](../../images/unit01/session01/pg7-motion.svg)

#### Waiting

  * More efficient use of employee’s time
  * Equipment is used more efficiently
  * Less likelihood of operator boredom
  * Possibly fewer accidents if employees aren’t bored

![Waiting](../../images/unit01/session01/pg7-waiting.svg)

#### Overproduction

  * Saves money on unnecessary overproduction
  * Underlying causes of overproduction are revealed
  * Less chance of being stuck with stock you can’t sell

![Overproduction](../../images/unit01/session01/pg7-overproduction.svg)

#### Overprocessing

  * Savings from cutting out unnecessary processes
  * Equipment is utilised more effectively
  * More efficient workflows

![Overprocessing](../../images/unit01/session01/pg7-overprocessing.svg)

#### Defects

  * More chance of customer satisfaction
  * Less money spent on reworking and fixing
  * Reduced waste disposal costs
  * Reduces need for overproduction

![Defects](../../images/unit01/session01/pg7-defects.svg)
